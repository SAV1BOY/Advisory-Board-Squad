## Constraints

1. **Engineering Capacity:** Current headcount, hiring pipeline, and realistic output expectations.
2. **Technical Debt:** Architecture or infrastructure limitations that constrain product options.
3. **Customer Commitments:** Features or timelines promised to key customers or in contracts.
4. **Regulatory Requirements:** Product changes mandated by regulation with hard deadlines.
5. **Partner Dependencies:** Technology or distribution partners whose roadmaps affect ours.
6. **Budget:** Product development budget and any approved changes needed.

## Frameworks Applied

### Product Strategy Validation Checklist
- [ ] Customer problem validated with primary research (not just anecdote)
- [ ] Market size estimated with bottom-up methodology
- [ ] Competitive moat identified and defensibility assessed
- [ ] Unit economics modeled with sensitivity analysis
- [ ] Technical feasibility confirmed by engineering leadership
- [ ] Go-to-market approach defined and resource requirements identified
- [ ] Success metrics defined with kill criteria
- [ ] Pre-mortem conducted: "If this fails in 12 months, why did it fail?"
