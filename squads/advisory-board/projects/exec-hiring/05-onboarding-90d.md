# Exec Hiring: Onboarding 90-Day Plan

## Objective
Set the new executive up for success with a structured 90-day plan that defines expectations, provides support, and creates early accountability.

## Inputs
- Role scorecard and 12-month outcomes (00-role-scorecard.md).
- Hiring decision with identified risks and mitigations (04-decision.md).
- Organizational context: team, culture, strategic priorities.

## Process
1. Define 30-day objectives: learn the business, build key relationships, identify quick wins.
2. Define 60-day objectives: deliver first substantive contributions, assess the team, propose initial changes.
3. Define 90-day objectives: measurable outcomes that validate the hire, including at least one item from the 12-month scorecard.
4. Assign an onboarding buddy or mentor from the leadership team.
5. Schedule check-in cadence: weekly 1:1 with CEO, biweekly with board advisor, monthly with board.
6. Define early warning signals: behaviors or results that indicate a potential mismatch.
7. Define the course correction protocol: what happens if 30 or 60-day objectives are missed.
8. Schedule the 90-day review with the hiring committee.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/hiring-blocks.md` (Block 6: Onboarding Accountability Plan)
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` (Block 6: Culture Due Diligence for integration)
- `data/registries/hiring-registry.yaml` (update with onboarding status)

## Output
- 90-day onboarding plan document shared with the new hire, their manager, and the board.
- Check-in calendar scheduled.
- Early warning signals and course correction protocol defined.
- 90-day review date confirmed.

## Next Phase
The 90-day review marks the transition from onboarding to ongoing performance management. Update the hiring registry with the 90-day outcome assessment.
