# Task: Review Board and Advisor Compensation

## Objective

Evaluate current board member and advisor compensation against market benchmarks, company stage, and strategic value delivered. Produce recommendations for adjustments that attract and retain top governance talent while maintaining fiscal discipline.


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → decision-quality-review`

- **Lead:** governance-clerk (Simon Sinek)
- **Support:** board-chair (Board Chair), capital-allocator (Charlie Munger), ethics-compass (Yvon Chouinard)

## Context

Board and advisor compensation must balance multiple objectives: attracting experienced directors, aligning incentives with long-term value creation, maintaining independence, and demonstrating responsible capital stewardship. Under-compensating leads to disengaged directors; over-compensating signals poor governance.

## Scope

- Board of Directors compensation (all members)
- Advisory Board compensation (all advisors)
- Committee chair and member premiums
- Special project or ad hoc advisory fees
- Equity grants, vesting schedules, and dilution impact

## Deliverables

### 1. Current State Analysis
Document for each board member and advisor:
- Cash compensation (retainer, per-meeting fees, committee premiums)
- Equity compensation (grant size, type, vesting schedule, current value)
- Total compensation value (cash + equity at current valuation)
- Time commitment (meetings attended, hours contributed, availability)
- Tenure and remaining term

### 2. Benchmark Comparison
Compare current compensation against:
- Companies of similar stage and size (revenue, headcount, funding)
- Companies in the same industry vertical
- Geographic market norms (Silicon Valley vs. other markets)
- Public company benchmarks where relevant for late-stage companies

### 3. Value Assessment
For each director and advisor, evaluate:
- Strategic contributions (introductions, deals influenced, decisions shaped)
- Attendance and engagement level
- Specialized expertise applied (finance, legal, technical, industry)
- Reputation and signaling value to investors, customers, and recruits
- Comparison of value delivered to compensation received

### 4. Recommendations
Produce specific recommendations for:
- Adjustments to cash and equity compensation by role
- Changes to vesting schedules or equity grant structures
- New committee premium structures if committees are being formed
- Advisor agreement renewals, modifications, or terminations
- Compensation philosophy statement for governance documentation

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Collect all current compensation agreements and grant records
- [ ] Verify equity grant details with cap table and legal counsel
- [ ] Gather benchmark data from at least three comparable sources
- [ ] Interview CEO and board chair on satisfaction with current arrangements
- [ ] Survey directors and advisors on their perception of compensation fairness
- [ ] Review proxy statements of comparable public companies (if applicable)
- [ ] Assess total dilution from board and advisor equity grants
- [ ] Evaluate D&O insurance coverage and whether it meets director expectations
- [ ] Check compliance with any investor or governance agreements that constrain compensation
- [ ] Draft updated compensation philosophy and recommendation memo

## Key Questions to Answer

1. Are we paying enough to attract the caliber of directors and advisors we need for the next stage?
2. Is the equity-to-cash ratio appropriate for our stage and cash position?
3. Are vesting schedules creating the right retention incentives?
4. Is there meaningful differentiation between high-contributing and low-contributing members?
5. Does our compensation structure maintain director independence per governance best practices?
6. What is the total dilutive impact of all board and advisor equity grants?

## Output Format

Final deliverable is a compensation review memo to the board (or compensation committee if one exists) containing:
- Executive summary with key findings and top-line recommendations
- Detailed current-state analysis with benchmark comparisons
- Role-by-role recommendations with rationale
- Proposed compensation philosophy statement
- Implementation timeline and next steps

## Success Criteria

- Board compensation is within the 50th-75th percentile of relevant benchmarks
- Equity dilution from governance roles is documented and within acceptable range
- All agreements are current, properly documented, and legally reviewed
- A forward-looking compensation philosophy is established and approved

## Time Estimate

Data collection and benchmarking: 2 weeks
Analysis and recommendations: 1 week
Board review and approval: 1 meeting cycle
