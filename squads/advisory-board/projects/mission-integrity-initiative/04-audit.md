# Mission Integrity Initiative: Audit

## Objective
Conduct ongoing audits to verify that mission integrity policies are being followed and producing the intended results.

## Inputs
- Implemented policies (03-implementation.md). Decision registry data. Culture health assessments. Stakeholder feedback.

## Process
1. Review a sample of significant decisions from the past quarter against mission integrity criteria.
2. Assess whether incentive structures are producing mission-aligned behavior.
3. Survey employees and customers on perceived mission integrity.
4. Review any mission integrity concern reports filed through reporting channels.
5. Identify areas of drift or erosion.
6. Report findings to the board.
7. Update policies and practices based on audit findings.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/utilities/culture-health-rubric.md` (culture dimensions related to mission)
- `data/registries/decision-registry.yaml` (decision-mission alignment review)
- `data/metrics/strategic-alignment-score.md`

## Output
Mission integrity audit report. Recommendations for policy updates. Board presentation.

## Next Phase
Continuous: conduct mission integrity audits semi-annually. Integrate findings into culture health assessments.
