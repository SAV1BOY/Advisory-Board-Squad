# Exec Hiring: Interviews

## Objective
Evaluate candidates through a structured interview process that covers all scorecard dimensions, minimizes bias, and produces comparable data across candidates.

## Inputs
- Screened candidate shortlist (01-sourcing.md).
- Role scorecard with competency requirements.
- Interview architecture from hiring-blocks.md Block 3.

## Process
1. Design the interview sequence: screening, competency interviews, case study, cultural interview, final interview.
2. Assign interviewers to specific competencies — ensure every scorecard dimension is covered.
3. Prepare structured questions for each interview stage.
4. Conduct interviews. Each interviewer writes their assessment independently before any group discussion.
5. Score candidates on each competency using the scorecard scale.
6. Conduct a structured debrief: share scores, discuss evidence, identify gaps.
7. Narrow to 2-3 finalists for deep reference checks.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/hiring-blocks.md` (Block 3: Interview Architecture)
- `lib/utilities/decision-quality-rubric.md` (applied to the hiring decision process)

## Output
- Interview scorecards for all candidates.
- Structured debrief notes.
- Finalist shortlist (2-3 candidates) for reference phase.
