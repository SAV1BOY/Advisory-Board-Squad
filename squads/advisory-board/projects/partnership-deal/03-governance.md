# Partnership Deal: Governance

## Objective
Establish the ongoing management and measurement framework that will govern the partnership after signing.

## Inputs
- Signed partnership agreement (02-terms.md). Integration and launch plan.

## Process
1. Establish governance cadence: operational meetings weekly, strategic monthly, executive quarterly.
2. Define 5-7 KPIs that measure partnership health and value delivery.
3. Create escalation protocol for issues.
4. Design annual review process for ROI assessment and renewal decision.
5. Define relationship health checks.
6. Set evolution triggers for expanding, contracting, or restructuring.
7. Define sunset criteria for termination.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/partnership-blocks.md` (Block 5: Ongoing Governance)
- `data/registries/partnership-registry.yaml`

## Output
Governance framework document. KPI dashboard. Escalation protocols defined.
