# Strategic Reset: Execution Plan

## Objective
Translate the strategic choice into a concrete, accountable execution plan with initiatives, owners, milestones, resource allocation, and decision points. Strategy without execution is aspiration.

## Inputs
- Approved strategic direction (02-choices-tradeoffs.md).
- Resource inventory: available people, budget, and capabilities.
- Existing commitments and initiatives that must be continued, modified, or killed.

## Process
1. Define 3-5 strategic initiatives that implement the chosen direction. Fewer is better — focus compounds.
2. For each initiative, define: objective, owner (single person), key milestones with dates, resource requirements, dependencies, success criteria, and kill criteria.
3. Build the first 90-day sprint: specific actions that create momentum and test key assumptions.
4. Allocate resources using capital-blocks.md framework: move budget and people from deprioritized activities to strategic priorities.
5. Identify what to stop: which current activities, projects, or initiatives must be killed or paused to free resources.
6. Schedule decision points: pre-defined moments where the strategy will be evaluated and potentially adjusted.
7. Design the communication plan: how the strategy and execution plan will be communicated to the team, investors, customers, and partners.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` (Block 4: Resource Allocation, Block 5: Coherent Action Set)
- `lib/components/capital-blocks.md` (Block 2: Capital Allocation Framework)
- `lib/patterns/kill-decision-pattern.md` (for initiatives being terminated)
- `lib/patterns/delegation-pattern.md` (for assigning initiative ownership)

## Output
- Execution plan document with initiative briefs, 90-day sprint, resource allocation, and communication plan.
- Updated budget reflecting strategic reallocation.
- Initiative owners confirmed and accountable.
- Kill list: initiatives and activities being terminated with transition plans.
