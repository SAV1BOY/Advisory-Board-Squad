# Crisis Response: Postmortem

## Objective
Capture lessons from the crisis within 2 weeks of resolution while institutional memory is fresh. Convert the painful experience into organizational knowledge.

## Inputs
- Complete crisis timeline. Containment and resolution records. Communications log. Team reflections.

## Process
1. Reconstruct the full timeline from discovery through resolution.
2. Identify what went well in the response (codify these practices).
3. Identify what went poorly (diagnose root causes of response failures).
4. Identify what was lucky (favorable outcomes that were not the result of good process).
5. Extract specific, actionable lessons.
6. Define process changes for future crisis response.
7. Calculate total cost: financial, reputational, operational, and human.
8. Present postmortem to the board.
9. Archive in the lessons-learned registry.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/crisis-blocks.md` (Block 6: Crisis Postmortem)
- `lib/patterns/postmortem-pattern.md`
- `data/registries/lessons-learned-registry.yaml`

## Output
Crisis postmortem document. Updated crisis response procedures. Lessons learned entries. Board debrief completed.

## Next Phase
Return to normal operations with improved crisis readiness.
