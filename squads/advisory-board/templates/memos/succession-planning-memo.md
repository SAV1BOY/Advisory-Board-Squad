## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **CEO-only focus:** Succession planning must cover all key executive roles, not just the CEO. A sudden CTO departure can be just as disruptive.
2. **Paper exercise:** If development plans exist only in this document and are not actively executed with resources and accountability, the plan is theater.
3. **Avoiding difficult conversations:** If the incumbent is underperforming, the succession plan should acknowledge this. Polite fiction wastes time.
4. **Single-candidate plans:** Having only one successor candidate for a critical role is not a plan — it is a single point of failure.
5. **Stale plans:** Succession plans that are not reviewed and updated at least annually become dangerously outdated. People's readiness, intentions, and circumstances change.
