# Task: Update Registries

## Objective
Maintain all board governance registries and logs as living documents. Ensure the decision log, risk register, action item tracker, and other governance artifacts are accurate, current, and useful.

## Trigger
- Weekly for action item tracker
- Monthly for risk register
- After every board meeting for decision log
- Immediately upon any material event

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Ops
- **Support:** Functional leaders for their domain registries, Legal for compliance registries
- **Advisory:** Board Chair reviews quarterly


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → cross-squad-sync`

- **Lead:** governance-clerk (Simon Sinek)
- **Support:** board-chair (Board Chair)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current registry documents available
- [ ] Update inputs collected from stakeholders
- [ ] Board meeting minutes processed if applicable
- [ ] Risk events documented if applicable

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Update the decision log with any decisions made since last update including async decisions and management decisions within delegated authority that have board reporting requirements

2. **Step 2:** Update the action item tracker with current status for all open items, close completed items with outcome notes, and flag overdue items per escalation protocol

3. **Step 3:** Update the risk register with new risks, changed assessments, and closed risks

4. **Step 4:** Update the board scorecard with current metrics if data has refreshed since last update

5. **Step 5:** Review all registries for staleness identifying any entries that have not been reviewed or updated within their required cadence

6. **Step 6:** Maintain the board calendar ensuring upcoming meetings and deadlines are current

7. **Step 7:** Archive completed or superseded entries per retention policy

8. **Step 8:** Updated decision log; Updated action item tracker; Updated risk register; Updated board scorecard; Updated board calendar; Archival of stale entries

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Operational Excellence:** Standardized processes with defined cadence and quality standards
- **Continuous Improvement:** Each cycle identifies and implements at least one process improvement
- **Single Source of Truth:** Every governance artifact has one canonical location and one owner

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All required updates completed within the defined cadence
- [ ] Data accuracy verified before distribution
- [ ] Stakeholders notified of material changes
- [ ] Process followed consistently (not ad hoc)
- [ ] Feedback collected and incorporated into process improvement
- [ ] Documentation current and accessible

## Output
- Current registries feed into meeting-prep package
- Action item status feeds into meeting agenda
- Risk register updates feed into quarterly board review
- Stale items flagged for owner follow-up
