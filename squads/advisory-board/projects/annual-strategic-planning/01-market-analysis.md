# Annual Strategic Planning: Market Analysis

## Objective
Develop a clear-eyed view of the current market landscape including trends, competitive dynamics, customer evolution, and emerging threats and opportunities.

## Inputs
- Industry reports and market research. Competitive intelligence. Customer feedback and behavior data. Technology trend analysis. Regulatory environment updates.

## Process
1. Assess market size and growth: TAM, SAM, SOM with updated data.
2. Analyze competitive landscape: who is winning, who is losing, and why. New entrants and potential disruptors.
3. Synthesize customer insights: how are customer needs, behaviors, and expectations evolving?
4. Identify technology trends that create opportunities or threats.
5. Assess regulatory and macroeconomic factors.
6. Build 3-5 scenarios for how the market could evolve over the next 1-3 years.
7. Identify the single most important market insight that should inform the strategy.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/utilities/scenario-table.md` (market scenario development)
- `lib/utilities/moat-strength-rubric.md` (competitive position assessment)
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` Block 1 (Strategic Context)

## Output
Market analysis document with competitive landscape, trend synthesis, scenario projections, and key insight.
