# Task: Deal Terms Review

## Objective
Review and negotiate the specific terms of a partnership, vendor contract, or commercial agreement to protect company interests and maximize value. Ensure board visibility into material commitments.

## Trigger
- Partnership evaluation recommends proceeding to terms negotiation
- Vendor contract renewal with material terms changes
- New commercial agreement exceeding delegated authority threshold
- Any agreement with exclusivity, non-compete, or long-term commitment

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Legal Counsel with CEO or Business Development Lead
- **Support:** CFO for financial terms, CTO for technology terms, relevant functional leaders
- **Advisory:** Board members for strategic terms guidance

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Partner evaluation completed and board direction to proceed
- [ ] Legal counsel engaged
- [ ] Business terms generally agreed in principle
- [ ] Authority to negotiate confirmed by board

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Review the complete draft agreement identifying all material terms and their implications

2. **Step 2:** Assess financial terms including revenue share, minimum commitments, payment terms, and penalties for full economic impact

3. **Step 3:** Evaluate operational terms including SLAs, integration requirements, support obligations, and resource commitments

4. **Step 4:** Analyze risk and liability terms including indemnification, limitation of liability, insurance requirements, and data protection

5. **Step 5:** Review exit terms including termination conditions, notice periods, wind-down obligations, IP treatment on termination, and change-of-control provisions

6. **Step 6:** Identify negotiation priorities distinguishing between must-have terms, important-to-have terms, and nice-to-have terms

7. **Step 7:** Negotiate with partner seeking to optimize across all priorities while maintaining the relationship

8. **Step 8:** Present final terms to board for approval against originally authorized parameters

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Partnership Health Scorecard:** Assess relationship health, value delivery, dependency balance, and strategic alignment
- **RACI for Partnerships:** Clear responsibility assignment across both organizations
- **Value Chain Analysis:** Map where each partner adds value and where dependencies exist

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Both parties' interests and value contribution are clearly documented
- [ ] Risks are identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled and understood
- [ ] Legal review completed for all material terms
- [ ] Exit strategy and costs are understood
- [ ] Governance structure ensures accountability without bureaucracy
- [ ] Board has appropriate visibility into material partnerships

## Output
- Board-approved agreement
- Terms summary document
- Risk assessment of accepted terms
- Implementation requirements checklist

## Handoffs
- Signed agreement feeds into partnership-governance-setup
- Financial commitments feed into budget and runway model
- Operational requirements feed into resource planning
- Risk terms feed into risk-register-update
