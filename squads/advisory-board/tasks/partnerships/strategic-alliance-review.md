# Task: Review Existing Strategic Alliances

## Objective

Conduct a comprehensive review of all existing strategic alliances, partnerships, and joint ventures to assess performance, alignment, and whether each relationship should be expanded, maintained, restructured, or terminated.

## Context

Strategic alliances accumulate over time. Without periodic review, companies maintain partnerships that no longer serve strategic objectives, miss opportunities to deepen high-performing relationships, and fail to recognize partnerships that have become liabilities.

## Scope

Review all formal partnerships including:
- Technology integration partnerships
- Channel and distribution partnerships
- Co-marketing and co-selling agreements
- Joint development agreements
- Licensing arrangements (inbound and outbound)
- Reseller and referral agreements
- Joint ventures and equity partnerships

## Deliverables

### 1. Partnership Inventory
For each alliance, document:
- Partner name and primary contact
- Agreement type, start date, and term/renewal dates
- Original strategic rationale for the partnership
- Key contractual terms (exclusivity, minimums, termination provisions)
- Financial impact (revenue generated, costs incurred, investment required)
- Operational dependencies (shared customers, integrated technology, co-branded assets)

### 2. Performance Assessment
For each alliance, evaluate:
- **Revenue impact:** Direct and attributed revenue relative to expectations
- **Strategic value:** Does this partnership still serve a current strategic priority?
- **Relationship health:** Quality of communication, responsiveness, mutual investment
- **Competitive dynamics:** Has the partner's competitive posture changed since inception?
- **Opportunity cost:** What resources does this partnership consume that could be deployed elsewhere?

### 3. Classification and Recommendation
Categorize each partnership:
- **Expand:** High-performing, strategically aligned, mutual upside from deeper engagement
- **Maintain:** Performing adequately, low maintenance cost, no compelling reason to change
- **Restructure:** Valuable but underperforming; terms, scope, or governance need adjustment
- **Terminate:** No longer strategically relevant, underperforming, or creating risk

### 4. Gap Analysis
Identify:
- Strategic capabilities the company needs but has no partnership to address
- Markets or customer segments accessible through partnerships not yet established
- Technology or integration partnerships that competitors have secured exclusively
- Potential partners who have been approached but not yet engaged

## Review Framework

### Strategic Alignment Score (1-5)
| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | Directly enables a top-3 strategic priority |
| 4 | Supports a current strategic initiative |
| 3 | Relevant but not tied to a specific priority |
| 2 | Tangentially related to current strategy |
| 1 | No connection to current strategic direction |

### Performance Score (1-5)
| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | Exceeding all targets; strong mutual investment |
| 4 | Meeting targets; healthy relationship |
| 3 | Mixed results; some value but below potential |
| 2 | Underperforming targets; relationship friction |
| 1 | Failing on all metrics; negative impact |

### Action Matrix
| | High Performance | Low Performance |
|---|-----------------|-----------------|
| **High Alignment** | Expand | Restructure |
| **Low Alignment** | Maintain/Harvest | Terminate |

## Process

### Phase 1: Data Collection (Weeks 1-2)
- Gather all partnership agreements and amendments
- Collect financial data on each partnership's contribution
- Interview internal partnership owners and stakeholders
- Survey partners on their assessment of the relationship

### Phase 2: Analysis (Week 3)
- Score each partnership on strategic alignment and performance
- Plot partnerships on the action matrix
- Identify patterns: which types of partnerships perform best?
- Calculate total partnership portfolio ROI

### Phase 3: Recommendations (Week 4)
- Develop specific action plans for each partnership
- Prioritize restructuring and termination conversations
- Identify 3-5 new partnership opportunities from gap analysis
- Present findings and recommendations to leadership

## Success Criteria

- Complete inventory of all active partnerships with current status
- Clear action plan for each partnership: expand, maintain, restructure, or terminate
- Identified gaps where new partnerships would create strategic value
- Established cadence for ongoing partnership portfolio review (annually)

## Time Estimate

Full review: 4 weeks
Annual refresh: 2 weeks
