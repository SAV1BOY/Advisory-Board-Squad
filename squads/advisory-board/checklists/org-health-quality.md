# Org Health Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Provides a systematic assessment of organizational health beyond financial metrics. Healthy organizations execute faster, retain talent, and adapt to change. Unhealthy organizations produce good numbers temporarily while accumulating organizational debt that eventually causes failure. This checklist surfaces leading indicators of dysfunction before they become crises.

## When to Use
- Quarterly as part of leadership team operational reviews.
- When employee engagement survey results are available.
- When attrition rates change significantly (up or down).
- Before major strategic initiatives that depend on organizational capacity.
- When board members request an assessment of organizational readiness.

## Gate Criteria

### Engagement and Morale
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Employee engagement data is collected at least twice annually through a validated instrument.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Engagement scores are analyzed by team, level, tenure, and demographic to identify pockets of concern.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Action plans exist for teams or segments with below-threshold engagement scores.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Manager effectiveness scores are tracked and managers below threshold receive coaching or reassignment.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Exit interview data is analyzed for systemic themes, not just individual grievances.

### Retention and Talent Density
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Regrettable attrition rate is tracked separately from total attrition.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Regrettable attrition is below the defined threshold for the industry and stage.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: High-performer retention is tracked specifically and is above 90%.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The ratio of top performers to average performers is stable or improving.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Time-to-fill for critical roles is tracked and within acceptable range.

### Execution Velocity
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Key initiatives are delivered within 20% of planned timelines.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Decision-making speed is assessed: the time from problem identification to decision to action.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Bureaucratic overhead is measured: approval steps, meeting load, and cross-functional handoff delays.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Teams can articulate their top three priorities and those align with company priorities.

### Psychological Safety and Trust
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Employees report feeling safe to raise concerns without retaliation (measured via survey).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Bad news travels up the organization quickly, not slowly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Post-mortems focus on systems and processes, not blame.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Leadership models vulnerability and acknowledges mistakes publicly.

### Alignment and Clarity
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Employees at all levels can articulate the company strategy in their own words consistently.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Goals cascade clearly from company to team to individual.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Role clarity scores are high: people know what they are accountable for and what they are not.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Cross-functional collaboration is rated as effective by participants in key workflows.

## Red Flags
- Engagement scores are high but attrition is also high (suggesting survey gaming or dissonance).
- No one has been fired or managed out in over a year despite known performance issues.
- Meeting load has increased by more than 20% year-over-year.
- The leadership team avoids difficult conversations or defaults to artificial harmony.
- Employee referrals have declined significantly, suggesting insiders are not recommending the company.
- There is a significant gap between what leadership says the culture is and what employees experience.

## Escalation Triggers
- Regrettable attrition exceeds 20% annualized in any critical department.
- Engagement scores drop by more than 10 points quarter-over-quarter.
- A credible allegation of systemic harassment, discrimination, or retaliation surfaces.
- Three or more key initiatives miss deadlines by more than 30% in a single quarter.
- An organizational health issue is identified but leadership takes no corrective action for 90 days.
