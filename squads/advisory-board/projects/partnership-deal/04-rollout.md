# Partnership Deal: Rollout

## Objective
Execute the partnership launch with clear milestones, stakeholder communication, and early-win targeting.

## Inputs
- Signed agreement and governance framework (03-governance.md). Integration workstreams.

## Process
1. Activate integration workstreams: technical, commercial, operational, communication.
2. Execute milestone map from signing to first value delivery.
3. Deploy resources per the partnership plan.
4. Communicate the partnership internally and externally per stakeholder plan.
5. Target a quick win within 30-60 days to build momentum.
6. Monitor top 5 integration risks.
7. Begin governance cadence from day one.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/partnership-blocks.md` (Block 4: Integration and Launch)
- `lib/components/risk-blocks.md` (integration risk tracking)
- `scripts/tracking/action-item-tracker.md`

## Output
Partnership launched. Quick win achieved. Governance cadence operating. Integration risks monitored.

## Next Phase
Transition to ongoing partnership governance per the governance framework.
