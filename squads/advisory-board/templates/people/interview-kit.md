## Interview Questions by Stage

### Stage 2: Hiring Manager Deep Dive

**Section A: Functional Expertise (20 min)**

1. "Walk me through the most significant [function-specific achievement] in your career. What was the starting state, what did you do, and what was the measurable outcome?"
   - **Listen for:** Specificity, personal ownership vs. team credit, measurable impact, honest assessment of what did not work.
   - **Red flag:** Vague descriptions, inability to quantify impact, takes credit for team work without acknowledgment.

2. "Describe a time you had to make a critical [function-specific] decision with incomplete information. What was the decision, what framework did you use, and how did it turn out?"
   - **Listen for:** Decision-making process, comfort with ambiguity, intellectual honesty about outcomes.
   - **Red flag:** Always waits for perfect information, cannot describe a framework, only shares successes.

3. "What is your diagnosis of [specific challenge relevant to this role at our company]? What would be your approach in the first 90 days?"
   - **Listen for:** Quality of analysis, appropriate humility about what they do not yet know, action orientation.
   - **Red flag:** Prescriptive answers without asking clarifying questions, over-confidence without context.

**Section B: Leadership and Strategic Thinking (20 min)**

4. "Tell me about a time you inherited or built a team. What was your approach to assessing talent, and what changes did you make?"
   - **Listen for:** Speed of assessment, willingness to make difficult people decisions, how they treat people during transitions.
   - **Red flag:** Never made a tough people call, describes team changes as easy, no empathy for those affected.

5. "Describe a strategic bet you championed that failed. What happened, and what did you learn?"
   - **Listen for:** Accountability, genuine learning, willingness to be vulnerable, quality of post-mortem thinking.
   - **Red flag:** Cannot name a failure, blames others, superficial learning ("I learned to communicate better").

6. "How do you think about the trade-off between speed and quality at a [company stage] company?"
   - **Listen for:** Stage-appropriate calibration, nuance, examples of navigating this tension.
   - **Red flag:** Dogmatic answers ("always move fast" or "never ship without X"), no examples from experience.

### Stage 4: Cross-Functional / Culture

**Section D: Collaboration and Values (30 min)**

10. "Tell me about a significant disagreement with a peer executive. How did you handle it, and what was the outcome?"
    - **Listen for:** Respect for other functions, willingness to escalate appropriately, resolution orientation.
    - **Red flag:** Frames all conflicts as the other person's fault, avoids conflict entirely, or is combative.

11. "How do you build trust with a new team that did not choose you?"
    - **Listen for:** Empathy, listening-first approach, concrete trust-building actions, patience with appropriate urgency.
    - **Red flag:** Assumes authority equals trust, plans to make immediate changes without learning.

12. "What kind of company culture do you do your best work in, and what kind drains you?"
    - **Listen for:** Self-awareness, alignment with our actual culture (not aspirational), honesty.
    - **Red flag:** Tells you what they think you want to hear, no self-awareness about cultural preferences.

### Stage 5: Executive Panel

**Section E: Executive Judgment (30 min)**

13. "If you were advising our board, what are the three biggest risks to our business in the next 18 months, and how would you address each?"
    - **Listen for:** Quality of external research, strategic thinking, prioritization ability.

14. "How do you communicate bad news upward? Give me a specific example."
    - **Listen for:** Transparency, timeliness, solution-orientation, courage.

- **5 — Exceptional:** Top 5% of candidates interviewed for similar roles. Clear and compelling evidence.

- **3 — Meets Bar:** Sufficient evidence of competence. No concerns.
- **2 — Below Bar:** Some evidence but gaps or concerns identified.
- **1 — Significant Concern:** Clear evidence of misfit or inability.
