# Organizational Design Change Memo

## Template Guidelines
**Word Limit:** 1,500 words maximum (excluding headers and tables)
**Reading Time:** Under 10 minutes
**Use When:** Any material change to organizational structure, reporting lines, team composition, or operating model that requires board awareness or approval.

### Executive Summary (75-100 words)
Describe the organizational change being proposed, why it is needed, and its expected impact on the business. Include scope (which teams, how many people), timeline, and the strategic objective this change supports.

---

### Strategic Context (150-200 words)
Explain the business situation driving this change. Address:
1. **Current State:** How is the organization structured today? What is working and what is not?
2. **Strategic Shift:** What business strategy change or growth milestone necessitates a new structure?
3. **Competitive Context:** How does this compare to how high-performing peers are organized?
4. **Cost of Inaction:** What happens if we do not make this change?
