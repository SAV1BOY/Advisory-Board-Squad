# Meeting Cadence

## Overview

The Advisory Board operates on a fixed meeting cadence designed to provide consistent oversight without creating unnecessary overhead. Every meeting has a clear purpose, format, and output.

## Meeting Schedule

### Quarterly Board Session

**Frequency:** Every 3 months (January, April, July, October)
**Duration:** 90-120 minutes
**Attendees:** All board agents + CEO
**Purpose:** Strategic review, decision-making, risk assessment

**Standing Agenda:**
| Block | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 5 min | Quorum, conflicts of interest, agenda overview |
| Strategy Review | 30 min | Strategic progress, market update, competitive landscape |
| Decision Queue | 30 min | Pre-read items requiring board decision |
| Risk Register | 15 min | Risk updates, new risks, mitigation status |
| Action Review | 10 min | Follow-through scorecard from previous meeting |
| Open Floor | 15 min | Emerging issues, advisor observations, cross-squad signals |
| Close | 5 min | Decisions summary, action items, next meeting |

**Pre-reads:** Distributed at T-5. Prepared by Strategy-Analyst, reviewed by Governance-Ops.
**Minutes:** Distributed within 24 hours. Prepared by Decision-Tracker, approved by Board-Chair.
**Follow-up:** Follow-through scorecard at T+21.

### Monthly Chair Check-In

**Frequency:** Monthly (months without a quarterly session)
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Attendees:** Board-Chair + CEO (others as needed)
**Purpose:** Action tracking, emerging issues, relationship maintenance

**Standing Agenda:**
| Block | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Action Item Review | 20 min | Status of all open items, blockers, escalations |
| Emerging Issues | 20 min | New developments that may affect the board's agenda |
| CEO Pulse | 15 min | CEO's assessment of the company and any support needed |
| Planning | 5 min | Confirm next meeting, pre-read owners, calendar |

**Output:** Updated action tracker. Brief summary notes (not full minutes).

### Ad-Hoc Crisis Call

**Frequency:** As needed (triggered by Board-Chair)
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Attendees:** All available board agents + CEO + crisis team leads
**Purpose:** Crisis assessment, decision-making, communication approval

**Agenda:** Determined by the crisis. Typically:
1. Situation briefing (10 min).
2. Board questions and assessment (20 min).
3. Decision items (20 min).
4. Communication review (10 min).

**Trigger:** Board-Chair activates per the crisis response protocol (workflow 07).

### Annual Planning Session

**Frequency:** Annually (Q3 or early Q4)
**Duration:** Half day (4 hours)
**Attendees:** All board agents + full executive team
**Purpose:** Strategic planning for the coming fiscal year

**Agenda:**
| Block | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Prior Year Review | 60 min | Performance vs. plan, lessons learned |
| Environmental Analysis | 45 min | Market trends, competitive landscape, risks and opportunities |
| Strategic Questions | 60 min | Identify and debate the key strategic choices |
| Priority Setting | 45 min | Agree on 3-5 priorities with owners |
| Wrap-Up | 30 min | Next steps, OKR development timeline, calendar |

**Output:** Strategic priorities, planning timeline, pre-read assignments for OKR session.

### Annual Effectiveness Review

**Frequency:** Annually (Q4)
**Duration:** 90 minutes
**Attendees:** All board agents
**Purpose:** Board self-assessment and improvement

**Agenda:**
| Block | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Survey Results | 30 min | Dimension scores, themes, year-over-year comparison |
| Decision Outcomes | 15 min | Review of decision quality over the past year |
| Discussion | 30 min | What to change, what to maintain, what to try |
| Action Plan | 15 min | 3-5 changes with owners, timelines, measures |

**Output:** Board Effectiveness Action Plan.

## Calendar Rules

1. **Lock dates 12 months in advance.** Send calendar holds for all quarterly sessions, the annual planning session, and the annual effectiveness review.
2. **No rescheduling without Board-Chair approval.** Moving a meeting requires a compelling reason and 2 weeks' notice.
3. **Pre-read deadlines are firm.** Materials due at T-5. Late materials are not included in the meeting — they roll to the next session.
4. **Monthly check-ins can be async.** If both the Board-Chair and CEO agree, the monthly check-in can be conducted via written exchange instead of a live meeting.
5. **Buffer for crises.** Hold one "flex" slot per quarter for ad-hoc sessions. If unused, cancel it 48 hours in advance.

## Attendance Expectations

- **Quarterly sessions:** Mandatory for all agents. Absence requires Board-Chair approval and a written input submission in advance.
- **Monthly check-ins:** Board-Chair and CEO mandatory. Others as invited.
- **Annual sessions:** Mandatory for all agents and the full executive team.
- **Crisis calls:** All available agents. Missing a crisis call is acceptable; missing the follow-up is not.

## Between Meetings

The board does not go silent between meetings. Standing between-meeting activities:
- Weekly action item status email (Governance-Ops → all owners).
- Monthly risk register update (Risk-Analyst → Board-Chair).
- Ongoing crisis monitoring (Risk-Analyst, with escalation to Board-Chair as needed).
- Monthly chair review (Board-Chair, workflow 20).
