# 13 — Culture Audit Flow

## Overview

A structured assessment of organizational culture — measuring the gap between stated values and lived behaviors. Produces a diagnosis of cultural strengths and dysfunctions, a targeted action plan, and ongoing measurement to ensure culture evolves intentionally rather than accidentally.

## Trigger

- Annual calendar (standing Q2 audit).
- Post-merger or post-acquisition integration.
- Significant leadership change (new CEO, executive team turnover >30 %).
- Org health turnaround (workflow 06) identifies culture as a root cause.
- Rapid growth (headcount doubles within 12 months).

## Agents

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| Culture-Guardian | Leads the audit, designs interventions |
| Board-Chair | Sponsors the audit, ensures executive accountability |
| Strategy-Analyst | Correlates culture data with business performance |
| Devil's-Advocate | Challenges the gap between rhetoric and reality |
| Governance-Ops | Administers surveys, manages logistics, archives findings |

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Survey Design & Deployment (Days 1-10)

1. Culture-Guardian designs the culture survey. Standard dimensions:
   - Values alignment: "I see our stated values reflected in daily decisions."
   - Psychological safety: "I can disagree with my manager without negative consequences."
   - Accountability: "Underperformance is addressed quickly and fairly."
   - Communication: "I understand the company's strategy and how my work connects to it."
   - Decision-making: "Decisions are made at the appropriate level with the right information."
   - Inclusion: "Diverse perspectives are genuinely sought and valued."
   - Growth: "I have opportunities to develop my skills and career here."
   - Trust in leadership: "I trust the leadership team to make good decisions for the company."
2. Use a 1-5 agreement scale with optional free-text comments for each dimension.
3. Include demographic segmentation (team, tenure, level) to identify pockets of dysfunction.
4. Governance-Ops deploys the survey with a 10-day response window. Target: ≥75 % response rate.
5. Anonymity is guaranteed and technically enforced. No segment with fewer than 5 respondents is reported.

### Phase 2 — Qualitative Interviews (Days 10-20)

1. Culture-Guardian conducts 15-20 structured interviews across:
   - Senior leaders (5-7): "What cultural behaviors do you reward? What do you tolerate that you shouldn't?"
   - Mid-level managers (5-7): "What messages are you receiving from above? What are you hearing from below?"
   - Individual contributors (5-6): "Describe a recent decision that felt right culturally. Describe one that felt wrong."
2. Observe artifacts: meeting behaviors, communication patterns, how conflict is handled in real time.
3. Review formal artifacts: performance review criteria, promotion decisions, who gets recognized and for what.
4. Devil's-Advocate interviews: "What would a skeptic say about our culture? What are we pretending not to know?"

### Phase 3 — Diagnosis (Days 20-30)

1. Culture-Guardian synthesizes quantitative and qualitative data:
   - Dimension scores with demographic breakdowns. Identify the highest and lowest scoring segments.
   - Theme analysis from free-text comments and interviews. What patterns emerge?
   - Values gap analysis: for each stated value, what is the evidence that it is lived? What is the evidence that it is not?
   - Cultural strengths: what is genuinely working well?
   - Cultural dysfunctions: what behaviors are tolerated that contradict stated values?
2. Strategy-Analyst correlates culture data with business performance:
   - Do high-engagement teams outperform? By how much?
   - Is there a correlation between psychological safety scores and innovation metrics?
   - Does cultural dysfunction correlate with attrition hot spots?
3. Produce the Culture Audit Report (5-7 pages):
   - Executive summary (1 page).
   - Quantitative results with visualizations (2 pages).
   - Qualitative themes and values gap analysis (2 pages).
   - Recommended actions (1-2 pages).

### Phase 4 — Action Planning (Days 30-40)

1. Board-Chair and Culture-Guardian present the audit to the executive team.
2. For each cultural dysfunction, design an intervention:
   - Structural: org design changes, process changes, incentive realignment.
   - Behavioral: leadership modeling, feedback protocols, meeting norms.
   - Symbolic: what the company celebrates, rewards, and punishes sends the strongest cultural signals.
3. Prioritize interventions: pick the 3-5 with the highest impact-to-effort ratio.
4. Each intervention gets an owner, timeline, success metric, and review date.
5. Devil's-Advocate asks: "Will leadership actually model this change, or is this a program for everyone else?"
6. Board reviews and approves the culture action plan.

### Phase 5 — Measurement & Iteration (Days 40-180)

1. Pulse surveys at Day 60 and Day 120: re-measure the 2-3 lowest-scoring dimensions.
2. Culture-Guardian monitors leading indicators: participation in cultural initiatives, behavioral observations, exit interview themes.
3. Strategy-Analyst tracks the business performance correlation: are improvements in culture metrics showing up in business metrics?
4. Board-Chair reviews progress at the quarterly board meeting.
5. At Day 180, Culture-Guardian produces a follow-up report:
   - What improved?
   - What remained unchanged?
   - What new issues emerged?
6. Findings feed into the next annual culture audit.
7. Archive the full audit package in `archive/culture-audits/YYYY/`.

## Quality Gates

| Gate | Criteria | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| G1 — Survey deployed | ≥75 % response rate achieved | Governance-Ops |
| G2 — Interviews complete | 15-20 interviews across three levels | Culture-Guardian |
| G3 — Diagnosis complete | Culture Audit Report with quantitative and qualitative findings | Culture-Guardian |
| G4 — Action plan approved | 3-5 interventions with owners, metrics, timelines | Board-Chair |
| G5 — Follow-up measured | Day 180 report with trend data | Culture-Guardian |

## Outputs

- Culture survey results (anonymized, segmented).
- Interview synthesis and theme analysis.
- Culture Audit Report (5-7 pages).
- Values gap analysis.
- Culture action plan with interventions.
- Pulse survey results (Day 60, Day 120).
- Follow-up report (Day 180).

## Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Survey response rate | ≥75 % |
| Interview coverage | ≥3 organizational levels, ≥4 functions |
| Values gap closure | ≥1 dimension improves by ≥0.5 points at Day 180 |
| Action plan implementation | ≥80 % of interventions executed on schedule |
| Business performance correlation | Positive correlation between culture improvement and performance metrics |
| Leadership modeling score | Improvement in "trust in leadership" dimension |

## Common Failures

| Failure Mode | Symptom | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Survey fatigue | Low response rate, superficial answers | Keep the survey short (10 min max); communicate why it matters and what happened last time |
| Leadership bubble | Leaders rate culture high while ICs rate it low | Demographic segmentation exposes the gap; present it directly |
| Action without modeling | Programs launched but leadership behavior unchanged | Devil's Advocate asks: "What are leaders doing differently?" |
| Anonymity breach | Employees fear identification from their comments | Technical guarantees; minimum segment size of 5; external survey tool if needed |
| One-time event | Audit happens once, never repeated | Annual calendar; Board-Chair treats it as a standing commitment |
| Correlation confusion | Attributing business results to culture changes without evidence | Strategy-Analyst uses proper controls and acknowledges confounders |
