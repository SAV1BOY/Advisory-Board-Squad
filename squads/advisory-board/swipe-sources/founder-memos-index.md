# Founder Memos Index

## Overview
An index of notable founder memos, internal communications, and strategic documents that have shaped company trajectories and become public domain knowledge.

## Essential Founder Memos

### Steve Jobs — "Thoughts on Flash" (2010)
- **Context:** Apple's decision not to support Flash on iOS
- **Key insight:** How to communicate a controversial technology decision publicly with clear reasoning
- **Why it matters:** Template for explaining strategic technology choices to stakeholders

### Stewart Butterfield — "We Don't Sell Saddles Here" (Slack, 2013)
- **Context:** Internal memo to Slack team before launch
- **Key insight:** Sell the transformation, not the product. Customers don't want messaging software; they want organizational transformation.
- **Why it matters:** The best product positioning memo ever written. Essential reading for any product launch.

### Brian Chesky — "Don't F*** Up the Culture" (Airbnb, 2014)
- **Context:** CEO email to team as Airbnb scaled rapidly
- **Key insight:** Culture is the only thing that persists when everything else changes. Protect it fiercely.
- **Why it matters:** Demonstrates CEO responsibility for culture during hypergrowth

### Mark Zuckerberg — IPO Letter (Facebook, 2012)
- **Context:** Founder's letter included in Facebook's S-1 filing
- **Key insight:** "We don't build services to make money; we make money to build better services." Mission-first framing for public market investors.
- **Why it matters:** Template for communicating long-term mission to short-term-oriented investors

### Larry Page — Founders' IPO Letter (Google, 2004)
- **Context:** Google's S-1 filing included a direct letter from founders
- **Key insight:** Dual-class share structure explained and justified. "Don't be evil" as a governance principle.
- **Why it matters:** How to structure governance that protects founder vision through public markets

### Reed Hastings — "Netflix Culture: Freedom and Responsibility" (2009)
- **Context:** Internal culture deck that was made public
- **Key insight:** Culture is not perks—it's how you make decisions, who you hire, and who you fire.
- **Why it matters:** Most influential culture document in tech history

### Satya Nadella — "Hit Refresh" Memo (Microsoft, 2014)
- **Context:** New CEO's first communication to Microsoft employees
- **Key insight:** Growth mindset as organizational transformation strategy. "Our industry does not respect tradition—it only respects innovation."
- **Why it matters:** Template for new CEO communication when transforming company culture

## Specialized Founder Memos

### Ben Thompson — "Aggregation Theory" (Stratechery)
- Not a company memo but an independent analysis that became a foundational strategy framework for tech companies

### Keith Rabois — Various essays on operations
- Operational philosophy essays that influenced how a generation of startup operators think about scaling

### Elad Gil — "High Growth Handbook" (supplementary materials)
- Interviews and memos from founders navigating hypergrowth transitions

## Board Application
- Share relevant memos with founders as reference material when they face similar decisions
- Use the memo structures as templates for the company's own internal and external communications
- Study how the best founders communicate strategic decisions to build a shared communication standard
