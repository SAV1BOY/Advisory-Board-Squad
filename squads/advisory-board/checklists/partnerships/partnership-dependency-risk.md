# Partnership Dependency Risk Checklist

## Purpose
Assesses and manages the risk created by organizational dependency on partners. Partnerships create value but also create vulnerability when the organization cannot function without the partner. This checklist ensures dependency is intentional, bounded, and mitigated.

## When to Use
- Before entering significant partnerships.
- Annually during partnership reviews.
- When a partner's situation changes (acquisition, strategy shift, financial trouble).
- When the organization's dependency on a partner increases.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Dependency level for each partner is assessed (low, moderate, high, critical).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Revenue or operational dependency on any single partner is quantified.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Alternative options (other partners, internal capability) are identified for each critical dependency.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The cost and timeline for transitioning away from a critical partner is estimated.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Partner financial stability and strategic commitment are monitored.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Contractual protections (SLAs, transition support, data portability) are in place.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization maintains enough internal capability to survive a partner failure.

## Red Flags
- A partner provides a critical service and no alternative has been identified.
- The organization has outsourced a core competency entirely to a partner.
- The partner has the power to dictate terms due to the organization's dependency.
- No one monitors the partner's financial or strategic health.

## Escalation Triggers
- A critical partner shows signs of financial distress or strategic divergence.
- A partner is acquired by a competitor.
- The partner unilaterally changes terms in a way that harms the organization.
- Dependency on a single partner exceeds the board-approved threshold.
