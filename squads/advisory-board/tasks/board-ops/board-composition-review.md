# Task: Board Composition Review

## Objective
Assess whether the current board composition matches the company's current and anticipated needs. Identify skill gaps, diversity shortfalls, and rotation opportunities. Recommend changes.

## Trigger
- Annually (aligned with board effectiveness review)
- When a board seat becomes vacant
- Significant company stage change (e.g., approaching IPO, entering new market)
- Board effectiveness review identifies composition gaps

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Chair
- **Support:** Board Ops, CEO
- **Advisory:** Governance advisor or legal counsel (if governance changes needed)

- [ ] Current skills matrix exists

- [ ] Company strategic plan current
- [ ] Board member term status known

## Steps

1. **Update Skills Matrix**
   - Review and update the skills matrix against current company needs
   - Add any new competency requirements driven by strategy (e.g., AI expertise, international expansion)
   - Assess each member's current contribution level against their mapped competencies
   - Identify gaps: which critical competencies have no strong board-level coverage?

2. **Assess Current Members**
   - For each member: tenure, attendance record, contribution quality, engagement level, term status
   - Flag members approaching term limits or expressing desire to step down
   - Candidly assess any members whose contribution has declined or whose expertise is no longer aligned

3. **Diversity Assessment**
   - Assess board diversity across: gender, ethnicity, age, professional background, industry experience, geographic perspective, thinking style
   - Compare to governance best practices and any commitments made to investors or stakeholders
   - Identify specific diversity dimensions to prioritize in any future additions

4. **Forward-Looking Needs Analysis**
   - Based on the 12-24 month strategic plan, what new competencies will the board need?
   - Anticipate stage transitions: what expertise is needed for the next phase (fundraise, international expansion, M&A, pre-IPO)?
   - Consider whether the board size should change

5. **Develop Recommendations**
   - Recommend: additions, rotations, or retirements
   - For additions: define the ideal profile and begin target list development
   - For rotations: propose graceful transition timelines
   - For the full board: recommend any structural changes (committees, chair rotation)

6. **Board Discussion and Approval**
   - Present composition review to the full board (or executive session if sensitive)
   - Agree on changes and timeline
   - Assign recruitment responsibilities for new member searches

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Skills Matrix:** Competency mapping across all members
- **Stage-Fit Analysis:** Match board composition to company stage requirements
- **Diversity Scorecard:** Multi-dimensional diversity assessment

## Checklists

### Composition Quality Gate
- [ ] Skills matrix updated and gaps identified
- [ ] All members assessed on contribution and engagement
- [ ] Diversity assessment completed
- [ ] Forward-looking needs analyzed against strategic plan
- [ ] Recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Sensitive conversations planned with care and respect

## Output
- Updated skills matrix
- Board composition assessment report
- Recommendations for changes (additions, rotations, structural)
- Target profiles for any new member searches

## Handoffs
- New member recruitment feeds into board-setup.md (member recruitment section)
- Rotation plans communicated to affected members by Chair
- Updated composition informs annual-planning-session.md

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Skills matrix coverage | >80% of critical competencies covered | Matrix audit |
| Diversity improvement | Year-over-year improvement on priority dimensions | Annual comparison |
| Member engagement | All members attend >75% of meetings | Attendance records |
| Composition-strategy alignment | Board composition matches stage needs | Chair assessment |
