## Scoring Guide

| Score | Definition |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Dominant — industry-leading position that would take years and significant capital for competitors to overcome |
| 7-8 | Strong — clear advantage recognized by customers and competitors, with active investment to maintain |
| 5-6 | Moderate — some advantage but competitors are closing the gap or could with focused effort |
| 3-4 | Weak — minimal advantage, easily replicable, or actively being eroded |
| 1-2 | None — no meaningful advantage in this dimension |
