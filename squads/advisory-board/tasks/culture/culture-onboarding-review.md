# Task: Culture Onboarding Review

## Objective
Assess how effectively the company's onboarding process transmits culture to new employees. Ensure new hires understand, experience, and can practice the company's values from day one.

## Trigger
- Semi-annually or after significant changes to the onboarding program
- When new hire attrition in the first 6 months exceeds target
- When engagement scores for employees with less than 1 year tenure are below company average
- After the company adopts new or revised values

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CHRO or People Ops Lead
- **Support:** Hiring managers, recent hires, onboarding program designers
- **Advisory:** Board sponsor for culture if one exists


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → culture-audit`

- **Lead:** culture-steward (Brene Brown)
- **Support:** talent-advisor (Patrick Lencioni), governance-clerk (Simon Sinek)
- **Review:** board-chair (Board Chair)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current onboarding program documented
- [ ] New hire survey data available
- [ ] First-year attrition data available
- [ ] Current values and culture materials available
- [ ] Feedback from recent hires within 6 months collected

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Review current onboarding program content for cultural transmission quality assessing whether values are taught, modeled, and practiced or just presented in a slide deck

2. **Step 2:** Interview recent hires at 30, 60, and 90 day marks about their cultural onboarding experience asking what they understand about the culture and how they learned it

3. **Step 3:** Assess whether managers are equipped and accountable for cultural onboarding of their new hires

4. **Step 4:** Evaluate the buddy or mentor program effectiveness in cultural integration

5. **Step 5:** Identify gaps between intended cultural onboarding and actual experience

6. **Step 6:** Develop improvements with specific curriculum changes, manager enablement, and measurement mechanisms

7. **Step 7:** Pilot improvements with the next cohort and measure impact

8. **Step 8:** Culture Onboarding Assessment Report; Gap analysis; Improved onboarding curriculum; Manager enablement guide; Measurement plan

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Competing Values Framework:** Assess culture across clan, adhocracy, market, and hierarchy dimensions
- **Edgar Schein's Three Levels:** Analyze culture at the artifact, espoused values, and underlying assumptions levels
- **Behavioral Indicators:** Ground all cultural assessment in observable behaviors, not aspirational statements

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment combines quantitative data and qualitative insights
- [ ] Multiple organizational levels and functions represented in data collection
- [ ] Findings distinguish between symptoms and root causes
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Success metrics defined with measurement plan
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (culture change takes quarters, not weeks)
- [ ] Leadership commitment secured before proceeding

## Output
- Onboarding improvements feed into the standard onboarding process
- Manager development needs feed into leadership-coaching
- Cultural gaps feed into culture-audit
- New hire feedback informs culture-health-tracker
