# Task: Organization Design Review

## Objective
Assess whether the current organizational structure supports the company strategy and scale requirements. Recommend structural changes to improve execution velocity and clarity.

## Trigger
- Annual planning identifies structural issues
- Post-acquisition integration requires restructuring
- Rapid growth creates structural strain
- Board or CEO identifies coordination failures
- New strategy requires different organizational capabilities

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or COO
- **Support:** CHRO, functional leaders
- **Advisory:** Board members with organizational expertise

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current org chart and role descriptions available
- [ ] Strategic priorities defined
- [ ] Performance data by team available
- [ ] Employee feedback on cross-functional friction available

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Map current organization against strategic priorities identifying structural support and gaps

2. **Step 2:** Assess span of control, layers of management, and decision-making velocity

3. **Step 3:** Identify coordination failures, duplicated efforts, and organizational bottlenecks

4. **Step 4:** Develop 2-3 structural options with trade-offs for each

5. **Step 5:** Model the transition plan including communication, timeline, and people impact

6. **Step 6:** Present to board if changes involve executive reporting or significant headcount shifts

7. **Step 7:** Implement approved changes with clear communication plan

8. **Step 8:** Revised org structure; Transition plan; Updated role definitions; Communication plan

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Structured Assessment:** Use consistent frameworks and rubrics to reduce bias and increase rigor
- **Data-Driven People Decisions:** Ground all people decisions in observable evidence, not gut feeling
- **Stakeholder Triangulation:** Gather input from multiple perspectives (manager, peers, reports, cross-functional) for balanced assessment

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment is grounded in specific, observable evidence
- [ ] Multiple perspectives gathered (not single-source input)
- [ ] Outcomes and recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Confidentiality maintained throughout the process
- [ ] Legal and compliance requirements met
- [ ] Timeline and owners assigned for all actions

## Output
- Structural changes may trigger exec-hiring for new roles
- Role changes feed into performance-review-exec
- Org health monitored through team-health-check
