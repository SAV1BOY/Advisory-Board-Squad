# Competitive Intelligence Template

## Key Findings

1. **Structured competitive intelligence beats ad hoc monitoring.** Companies with a formalized CI process make faster strategic decisions (2.3x faster response to competitive moves) and are 40% less likely to be surprised by a competitor's product launch or pricing change.
2. **Most startups over-index on product comparison and under-index on go-to-market intelligence.** Feature matrices are necessary but insufficient. Competitive advantage is increasingly determined by distribution, pricing strategy, and ecosystem partnerships rather than feature parity.
3. **Competitive intelligence decays rapidly.** Competitor positioning, pricing, and strategy shifts every 3-6 months in high-growth markets. CI that is older than 90 days should be treated as provisional.
4. **Win/loss analysis is the highest-ROI competitive activity.** Systematic post-deal interviews (both wins and losses) produce more actionable intelligence than market research, analyst reports, or social media monitoring combined.
5. **Boards that review competitive intelligence quarterly make better strategic allocation decisions.** Board-level CI review should focus on strategic shifts (new market entries, funding rounds, leadership changes, M&A), not feature-level comparisons.

## Data Points

| Element | Best Practice | Common Failure |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| CI update cadence | Monthly tactical, quarterly strategic | Annual competitive slide that is stale by Q2 |
| Win/loss interviews per quarter | 8-12 (mix of wins and losses) | Zero — sales team self-reports without customer interviews |
| Competitor profiles maintained | Top 5 direct + 3 adjacent | 20+ profiles, none updated |
| Time from competitor signal to board awareness | < 2 weeks for material moves | 3-6 months or never |
| CI ownership | Dedicated owner (Product Marketing or Strategy) | "Everyone's job" which means nobody's job |

## Template Structure

### Competitor Profile

```markdown
# [Competitor Name] — Intelligence Profile

**Last updated:** [Date]
**Confidence level:** High / Medium / Low
**Updated by:** [Name]

## Company Overview
- **Founded:** [Year] | **HQ:** [Location] | **Employees:** [Count]
- **Total funding:** [Amount] | **Last round:** [Type, Amount, Date]
- **Revenue estimate:** [Range] | **Growth rate estimate:** [Range]

## Strategic Positioning
- **Target market:** [Segments, verticals, geography]
- **Value proposition:** [One sentence — what they claim to do better]
- **Pricing model:** [Structure, entry price, enterprise price]
- **Key differentiators:** [2-3 genuine differentiators, not marketing claims]

## Product Intelligence
- **Core capabilities:** [Bulleted list]
- **Recent launches (last 6 months):** [Features, products]
- **Roadmap signals:** [Job postings, patents, conference talks, beta features]
- **Technical architecture:** [Known stack, infrastructure choices]

## Go-to-Market Intelligence
- **Sales motion:** [PLG / Sales-led / Hybrid / Channel]
- **Key partnerships:** [Technology, channel, ecosystem]
- **Marketing positioning shifts:** [Recent messaging changes]
- **Customer wins/losses (known):** [Notable logos gained or lost]

## Leadership and Organizational Signals
- **Key hires (last 6 months):** [Notable executive or leadership hires]
- **Key departures:** [Notable exits]
- **Hiring patterns:** [What roles are they hiring for? Volume signals]

## Threat Assessment
- **Overlap with our market:** [High / Medium / Low]
- **Trajectory:** [Growing threat / Stable / Declining threat]
- **Most likely competitive move (next 12 months):** [Prediction with reasoning]
- **Our vulnerability:** [Where could they take our customers?]
- **Our advantage:** [Where do we win against them and why?]
```

## Implications for Board

- **Quarterly CI briefing.** Include a 1-page competitive landscape summary in every quarterly board pre-read. Focus on strategic shifts, not feature comparisons.
- **Win/loss program mandate.** The board should verify that a structured win/loss interview program exists and is producing actionable insights. Ask to see the last quarter's win/loss summary.
- **Competitive response governance.** Not every competitor move requires a response. The board should help management distinguish between moves that require strategic response and those that should be ignored.
- **Adjacent competition monitoring.** The most dangerous competitors are often not the obvious ones. Include 2-3 adjacent-market companies in monitoring — the startup that could pivot into your space or the platform that could build your feature.

## Update Frequency

- **Template review:** Annually (update fields and structure)
- **Individual competitor profiles:** Monthly refresh for top 3, quarterly for others
- **Board-level CI summary:** Quarterly
- **Trigger-based update:** Immediately upon competitor funding, M&A, leadership change, or major product launch
