# Executive Talent Market Trends

## Key Findings

1. **Executive tenure is compressing.** Median CEO tenure at venture-backed startups has declined from 5.2 years (2020) to 3.8 years (2025). CTO tenure is even shorter at 2.9 years. This acceleration creates board-level succession planning urgency — the next CEO transition is statistically closer than most boards assume.
2. **Fractional and interim executives are mainstreaming.** 34% of Series A-B companies now employ at least one fractional C-suite executive (typically CFO or CMO). This is no longer a compromise — it reflects rational capital allocation at the growth stage.
3. **Compensation structures are shifting toward equity-heavy packages.** Base salary growth has plateaued at 3-4% annually for executive roles, but total compensation continues to climb through equity. Top-quartile VP Engineering packages now include 0.3-0.8% equity grants at Series B, up from 0.15-0.4% in 2022.
4. **AI and data leadership is the scarcest skill set.** Time-to-fill for VP of AI/ML roles averages 6.4 months, compared to 3.1 months for VP of Sales. Companies that begin executive AI hiring before demonstrating product-market fit for AI features face the longest search times.
5. **Remote-first has permanently expanded the talent pool but complicated culture.** 61% of executive hires in 2025 were remote or hybrid. However, companies with fully distributed executive teams report 1.4x higher conflict escalation rates compared to co-located teams.
6. **Diversity in executive hiring has stalled.** After gains from 2020-2023, the percentage of underrepresented groups in C-suite hires has plateaued at 28%. Board-level attention to diversity pipeline programs is required to resume progress.

## Implications for Board

- **Succession planning cadence.** Given compressing tenures, the board should maintain a warm successor shortlist for CEO, CTO, and CFO roles, refreshed annually. Do not wait for a vacancy to begin the search.
- **Compensation benchmarking.** Equity-heavy compensation requires the board to understand dilution impact. Every executive hire or retention package should include a dilution analysis presented alongside the offer terms.
- **Fractional executive strategy.** For pre-Series C companies, the board should actively endorse fractional CFO and CMO roles where full-time hires would be premature. This preserves capital without sacrificing capability.
- **AI talent pipeline.** If the company's strategy depends on AI capabilities, begin VP-level AI hiring 6-9 months before the capability is needed. The board should verify this timeline in workforce planning discussions.
- **Remote leadership risk.** If the executive team is distributed, the board should monitor culture health scores and conflict escalation rates with additional rigor. See culture-health-score.md.
