# Mission Integrity Initiative: Tradeoffs

## Objective
Identify and document the specific tradeoffs between mission purity and business pragmatism, making them visible rather than leaving them as hidden compromises.

## Inputs
- Initiative brief (00-brief.md). Current business practices. Revenue model. Supply chain. Product decisions.

## Process
1. Map every area where mission and commercial interests potentially conflict.
2. For each conflict, document: the mission ideal, the current practice, the gap, and the business reason for the gap.
3. Classify each gap: intentional compromise (acknowledged), unintentional drift (not previously recognized), or systemic conflict (built into the business model).
4. Assess which gaps are acceptable (pragmatic compromise) and which are unacceptable (integrity violations).
5. Quantify the cost of closing each gap where possible.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/utilities/incentive-map.md` (incentives driving mission-compromising behavior)
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` Block 3 (explicit tradeoffs)

## Output
Tradeoff map document. Classification of all mission-commerce conflicts. Priority list for resolution.
