# Lencioni Ideal Team Player Check Checklist

## Purpose
Evaluates individuals and teams against Lencioni's Ideal Team Player model: humble, hungry, and smart (people-smart). Lencioni argues that team players who possess all three virtues are dramatically more effective and less toxic than those who lack one or more. This checklist provides a framework for hiring, evaluating, and developing team members.

## When to Use
- During hiring processes to assess candidate fit for team-based environments.
- During performance reviews to provide specific developmental feedback.
- When diagnosing team dynamics issues.
- When coaching individuals on interpersonal effectiveness.
- When building teams for new initiatives.

## Gate Criteria

### Humble
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual shares credit for successes and takes ownership of failures.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual shows genuine interest in others' contributions and acknowledges them.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual does not seek disproportionate attention or recognition.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual is willing to do work below their title or skill level when needed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual's self-assessment is accurate, neither inflated nor deflated.

### Hungry
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual goes beyond minimum requirements without being asked.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual is always looking for the next thing to do or improve.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual has a strong work ethic and is self-motivated.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual thinks about the organization's needs, not just their own tasks.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual demonstrates passion and energy for the work.

### Smart (People-Smart)
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual reads situations and adjusts their communication style.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual is aware of the impact of their words and actions on others.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual navigates group dynamics constructively.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual builds relationships across the organization, not just within their team.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The individual handles conflict with emotional intelligence.

### Team Assessment
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team as a whole demonstrates a balance of all three virtues.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Team members who lack one virtue are identified and supported with development plans.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Hiring criteria include assessment of all three virtues.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team culture reinforces humble, hungry, and smart behaviors.

## Red Flags
- An individual is hungry and smart but not humble (the skilled politician who advances at others' expense).
- An individual is humble and smart but not hungry (the lovable slacker who is pleasant but does not drive results).
- An individual is humble and hungry but not smart (the accidental destroyer who works hard but damages relationships).
- A team tolerates individuals who lack one or more virtues because of their technical skill.
- Hiring focuses only on technical capability with no assessment of team-player virtues.

## Escalation Triggers
- An individual's lack of one virtue is causing measurable team dysfunction.
- Repeated feedback on a missing virtue has not resulted in improvement.
- A hiring mistake is made because team-player virtues were not assessed.
- Team members are reluctant to work with a specific individual due to consistent non-team-player behavior.
- The team's overall performance is limited by one or two individuals who drag down collaboration.
