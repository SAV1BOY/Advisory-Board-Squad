# M&A: Evaluation

## Objective
Conduct detailed evaluation of priority targets including strategic, financial, and cultural analysis to determine which (if any) merit formal diligence.

## Inputs
- Screened targets (00-screening.md). Public financial data. Product and market analysis.

## Process
1. Build a detailed strategic case for each target: what capability, customer base, or market position do they provide?
2. Estimate financial value: revenue, growth, margins, customer quality, technology value.
3. Assess cultural compatibility using culture-blocks.md Block 6.
4. Identify integration risks and synergy opportunities.
5. Conduct preliminary valuation modeling.
6. Present evaluation to board with recommendation on which targets to pursue.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/utilities/ev-calculator.md`, `lib/utilities/scenario-table.md`
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 6, `lib/components/risk-blocks.md`

## Output
Detailed evaluation report per target. Valuation models. Board recommendation on targets to pursue.
