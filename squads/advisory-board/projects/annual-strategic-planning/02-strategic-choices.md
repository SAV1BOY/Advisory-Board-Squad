# Annual Strategic Planning: Strategic Choices

## Objective
Make the explicit strategic choices for the coming year: where to play, how to win, what to stop, and how to sequence.

## Inputs
- Retrospective findings (00-retrospective.md). Market analysis (01-market-analysis.md). Current capabilities and financial position.

## Process
1. Revalidate or update the strategic diagnosis using strategy-blocks.md Block 2.
2. Generate 3-5 distinct strategic directions for the coming year.
3. Evaluate each direction: scenario table, EV calculation, incentive map, pre-mortem.
4. Make the strategic choice: guiding policy, where to play, how to win, what we will NOT do.
5. Define the strategic sequence: which moves come first and why.
6. Record the strategic choice in the decision registry with dissent captured.
7. Communicate the strategy to the full organization.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` Blocks 2-3
- `lib/utilities/ev-calculator.md`, `lib/utilities/scenario-table.md`, `lib/utilities/incentive-map.md`
- `lib/patterns/pre-mortem-pattern.md`, `lib/patterns/dissent-and-commit-pattern.md`
- `authority/workshop-kits/strategic-planning-workshop.md`

## Output
Strategic plan document with explicit choices, tradeoffs, and sequencing. Decision registry entry. Team communication.
