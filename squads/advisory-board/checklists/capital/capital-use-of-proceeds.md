# Capital Use of Proceeds Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that capital raised from external or internal sources is allocated to specific, high-return uses with accountability and tracking. Use of proceeds is a commitment to capital providers and a discipline for the organization. This checklist prevents the common failure of raising capital with specific intentions but spending it on general operations or low-return activities.

## When to Use
- When preparing fundraising materials that include use of proceeds.
- After capital is raised, to ensure deployment matches commitments.
- During quarterly reviews of capital deployment versus plan.
- When seeking board approval for reallocation of previously committed capital.

## Gate Criteria

### Allocation Specificity
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Every major use of proceeds is specified with dollar amounts, not percentages.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each allocation maps to a specific initiative or capability with expected outcomes.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The allocation includes a contingency reserve (minimum 10-15% of total).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Allocations are prioritized: if less capital is raised, the highest-priority uses are funded first.

### Return Expectations
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Expected return for each allocation is quantified with timeline.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The aggregate return on all allocations justifies the cost of the capital raised.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Return expectations are realistic based on the organization's track record.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The highest-return allocations receive the most capital.

### Tracking and Accountability
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Actual deployment is tracked against planned use of proceeds monthly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Variances between planned and actual deployment are explained to the board.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each allocation has a named owner accountable for deployment and return.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Results are measured against the stated expectations at 6 and 12 months.

### Governance
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Reallocation of proceeds requires board or appropriate authority approval.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The rationale for any reallocation is documented.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Investors are informed of material changes to use of proceeds.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Use of proceeds compliance is included in financial reporting.

## Red Flags
- Use of proceeds is described as "general corporate purposes" with no specifics.
- Capital raised with specific intended use is quietly redirected to operating expenses.
- No one tracks whether capital was deployed as committed.
- Return on deployed capital is never measured or reported.
- Allocations are changed without board awareness or approval.

## Escalation Triggers
- Actual deployment deviates from plan by more than 25% without board approval.
- Capital is deployed to an initiative not included in the use of proceeds without reauthorization.
- Returns on deployed capital are materially below expectations.
- An investor or board member raises concerns about use of proceeds compliance.
- The organization raises additional capital without demonstrating return on previous capital.
