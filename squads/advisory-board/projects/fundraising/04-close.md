# Fundraising: Close

## Objective
Execute the closing process efficiently, complete legal documentation, transfer funds, and finalize all governance changes associated with the new investment.

## Inputs
- Agreed term sheet (03-terms.md).
- Legal counsel for definitive documentation.
- Cap table management system.
- Board composition changes (if any).

## Process
1. Engage legal counsel to draft definitive documents (stock purchase agreement, investor rights agreement, right of first refusal, voting agreement).
2. Complete investor due diligence requirements: legal, financial, technical, and commercial.
3. Negotiate final document terms (definitive docs sometimes differ from term sheet on details).
4. Obtain board approval for the final transaction.
5. Execute documents and transfer funds.
6. Update cap table, board composition, and governance documents.
7. File any required regulatory notices.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/capital-blocks.md` (Block 1: updated capital position post-close)
- `data/registries/stakeholder-registry.yaml` (add new investor)
- `data/registries/decision-registry.yaml` (record the fundraise decision)

## Output
- Executed investment documents.
- Funds in the bank.
- Updated cap table and governance documents.
- Board composition updated.
