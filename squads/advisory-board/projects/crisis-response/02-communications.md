# Crisis Response: Communications

## Objective
Manage the narrative with stakeholders honestly and proactively. Communicate early but never before you have something true and useful to say.

## Inputs
- Containment status (01-containment.md). Stakeholder map. Facts confirmed vs. unconfirmed.

## Process
1. Develop stakeholder-specific messages per crisis-blocks.md Block 4.
2. Sequence communications: typically board first, then employees, then affected customers, then broader public.
3. Designate spokesperson(s) for each audience.
4. Prepare Q&A for anticipated questions.
5. Establish ongoing communication cadence: how often updates will be provided.
6. Monitor public response and adjust messaging as needed.
7. Principles: lead with what you know, acknowledge what you do not, commit to updates, never speculate.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/crisis-blocks.md` (Block 4: Stakeholder Communication Plan)
- `lib/taxonomies/stakeholder-types.md`

## Output
Stakeholder communications executed. Q&A document live. Communication cadence established.
