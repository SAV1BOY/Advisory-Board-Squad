# Fundraising: Brief

## Objective
Define the parameters, strategy, and readiness criteria for a fundraising round. This brief ensures the company approaches fundraising with clarity about why capital is needed, how much to raise, and what terms are acceptable.

## Inputs
- Current financial position (cash, burn, runway) from capital-blocks.md.
- Growth metrics and trajectory.
- Strategic plan requiring capital.
- Board input on timing and approach.

## Process
1. Validate the fundraising trigger: why now? What milestone requires external capital?
2. Determine the instrument: equity, SAFE, convertible note, debt, or revenue-based financing.
3. Set target amount and acceptable range.
4. Define use of proceeds with specific allocation by category.
5. Establish the milestone the raised capital must achieve (what must be true before the next raise).
6. Set timeline: target close date, key milestones (deck ready, first meetings, term sheets, close).
7. Define walkaway conditions: terms or investor behaviors that kill the deal.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/capital-blocks.md` (Block 1: Capital Position, Block 4: Fundraising Parameters)
- `lib/utilities/ev-calculator.md` (ROI on capital deployment)

## Output
- Fundraising brief (2-3 pages) with all parameters defined.
- Board-approved fundraising mandate.
- Timeline with milestones and owners.
