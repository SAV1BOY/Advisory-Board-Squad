# Product-Market Fit and Growth

## Defining Product-Market Fit

### The Sean Ellis Test
Survey existing users: "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?"
- **> 40% say "Very disappointed"** = PMF achieved
- **25-40%** = Getting close, iterate on positioning and features
- **< 25%** = Not there yet, fundamental product or market changes needed

### Behavioral Indicators of PMF
- Organic growth (word of mouth, inbound) exceeds paid acquisition
- Usage frequency increases over time (not just at signup)
- Customers actively resist switching to alternatives
- Revenue retention (NRR) exceeds 100%
- Sales cycles shorten as product reputation spreads
- Customer support requests shift from "how does this work?" to "can it also do X?"

### Common PMF Mistakes
- Confusing initial sales with PMF (anyone can sell to early adopters)
- Declaring PMF based on revenue growth funded by heavy marketing spend
- Optimizing product for vocal minority rather than core market segment
- Premature scaling before PMF is validated (the #1 startup killer)

## Growth Frameworks

### The Growth Equation
Growth = Acquisition x Activation x Retention x Revenue x Referral (AARRR)
- **Retention is the foundation.** Without retention, all acquisition spend leaks out.
- **Optimize in order:** Retention → Activation → Acquisition → Revenue → Referral

### Growth Loops vs. Funnels
- **Funnels:** Linear, one-directional (awareness → interest → purchase). Each stage leaks.
- **Loops:** Self-reinforcing cycles where output becomes input. More users → more content → more value → more users.
- **Types of loops:** Content loops, viral loops, paid acquisition loops, sales loops, user-generated data loops

### The S-Curve of Growth
- **Phase 1: Slow start** — Building product, finding PMF, early customers
- **Phase 2: Rapid growth** — PMF achieved, growth loops activated, scaling
- **Phase 3: Maturation** — Market saturation, growth decelerates, efficiency becomes critical
- **Phase 4: Decline or Reinvention** — Find new S-curves or stagnate

## Board Application
- Do not approve scaling spend (sales, marketing, international) until PMF is demonstrated through behavioral indicators
- Monitor retention as the primary health metric, not just growth
- Ask whether growth is loop-driven (sustainable) or spend-driven (unsustainable)
- When growth decelerates, diagnose whether it's market saturation (needs new S-curve) or execution failure (needs operational improvement)
