# Hiring Blocks

## Purpose

Reusable structural blocks for executive and critical-role hiring decisions at board level. These blocks ensure hiring conversations move beyond "gut feel" to structured evaluation, reduce bias, and create an auditable record of why someone was hired, passed, or rejected. Board-level hiring scrutiny typically applies to C-suite, VP-level, and any role where a bad hire creates existential risk.

## Structure

### Block 1 — Role Scorecard

Defines the role with enough precision that evaluation is objective.

**Fields:**
- **Role title:** Standardized title with reporting line.
- **Mission statement:** One sentence describing the role's reason for existing.
- **Outcomes (12 months):** 3-5 measurable results the hire must deliver in year one.
- **Competencies:** 5-7 skills or capabilities required, each rated as Must-Have or Nice-to-Have.
- **Culture attributes:** Behavioral characteristics that predict success in this organization specifically.
- **Anti-patterns:** Candidate characteristics that historically predict failure in this role or company.
- **Compensation range:** Base, equity, bonus, and total comp range with market benchmark source.
- **Hiring urgency:** Critical path impact of leaving the role unfilled (quantify if possible).

### Block 2 — Sourcing Strategy

Plans where and how candidates will be found.

**Fields:**
- **Target profile:** Ideal background, company stage experience, and domain expertise.
- **Sourcing channels:** Executive search firms, network referrals, inbound, direct outreach — with allocation across each.
- **Diversity commitment:** Specific targets for candidate slate diversity and process safeguards.
- **Search firm terms:** If applicable, fee structure, exclusivity, guarantee period.
- **Pipeline targets:** Number of candidates needed at each stage (sourced > screened > interviewed > finalist).
- **Timeline:** Key dates for each phase and target offer date.

### Block 3 — Interview Architecture

Structures the evaluation process to cover all scorecard dimensions.

**Fields:**
- **Interview stages:** Sequential list of interview steps with purpose of each.
- **Interviewers per stage:** Who participates and what they evaluate.
- **Competency coverage map:** Matrix showing which interview covers which scorecard competency.
- **Case study or work sample:** Description of any practical exercise and evaluation criteria.
- **Cultural interview:** Specific scenarios or questions designed to surface cultural fit/add.
- **Debrief format:** How interview feedback is collected (written before discussion, scored independently).
- **Bias safeguards:** Structured scoring, independent write-ups, diverse interview panel.

### Block 4 — Reference Architecture

Structures reference checks to surface real signal rather than confirmation.

**Fields:**
- **Reference types required:** Provided references, backchannel references, subordinate references, peer references.
- **Minimum reference count:** Number required per type (recommend 2 provided + 2 backchannel minimum).
- **Key questions per reference:** Standardized questions that map to scorecard competencies.
- **Red flag triggers:** Specific reference responses that should pause or kill the process.
- **Reference synthesis template:** Format for summarizing reference findings for decision-makers.

### Block 5 — Hiring Decision Record

Captures the hire/no-hire decision with full rationale.

**Fields:**
- **Candidate name:** (Confidential — restricted distribution).
- **Scorecard evaluation:** Rating per competency with evidence.
- **Strengths summary:** Top 3 reasons to hire.
- **Risk summary:** Top 3 concerns and mitigation plan for each.
- **Reference summary:** Synthesis of reference feedback.
- **Compensation terms:** Proposed offer details.
- **Decision:** Hire / Pass / Hold.
- **Decision rationale:** 2-3 sentences explaining the decision.
- **Dissenting views:** Any evaluators who disagreed and their reasoning.
- **90-day success criteria:** What success looks like at the first review.

### Block 6 — Onboarding Accountability Plan

Ensures the new hire is set up for success with board-level visibility.

**Fields:**
- **30-day objectives:** Learning, relationship-building, quick wins.
- **60-day objectives:** First substantive deliverables and team assessment.
- **90-day objectives:** Measurable outcomes tied to the scorecard.
- **Onboarding buddy/mentor:** Assigned support person.
- **Check-in cadence:** Scheduled 1:1s with manager, skip-level, and board advisor.
- **Early warning signals:** Behaviors or results that indicate a potential mis-hire.
- **Course correction protocol:** What happens if 30 or 60-day objectives are missed.
- **Board update schedule:** When the board receives updates on the new hire's progress.

## Usage

1. **Block 1** is non-negotiable for every board-level hire. A role without a scorecard produces interviews that measure charisma, not capability.
2. **Block 2** is created before outreach begins. Sourcing after the first candidate appears creates anchoring bias.
3. **Block 3** prevents interview overlap and ensures full scorecard coverage. Map it before scheduling.
4. **Block 4** is the most under-utilized block. Backchannel references on executives are where the real signal lives.
5. **Block 5** creates an auditable record. When a hire fails, the decision record reveals what was missed.
6. **Block 6** transitions from hiring to performance. A great hire with poor onboarding produces the same result as a bad hire.

## Example

```markdown
# Executive Hire: VP Engineering

## Role Scorecard
- **Mission:** Build and lead the engineering organization from 12 to 40 engineers while shipping the enterprise product roadmap on schedule.
- **12-month outcomes:**
  1. Ship SOC2 compliance and SSO by Q2
  2. Reduce deployment cycle from 2 weeks to 2 days
  3. Hire 15 engineers with < 10% regrettable attrition
  4. Establish architecture review process
- **Must-have competencies:** Scaled eng teams 10-50+, enterprise security experience, strong technical architecture, hiring track record
- **Anti-patterns:** Prefers process over shipping, empire-builder, avoids hard conversations

## Hiring Decision
- **Candidate:** [Redacted]
- **Decision:** Hire
- **Rationale:** Strong match on 4/4 must-have competencies. Scaled eng at two previous startups through similar growth phase. Reference from former CTO called them "the best engineering leader I've worked with." Risk: limited healthcare domain experience — mitigated by strong product partnership.
- **Dissent:** CTO preferred Candidate B for deeper technical depth. Acknowledged Candidate A's leadership edge.
```
