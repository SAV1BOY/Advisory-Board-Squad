# Board Best Practices

## Board Composition
- **Ideal size:** 5-7 members for early-stage, 7-9 for growth, 9-11 for public companies
- **Independence:** At least 50% independent directors (no financial relationship with company beyond board fees)
- **Skill matrix:** Map required competencies (finance, operations, industry, technology, legal, HR) and fill gaps deliberately
- **Diversity:** Gender, ethnicity, age, background, thinking style. Homogeneous boards produce homogeneous (and often blind) decisions
- **Term limits:** 3-year terms with staggered renewal. Prevents both entrenchment and total turnover
- **Refreshment:** Annual assessment of whether each member's contribution justifies their seat

## Meeting Cadence and Structure
- **Frequency:** Monthly for early-stage, quarterly for growth, quarterly + special sessions for public
- **Duration:** 2-4 hours, no more. If meetings routinely exceed 4 hours, the preparation is insufficient
- **Materials:** Distributed 72 hours in advance. No reading aloud what's in the deck
- **Executive sessions:** 15-30 minutes without management at every meeting. This is where real board conversation happens
- **Consent agenda:** Routine approvals bundled for efficiency, freeing time for strategic discussion

## Decision-Making
- **Clear authority matrix:** Document which decisions require board approval, which require board notification, and which are delegated to management
- **Pre-mortems:** For major decisions, conduct a structured exercise imagining the decision has failed
- **Dissent protocol:** Formally invite dissenting views. If a decision is unanimous and took less than 10 minutes, something is wrong
- **Vote recording:** Record votes, including dissents, in minutes. This creates accountability and protects dissenting members

## Information Flow
- **Dashboard:** Monthly KPI dashboard with leading and lagging indicators, actuals vs. plan, and commentary on variances
- **Bad news first:** CEOs should report problems before wins. Boards that punish bad news get surprised by crises
- **Board portal:** Secure digital platform for document sharing, communication, and voting between meetings
- **Direct access:** Board members should have direct access to key executives (CFO, GC) with CEO's knowledge and consent

## Board Culture
- **Trust is the foundation** — Apply Lencioni's Five Dysfunctions model to the board itself
- **Constructive challenge** — The board's job is to challenge management, not to rubber-stamp
- **No surprises** — Neither from management to the board nor from board members to management
- **Preparation is expected** — Members who consistently arrive unprepared should be counseled and, if necessary, removed
- **Confidentiality is absolute** — Leaks destroy trust. One leak should trigger immediate investigation

## Annual Board Calendar
- Q1: Annual strategy review, CEO performance evaluation, compensation review
- Q2: Budget mid-year review, risk assessment, board self-evaluation
- Q3: Succession planning review, governance policy update
- Q4: Annual planning, next-year budget approval, board composition review
