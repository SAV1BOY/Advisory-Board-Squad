# Naval Wealth Creation Principles

## Purpose

Naval Ravikant's wealth creation principles provide a comprehensive framework for building long-term wealth through ownership, leverage, and specific knowledge rather than through trading time for money. The core insight: you will not get rich by renting out your time. You must own equity — a piece of a business — and apply leverage to your specific knowledge. This framework translates Naval's principles into actionable guidance for founders and advisors.

## When to Use

- When advising founders on business model design and personal wealth building
- When evaluating compensation structures (salary vs. equity)
- When deciding between employment, consulting, and entrepreneurship
- When designing organizational compensation philosophy
- When coaching on career strategy at inflection points

## Core Model

### Naval's Wealth Principles

**1. Seek wealth, not money or status.** Wealth is assets that earn while you sleep. Money is how we transfer time and wealth. Status is your place in social hierarchy.

**2. You will not get rich renting out your time.** Even at $1,000/hour, time-based income is linear. Wealth requires non-linear returns through ownership.

**3. You must own equity to gain financial freedom.** Equity in a business, real estate, or intellectual property. The key is ownership with upside.

**4. Give society what it wants but does not yet know how to get — at scale.** Find needs, build solutions, deploy leverage.

**5. The Internet has massively broadened career possibilities.** Geographic and gatekeeping constraints have collapsed. Anyone can reach the entire world.

**6. Play long-term games with long-term people.** Returns in business compound through trust and reputation over many years. Short-term thinking destroys compound value.

**7. Pick business partners with high intelligence, energy, and integrity.** Intelligence and energy without integrity produce the most dangerous partners.

**8. Learn to sell and learn to build.** If you can do both, you are unstoppable. Most people can only do one.

**9. Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage.** This is the wealth creation formula: Specific Knowledge + Accountability + Leverage = Wealth.

**10. There are no get-rich-quick schemes.** If it sounds too good to be true, it is. Wealth creation is a long game.

## Steps

1. **Identify your specific knowledge** (see Naval Specific Knowledge Framework)
2. **Take accountability** — put your name on your work, accept risk, build reputation
3. **Apply leverage** — code, media, capital, labor (see Naval Leverage Stack)
4. **Own equity** — negotiate for ownership stakes, build businesses, invest
5. **Play long-term games** — build reputation and relationships that compound
6. **Learn to sell AND build** — develop both creation and distribution skills

## Application Guidelines

### For Board Advisors
- Help founders understand that their equity is their primary wealth-building mechanism. Salary is for living expenses; equity is for wealth.
- Evaluate compensation structures through the lens of wealth creation: does the structure incentivize long-term ownership thinking or short-term extraction?
- Advise on equity distribution that aligns all stakeholders for long-term wealth creation.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Optimizing for salary over equity.** Salary is capped; equity is not. In high-potential companies, equity dwarfs salary.
2. **Playing short-term games.** Maximizing this quarter at the expense of next decade.
3. **Choosing partners for convenience, not integrity.** Intelligence and energy without integrity is destructive.
4. **Not learning to sell.** Technical founders who can't sell limit their companies.
