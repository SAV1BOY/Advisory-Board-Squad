# Sinek Golden Circle

## Purpose

Simon Sinek's Golden Circle framework reveals that the most inspiring organizations and leaders communicate from the inside out — starting with WHY (purpose), then HOW (process), then WHAT (product) — rather than the conventional outside-in approach. This framework helps organizations articulate their purpose, align their strategy and messaging around it, and inspire action from employees, customers, and investors who share their beliefs.

## When to Use

- When defining or refining organizational purpose and mission
- When crafting messaging for investors, customers, or recruits
- When a company knows what it does but cannot articulate why it matters
- When marketing feels transactional rather than inspirational
- When hiring and the company needs to attract mission-aligned talent
- During board strategy sessions to ensure strategic clarity

## Core Model

### The Three Circles (Inside Out)

**WHY (Core):** The organization's purpose, cause, or belief. Not to make money — that's a result. WHY is the reason you exist beyond profit. It rarely changes.

**HOW (Middle):** The specific actions, values, or principles that bring the WHY to life. These are differentiating value propositions and processes.

**WHAT (Outer):** The tangible products, services, or outputs. Every organization knows WHAT it does. Few know WHY.

### The Biology of Why
The Golden Circle maps to brain structure:
- WHAT corresponds to the neocortex (rational, analytical thought, language)
- WHY and HOW correspond to the limbic brain (feelings, trust, loyalty, decision-making — no language center)

This is why features and specs (WHAT) inform but don't inspire. Purpose and belief (WHY) drive behavior because they speak to the part of the brain that controls decisions.

### The Law of Diffusion of Innovation
- Innovators (2.5%) and Early Adopters (13.5%) buy WHY, not WHAT
- They take the risk because they believe what you believe
- The Early Majority follows only after seeing early adopters succeed
- To cross the chasm, you must start with believers

## Steps

1. **Articulate your WHY.** In one sentence: "We exist because we believe [belief]." Not what you do or how you do it. Why you do it.
2. **Define your HOW.** What specific principles or processes bring your WHY to life? These should be 3-5 actionable statements.
3. **Align your WHAT.** Do your products and services actually deliver on the WHY and HOW? If not, you have an alignment problem.
4. **Communicate inside-out.** Lead every pitch, presentation, and conversation with WHY, then HOW, then WHAT.
5. **Hire for WHY alignment.** Skills can be trained. Belief alignment cannot.
6. **Test for authenticity.** If your WHY feels forced or aspirational rather than genuinely held, it is not your WHY.

## Application Guidelines

### For Board Advisors
- Every portfolio company should be able to articulate its WHY in one sentence. If it can't, it will struggle to attract believers (employees, customers, investors).
- Use the Golden Circle to evaluate pitch quality. Founders who lead with WHAT are forgettable. Founders who lead with WHY are compelling.
- Help founders distinguish between genuine WHY and marketing WHY. The test: would the founder do this even if it didn't make money?

## Common Mistakes

1. **WHAT masquerading as WHY.** "We exist to build the best CRM" is a WHAT. "We believe every relationship deserves attention" is a WHY.
2. **Aspirational WHY.** A WHY you wish you had but don't genuinely believe. People detect inauthenticity.
3. **Changing WHY based on market conditions.** WHAT and HOW can change. WHY should not.
4. **Skipping HOW.** WHY without HOW is a poster. HOW translates belief into action.

## Output Format

```markdown
# Golden Circle: [Organization]

## WHY: We exist because we believe...
[One sentence]

## HOW: We bring this to life by...
1. [Principle/Action]
2. [Principle/Action]
3. [Principle/Action]

## WHAT: This results in...
[Products, services, outputs]

## Alignment Check
- [ ] WHY is genuinely held, not aspirational
- [ ] HOW clearly connects to WHY
- [ ] WHAT delivers on HOW
- [ ] External communication leads with WHY
```
