# Risk Cyber and Fraud Gate Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the organization has appropriate controls against cyber threats and fraud. These risks can be existential: a data breach or fraud event can destroy customer trust, trigger regulatory action, and consume management attention for months. This checklist establishes minimum standards.

## When to Use
- Quarterly during security and risk reviews.
- Before launching new products or services that handle sensitive data.
- When expanding to new markets with different regulatory requirements.
- After any security incident or near-miss.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A security policy exists, is current, and is communicated to all employees.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Access controls follow the principle of least privilege.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Multi-factor authentication is required for all systems containing sensitive data.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Data encryption is implemented at rest and in transit for sensitive data.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Regular security assessments or penetration tests are conducted (at least annually).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Incident response procedures are documented and tested.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Employee security awareness training is conducted at least annually.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Fraud prevention controls exist for financial transactions (dual approval, reconciliation).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Third-party vendor security is assessed before engagement and periodically thereafter.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Backup and recovery procedures are tested at least annually.

## Red Flags
- No security assessment has been conducted in over 18 months.
- Admin access is shared or not properly managed.
- Security incidents are handled ad hoc with no documented procedure.
- The organization handles sensitive data without encryption.
- No employee has been designated as responsible for security.

## Escalation Triggers
- A security breach exposes customer or company data.
- A fraud attempt succeeds or comes close to succeeding.
- A regulatory inquiry into security practices is opened.
- A security assessment reveals critical vulnerabilities.
- A vendor security failure affects the organization.
