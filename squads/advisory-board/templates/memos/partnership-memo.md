## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Enthusiasm bias:** Partnerships are exciting. Counter this by asking: "What would a skeptical board member say about this?"
2. **Ignoring exit costs:** Every partnership should be entered with a clear understanding of how to exit gracefully. If exit is impossible, you are not entering a partnership — you are entering a dependency.
3. **Underestimating internal costs:** Partnerships consume management attention, engineering resources, and support bandwidth. Model the fully loaded internal cost.
4. **Asymmetric dependency:** If the partnership matters much more to you than to them, your negotiating position will erode over time. Monitor the dependency balance.
5. **Handshake governance:** Partnerships without formal governance (joint steering, escalation paths, review cadence) drift and deteriorate. Formalize from day one.
