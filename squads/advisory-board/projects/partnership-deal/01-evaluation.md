# Partnership Deal: Evaluation

## Objective
Conduct structured due diligence on the potential partner across financial, technical, reputational, and cultural dimensions.

## Inputs
- Partnership brief (00-brief.md). Public information. Partner-provided materials.

## Process
1. Assess financial health: partner stability and longevity.
2. Evaluate technical capability: can they deliver what they promise?
3. Check reputation: references from other partners, customer reviews, press coverage.
4. Assess key-person risk: is the partnership dependent on a specific champion?
5. Review competitive conflicts: do they work with our competitors?
6. Evaluate governance track record: how have their previous partnerships been managed?
7. Cultural compatibility assessment using culture-blocks.md Block 6.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/partnership-blocks.md` (Block 2: Due Diligence)
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 6

## Output
Due diligence report with risk assessment and Go/No-Go recommendation.
