# Strategic Reset: Review

## Objective
Conduct structured reviews of strategic progress at defined intervals, making evidence-based decisions about continuing, adjusting, or abandoning the strategy. Reviews prevent drift and ensure the strategy remains relevant as conditions change.

## Inputs
- Strategic metrics dashboard (04-metrics.md).
- Initiative progress reports from initiative owners.
- Market and competitive intelligence updates.
- Customer feedback and engagement data.
- Team health and capacity data.

## Process
1. Prepare the review package: current metrics vs. targets, initiative milestone status, assumption validation results, risk register updates, market changes.
2. Conduct the strategic review meeting (quarterly, using meeting-blocks.md):
   - Review metrics against targets (15 min)
   - Assess each initiative's progress and health (30 min)
   - Evaluate whether critical assumptions still hold (15 min)
   - Check pivot triggers: have any been hit? (10 min)
   - Decide: continue as planned, adjust, or escalate for potential pivot (15 min)
3. Update the strategy document with any adjustments and the rationale.
4. Communicate changes to the broader organization.
5. Capture lessons learned from the review period.
6. Schedule the next review.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/meeting-blocks.md` (Block 3: Agenda, Block 4: Decision Capture)
- `scripts/analysis/quarterly-review-builder.md` (automated review package assembly)
- `lib/patterns/pivot-pattern.md` (if pivot triggers are hit)
- `lib/patterns/kill-decision-pattern.md` (if initiative kill criteria are met)
- `data/registries/decision-registry.yaml` (record any strategic adjustment decisions)

## Output
- Quarterly strategic review record with decisions, adjustments, and lessons.
- Updated strategy document (if adjustments were made).
- Communication to stakeholders about strategic progress and any changes.
- Next review date confirmed with pre-work assignments.

## Next Phase
The strategic reset cycle is continuous. Each review either confirms the strategy, triggers an adjustment, or escalates to a new reset. The cycle continues until the strategy achieves its objectives or a new reset is triggered.
