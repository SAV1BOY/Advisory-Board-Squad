# Task: Product Strategy Review

## Objective
Review and validate the product strategy at board level. Ensure product direction aligns with company strategy, addresses customer needs, and builds competitive moat.

## Trigger
- Quarterly as part of the regular board cycle
- When product-market fit signals weaken (retention drops, NPS declines, churn increases)
- Before a major product direction change or significant investment
- When a competitor launches a product that threatens our positioning

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** VP Product / CPO
- **Support:** CTO (technical feasibility), VP Sales (market feedback), CEO (strategic alignment)
- **Advisory:** Board members with product or technology expertise

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Product metrics current (adoption, retention, NPS, engagement)
- [ ] Customer feedback synthesized (support tickets, interviews, surveys)
- [ ] Win/loss analysis current
- [ ] Competitive product analysis updated
- [ ] Engineering capacity and velocity data available

## Steps

1. **Product Health Assessment (Days 1-5)**
   - Pull all product-market fit metrics: retention curves, NPS, must-have score, time-to-value
   - Analyze product usage data: feature adoption, engagement depth, power user behavior
   - Review customer feedback themes from support, sales, and direct research
   - Assess engineering velocity and tech debt trajectory

2. **Market and Competitive Product Analysis (Days 5-8)**
   - Review competitor product moves since last assessment
   - Update feature parity matrix
   - Identify emerging product categories or approaches that could disrupt our positioning
   - Assess customer expectations trajectory: what will be table stakes in 12-18 months?

3. **Strategic Alignment Check (Days 8-10)**
   - Map current product roadmap against board-approved strategic priorities
   - Identify misalignments: where is product investment going vs. where strategy says it should go
   - Assess whether the product thesis is still valid given market evolution
   - Evaluate build vs. buy vs. partner decisions for key capabilities

4. **Roadmap Review and Options Development (Days 10-15)**
   - Review proposed roadmap for next 2-4 quarters
   - Develop options if strategic direction change is recommended
   - Estimate engineering investment and timeline for each option
   - Model unit economics impact of product changes

5. **Board Presentation (Day 15-20)**
   - Prepare product strategy brief using product-strategy-brief template
   - Present at board meeting with clear asks: validation, direction change approval, or resource request
   - Capture board feedback and decisions

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Product-Market Fit Assessment:** Sean Ellis test (must-have score), retention analysis, growth accounting
- **RICE Prioritization:** Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort scoring for roadmap items
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done:** Validate that the product still solves the core job better than alternatives
- **Kano Model:** Classify features as basic, performance, or delight to guide prioritization

## Checklists

### Review Quality Gate
- [ ] Product metrics include both quantitative data and qualitative customer voice
- [ ] Competitive analysis is evidence-based (product teardowns, not assumptions)
- [ ] Roadmap ties back to strategic priorities with clear rationale
- [ ] Unit economics impact of product changes is modeled
- [ ] Technical feasibility confirmed by engineering leadership
- [ ] Build vs. buy vs. partner analysis included for major investments

## Output
- Product Strategy Brief for the board
- Updated product roadmap (if changes approved)
- Product-market fit dashboard update
- Feature parity matrix update

## Handoffs
- Approved roadmap changes cascade to engineering sprints
- Capital allocation requests feed into capital-allocation-review.md
- Competitive product insights update competitive-landscape-report

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Product-market fit score (must-have %) | >40% | Survey |
| NPS trend | Improving or stable above 40 | Quarterly survey |
| Roadmap-strategy alignment | >80% of engineering effort on strategic priorities | Allocation audit |
| Roadmap predictability | >70% of planned features shipped on time | Delivery tracking |
