### Rating Scale Reference
- **5 — Exceptional:** Top 5% of executives at this level. Role model for the competency.
- **4 — Exceeds:** Consistently demonstrates at a high level. Reliable strength.
- **3 — Meets:** Performs at the expected level for their role. Competent and consistent.
- **2 — Development Needed:** Below expectations. Gap identified with clear development path.
- **1 — Significant Gap:** Well below expectations. Requires immediate intervention.
