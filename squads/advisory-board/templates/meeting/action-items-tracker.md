## Escalation Protocol

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Item is 7+ days overdue | Board Ops sends reminder to owner with cc to Chair |
| Item is 14+ days overdue | Chair contacts owner directly |
| Item is 30+ days overdue | Item escalated to full board at next meeting |
| Item is blocked | Owner must notify Board Ops within 48 hours of identifying blocker; Board Ops escalates to Chair |
| Item scope has changed | Owner submits revised scope and timeline for Chair approval |

---

## ID Convention

Action Item IDs follow the format: `AI-[YEAR]-[SEQUENTIAL NUMBER]`
- Example: AI-2026-001, AI-2026-002
- IDs are never reused, even after completion
- IDs reset annually on January 1
