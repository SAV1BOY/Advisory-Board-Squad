# Partnership Deal: Brief

## Objective
Define the strategic rationale and evaluation criteria for a potential partnership before any negotiation begins.

## Inputs
- Strategic plan identifying capability or market gaps. Partner identification. Preliminary research on the potential partner.

## Process
1. Articulate the strategic rationale: what gap does this partnership fill?
2. Evaluate alternatives: could we build, acquire, or find a different partner?
3. Define the value creation thesis for both sides.
4. Assess cultural compatibility at a high level.
5. Identify dependency and opportunity cost risks.
6. Score the opportunity: Go / Explore Further / Pass.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/partnership-blocks.md` (Block 1: Opportunity Assessment)

## Output
Partnership brief with strategic rationale, value thesis, and preliminary assessment.
