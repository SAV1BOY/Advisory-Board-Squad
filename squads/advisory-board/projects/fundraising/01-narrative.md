# Fundraising: Narrative

## Objective
Craft the compelling, honest story that connects the company's mission, traction, market opportunity, and capital needs into a coherent narrative that resonates with target investors.

## Inputs
- Fundraising brief (00-brief.md).
- Company metrics, milestones, and competitive position.
- Market research and TAM analysis.
- Team bios and track record.
- Product roadmap and strategic plan.

## Process
1. Define the narrative arc: Problem (market pain), Solution (your product), Traction (evidence it works), Opportunity (market size), Team (why you will win), Ask (what you need).
2. Build the pitch deck (12-15 slides maximum).
3. Prepare the data room: financials, cap table, legal documents, customer references.
4. Develop the 30-second, 2-minute, and 15-minute versions of the pitch.
5. Rehearse with board advisors and trusted contacts. Incorporate feedback.
6. Prepare FAQ document: anticipated tough questions with honest, concise answers.

## Tools/Frameworks

- `lib/components/capital-blocks.md` (Block 5: Unit Economics)

## Output
- Pitch deck, executive summary, and data room.
- Rehearsed pitch at three time lengths.
- FAQ and objection-handling document.
