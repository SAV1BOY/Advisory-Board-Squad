# Strategy Constraints Map Checklist

## Purpose
Identifies and maps the binding constraints that limit the organization's strategic execution. Every organization operates under constraints: capital, talent, time, technology, regulatory, or market. Strategy that ignores constraints is fantasy. This checklist ensures constraints are identified, prioritized, and addressed rather than assumed away.

## When to Use
- During strategic planning to assess feasibility.
- When strategic initiatives are stalling without clear diagnosis.
- When evaluating whether additional resources will actually remove bottlenecks.
- Quarterly to update the constraint landscape.

## Gate Criteria

### Constraint Identification
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: All material constraints are identified across categories: capital, talent, technology, regulatory, market, organizational.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each constraint is assessed for severity: how much does it limit execution.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The binding constraint (the single most limiting factor) is identified.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Constraints are distinguished from inconveniences.

### Constraint Analysis
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The cost of relaxing each constraint is estimated.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Constraints that are temporary versus structural are distinguished.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Interactions between constraints are mapped (solving one may reveal another).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team has asked: what constraint, if removed, would unlock the most strategic value.

### Constraint Management
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A plan exists to address the binding constraint with resources, timeline, and owner.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Strategy is designed to work within constraints, not assume they will be eliminated.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Constraints that cannot be removed are worked around through creative design.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The constraint landscape is monitored for changes.

### Organizational Awareness
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Leaders understand the current binding constraint and its impact.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Teams do not launch initiatives that are blocked by known constraints.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Resource allocation prioritizes constraint removal for the highest-value constraints.

## Red Flags
- The strategic plan assumes no constraints on talent acquisition or capital availability.
- The binding constraint has been the same for 12+ months with no progress.
- New initiatives are launched that require resources the organization does not have.
- Leadership disagrees about what the binding constraint is.

## Escalation Triggers
- A constraint causes a strategic initiative to fail that should have been anticipated.
- The organization repeatedly hits the same constraint without investing in removal.
- A competitor removes a shared constraint and gains a decisive advantage.
