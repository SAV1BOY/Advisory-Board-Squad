## Phase 1: Learn (Days 1-30)

### Objective
Absorb context. Build relationships. Understand what exists before proposing changes.

### Week 1: Immersion

| Day | Activity | Owner | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Welcome and logistics (IT setup, access, accounts) | People Ops | 2 hours |
| Day 1 | CEO welcome meeting — company vision, expectations, support offered | CEO | 60 min |
| Day 1 | Hiring manager 1:1 — role expectations, 90-day plan review, first priorities | Hiring Manager | 90 min |
| Day 2 | Company strategy deep dive — review strategic plan, board materials, decision log | Self (guided) | Half day |
| Day 2 | Finance overview — P&L, runway, unit economics, budget for their function | CFO | 60 min |
| Day 3 | Product and technology overview — product demo, architecture, roadmap | CTO / VP Product | 90 min |
| Day 3 | Customer overview — segments, personas, top accounts, NPS, churn data | VP Sales / CS | 60 min |
| Day 4 | Their team: 1:1 with each direct report (30 min each) | New Exec | Full day |
| Day 5 | Peer executive introductions (30 min each with every C-suite / VP) | New Exec | Full day |

### Weeks 2-4: Listening Tour

| Activity | Participants | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct report 1:1s | Each direct report | Weekly | Understand team, priorities, blockers |
| Skip-level meetings | 2-3 per week | Over 3 weeks | Understand ground truth beyond direct reports |
| Customer calls / visits | 3-5 customers | Over 3 weeks | Hear directly from customers |
| Cross-functional partner meetings | Key stakeholders in other functions | 1-2 per week | Understand interdependencies |
| Board member introductions | Each board member (15-30 min) | Over 2 weeks | Build board relationships |
| Competitor and market review | Strategy team / Self | Self-paced | Understand competitive landscape |

### Listening Tour Questions
Use these consistently across all meetings:
1. "What is working well that I should not break?"
2. "What is the biggest problem in [function/company] that everyone knows about but nobody talks about?"
3. "If you were in my role, what would you do in the first 90 days?"
4. "What do you need from my function that you are not getting today?"
5. "What should I know about the culture here that nobody will tell me unprompted?"

### Day 30 Checkpoint
- [ ] Listening tour completed (all direct reports, skip-levels, cross-functional, customers)
- [ ] Board member introductions completed
- [ ] Documented initial observations and hypotheses (for hiring manager review)
- [ ] Identified top 3 urgent issues and top 3 strategic opportunities
- [ ] 30-day check-in with hiring manager (formal)
- [ ] 30-day check-in with onboarding buddy (informal)

## Phase 2: Diagnose (Days 31-60)

### Objective
Synthesize learning into an actionable diagnosis. Begin building credibility through quick wins. Refine the 90-day plan based on reality.

### Key Activities

| Activity | Description | Deliverable | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| State of the Function assessment | Written assessment of current state, strengths, gaps, and opportunities | 2-3 page document | Day 45 |
| Quick wins execution | Identify and deliver 2-3 visible, low-risk improvements | Completed improvements | Days 35-55 |
| Team assessment | Evaluate each direct report: right role, right skills, right level? | Confidential assessment for hiring manager | Day 50 |
| Stakeholder feedback collection | Gather informal feedback on new exec's first month | Hiring manager collects | Day 45 |
| Revised 90-day goals | Update day 60-90 plan based on diagnosis | Updated plan | Day 50 |

### Day 45: State of the Function Presentation
Present to hiring manager (and optionally to peer executives):
1. What I have learned — key observations from the listening tour
2. What is working — strengths to preserve and build on
3. What needs attention — gaps, risks, and opportunities
4. Proposed priorities for days 60-90 and beyond
5. Resource needs — what I need to execute (people, budget, tools)

### Day 60 Checkpoint
- [ ] State of the Function assessment delivered and discussed
- [ ] At least 2 quick wins delivered
- [ ] Team assessment shared confidentially with hiring manager
- [ ] Cross-functional relationships established with all peer executives
- [ ] No surprises — hiring manager is informed of all significant findings
- [ ] 60-day formal check-in with hiring manager
- [ ] 60-day check-in with board sponsor

---

## Phase 3: Act (Days 61-90)

### Objective
Begin executing on the diagnosis. Set the function's direction for the next 6-12 months. Establish operating cadence.

### Key Activities

| Activity | Description | Deliverable | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional strategy draft | 6-12 month vision and plan for the function | Strategy document | Day 80 |
| Organizational changes (if needed) | Propose and begin any structural changes to the team | Org change plan (approved by hiring manager) | Day 75 |
| Operating cadence establishment | Set up recurring meetings, reporting, and rhythms | Documented operating model | Day 75 |
| Key hire requisitions (if needed) | Submit and begin recruiting for critical gaps | Approved req(s) | Day 70 |
| First board interaction | Present or contribute substantively at board meeting | Board contribution | Per schedule |
| 90-day retrospective | Written self-assessment and forward plan | 90-day report | Day 90 |

### Day 90 Review

**Formal 90-day review with hiring manager covering:**
1. Achievement against original 90-day outcomes (see Success Definition above)
2. State of the function — assessment and plan
3. Team assessment — who is strong, who needs development, any changes needed
4. Relationships — quality of relationships with peers, team, customers, board
5. Cultural integration — values fit, working style alignment
6. Self-assessment — what went well, what they would do differently
7. Forward plan — priorities for the next quarter
8. Support needed — from hiring manager, CEO, board

**Hiring manager assessment:**
- [ ] Business results on track
- [ ] Cultural fit confirmed
- [ ] Peer relationships healthy
- [ ] Team management effective
- [ ] Board interaction appropriate
- [ ] Proceed with full confidence / Watch list / Concerns identified
