# Board Scorecard Framework

## Purpose

This framework provides a structured approach to measuring and improving board performance. Most boards evaluate the companies they oversee but never evaluate themselves. The Board Scorecard creates accountability for board members, ensures the board is delivering value, and identifies areas for improvement. A board that doesn't measure its own effectiveness cannot claim to govern effectively.

## When to Use

- During annual board effectiveness reviews
- When board meetings feel unproductive
- When the CEO/founder is dissatisfied with board contribution
- When onboarding new board members (to set expectations)
- When restructuring or refreshing board composition

## Core Model

### Four Scorecard Dimensions

**1. Strategic Contribution:** Does the board add strategic value beyond governance?
- Quality of strategic input in meetings
- Relevance of industry knowledge and connections
- Proactive identification of opportunities and threats

**2. Governance Effectiveness:** Does the board fulfill its fiduciary duties?
- Attendance and preparation
- Quality of oversight (financial, legal, ethical)
- Decision-making rigor and documentation

**3. Relationship Quality:** Does the board-management relationship work?
- Trust level between board and CEO
- Appropriateness of involvement (neither overreach nor abdication)
- Quality of feedback and communication

**4. Composition and Development:** Is the board the right team?
- Skills coverage across critical domains
- Diversity of perspective and background
- Continuous development and learning

## Steps

1. **Design the scorecard** with 3-5 metrics per dimension.
2. **Collect input** from all board members AND from the CEO/management team.
3. **Score each dimension** using a consistent scale (1-5).
4. **Identify the lowest-scoring dimension** as the priority improvement area.
5. **Create an improvement plan** with specific actions, owners, and timelines.
6. **Review annually** and track progress year over year.
