# Decision Memo — 1-Pager

## Template Guidelines
**Word Limit:** 750 words maximum (excluding headers and tables)
**Reading Time:** Under 5 minutes
**Use When:** A single, clear decision is needed with well-understood options and bounded scope.

---

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Author:** [Name, Title]
**Decision Owner:** [Name — the person accountable for making and executing this decision]
**Approver:** [Name or body — Board / CEO / Committee]
**Status:** Draft / Under Review / Approved / Rejected

---

### Situation (100-150 words)
State the current situation in plain language. What is happening, what changed, and why does this require a decision now? Include only facts — no opinions or recommendations in this section.

**Good Example:** "Our largest customer (18% of ARR) has informed us they will not renew their annual contract in 90 days unless we deliver SSO integration. Our current roadmap has SSO scheduled for Q4, which is 60 days past their renewal deadline."

**Bad Example:** "We need to build SSO because customers want it and it is important for our growth." (Too vague, no urgency framing, no specifics.)

---

### Options (150-200 words)

| Option | Description | Cost | Timeline | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: [Name]** | [2-sentence description] | $[amount] + [effort] | [weeks/months] | [Primary risk] |
| **B: [Name]** | [2-sentence description] | $[amount] + [effort] | [weeks/months] | [Primary risk] |
| **C: Do Nothing** | [2-sentence description of status quo consequences] | $[cost of inaction] | N/A | [Primary risk] |

**Good Example Option:** "A: Accelerate SSO — Pull SSO forward by 8 weeks, deferring the analytics dashboard. Cost: 3 eng-months + $20K for IdP testing infrastructure. Timeline: 10 weeks. Risk: Analytics dashboard delay may affect 3 mid-market prospects in pipeline."

**Bad Example Option:** "A: Build SSO — We should build SSO because it is needed." (No specifics, no trade-offs, no cost.)

---

### Recommendation (100-150 words)
State your recommended option and the reasoning. The reasoning must address:
1. Why this option best serves the company's strategic priorities.
2. What trade-offs you are accepting and why they are acceptable.
3. What would change your recommendation (reversal conditions).

## Required Sections Checklist
- [ ] Situation clearly states what changed and why a decision is needed now
- [ ] At least 3 options presented (including "do nothing")
- [ ] Each option includes cost, timeline, and primary risk
- [ ] Recommendation includes reversal conditions
- [ ] Key data is sourced and dated
- [ ] Word count is under 750 words
- [ ] No jargon — a new board member could understand this in one read

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Burying the lead:** The decision needed should be obvious from the title and first paragraph.
2. **False options:** Presenting a strawman "do nothing" that no rational person would choose, making it feel like only one real option exists.
3. **Missing trade-offs:** Every option has downsides. If yours does not list any, you have not thought hard enough.
4. **Stale data:** Data older than 90 days should be flagged or refreshed.
5. **Scope creep:** A 1-pager that needs 2 pages is actually a strategy memo — use the 2-pager template instead.
6. **No reversal conditions:** Every recommendation should state "We would change course if [X]."
