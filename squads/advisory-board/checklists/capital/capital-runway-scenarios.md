# Capital Runway Scenarios Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the organization maintains a clear, honest understanding of its capital runway under multiple scenarios. Runway is not a single number; it is a range that depends on assumptions about revenue, spending, and external factors. This checklist prevents the common failure of having a single optimistic runway estimate that creates false confidence.

## When to Use
- Monthly as part of financial review.
- When significant changes in revenue or spending occur.
- Before fundraising decisions.
- When evaluating new spending commitments.
- During board financial reviews.

## Gate Criteria

### Scenario Modeling
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: At least three runway scenarios are modeled: base, optimistic, and pessimistic.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The pessimistic scenario is genuinely conservative, not base minus a small percentage.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each scenario specifies the assumptions that distinguish it from the others.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Revenue assumptions are stress-tested against customer pipeline reality.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Expense assumptions account for committed costs that cannot be easily cut.

### Cash Management
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Cash position is tracked weekly with a forward 13-week cash forecast.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The minimum acceptable cash balance is defined and monitored.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Cash burn rate is calculated on a trailing 3-month basis, not just monthly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Large one-time expenses are anticipated and planned for.

### Trigger Points
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Specific runway levels trigger predefined actions (hiring freeze, cost reduction, fundraising).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The trigger point for initiating fundraising accounts for realistic fundraising timelines.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Emergency cost-reduction plans are prepared in advance, not improvised during crisis.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The board is informed immediately when runway crosses a predefined threshold.

### Accountability
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A named executive is accountable for cash management and runway monitoring.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Runway projections are shared with the board at least quarterly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Variances between projected and actual runway are analyzed and explained.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Past runway projections are compared to actuals to improve forecast quality.

## Red Flags
- Only one runway scenario exists and it assumes plan-level performance.
- The cash forecast has not been updated in more than 30 days.
- The organization does not track committed versus discretionary spending.
- Fundraising is initiated with less than six months of runway remaining.
- No emergency cost-reduction plan has been prepared.

## Escalation Triggers
- Runway under the pessimistic scenario drops below 9 months.
- Actual cash burn exceeds the base scenario by more than 20% for two consecutive months.
- Revenue comes in below the pessimistic scenario for two consecutive months.
- A material unexpected expense reduces runway by more than one month.
- The board has not been updated on runway in more than 60 days.
