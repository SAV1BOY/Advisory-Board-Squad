## Risk Scoring Guide

### Likelihood Scale
| Score | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Almost Certain | >90% probability within 12 months |
| 4 | Likely | 60-90% probability within 12 months |
| 3 | Possible | 30-60% probability within 12 months |
| 2 | Unlikely | 10-30% probability within 12 months |
| 1 | Rare | <10% probability within 12 months |

### Impact Scale
| Score | Label | Financial | Operational | Reputational |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Critical | >$[X]M or existential | Complete shutdown >1 week | National media, regulatory action |
| 4 | High | $[X-Y] range | Major disruption 1-7 days | Industry media, customer escalations |
| 3 | Medium | $[X-Y] range | Moderate disruption <24 hours | Social media attention, some customer impact |
| 2 | Low | $[X-Y] range | Minor disruption <4 hours | Limited external awareness |
| 1 | Negligible | <$[X] | No operational impact | No external awareness |

### Risk Score Interpretation
| Score Range | Level | Board Reporting | Review Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20-25 | Critical | Every board meeting + immediate notification | Weekly |
| 12-19 | High | Every board meeting | Bi-weekly |
| 6-11 | Medium | Quarterly board report | Monthly |
| 1-5 | Low | Annual summary | Quarterly |

## Risk Appetite Statement

The board has established the following risk appetite thresholds:

| Risk Category | Appetite | Threshold for Board Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Financial | Moderate | Any single risk event >$[X] potential impact |
| Strategic | Moderate-High | Any risk to core business model or primary market |
| Operational | Low | Any risk of >24 hour service disruption |
| People | Low | Attrition >20% in any critical function |
| Legal/Compliance | Very Low | Any regulatory investigation or material lawsuit |
| Cybersecurity | Very Low | Any data breach or significant vulnerability |
| Reputational | Low | Any risk of material negative media coverage |
