# Board Deck Gallery Index

## Overview
An index of publicly available pitch decks, board decks, and investor presentations that serve as reference material for board-level communication standards.

## Iconic Pitch Decks (Public Domain)

### Airbnb Original Pitch Deck (2009)
- 10 slides. Problem → Solution → Market → Business Model → Traction → Team
- What makes it great: Simple, visual, focused on the core insight (monetize spare rooms)
- Source: Widely available via Google search "Airbnb original pitch deck"

### LinkedIn Series B Pitch Deck (2004)
- Reid Hoffman's deck for LinkedIn's Series B raise
- What makes it great: Clear network effects explanation, realistic financials
- Source: reidhoffman.org

### Buffer Transparent Fundraising Deck (2014)
- Buffer published their entire fundraising deck publicly
- What makes it great: Radical transparency, honest metrics including weaknesses
- Source: open.buffer.com

### Front App Series A Pitch Deck (2016)
- Well-structured SaaS pitch with clear metrics and competitive positioning
- What makes it great: Data-heavy, clear unit economics, realistic projections

### Sequoia Capital Pitch Deck Template
- The industry standard template for startup pitches
- Structure: Company purpose, Problem, Solution, Why now, Market, Competition, Product, Business model, Team, Financials
- Source: sequoiacap.com

## Board Deck References

### Y Combinator Board Deck Template
- Designed for early-stage companies doing their first board meetings
- Simple, focused on key metrics and strategic questions
- Source: ycombinator.com/library

### A16Z Board Deck Best Practices
- Andreessen Horowitz's guidance on effective board communication
- Emphasis on narrative over data, strategic questions over operational updates
- Source: a16z.com

### Carta Board Deck Template
- Comprehensive template for growth-stage board meetings
- Includes financial, operational, and strategic sections
- Source: carta.com/resources

## Board Application
- Use these examples to set expectations for board deck quality and structure
- Adapt templates to your company's stage and needs rather than copying verbatim
- Focus on substance over design: a clear narrative on plain slides beats a beautiful deck with no insight
- Review how your company's board materials compare to best-in-class examples
