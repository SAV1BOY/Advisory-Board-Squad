# Task: Board Meeting Preparation

## Objective
Prepare all materials, logistics, and stakeholders for a board meeting so that the meeting runs efficiently, decisions are well-informed, and board members' time is maximally valued.

## Trigger
- 3 weeks before any scheduled board meeting
- Immediately upon scheduling a special or emergency board session

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Ops
- **Contributors:** CEO, CFO, functional leaders presenting at the meeting
- **Reviewer:** Board Chair (approves final package)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Meeting date confirmed and calendar holds sent
- [ ] Agenda topics identified by CEO and Chair
- [ ] Prior meeting minutes and action items available
- [ ] Financial data current through most recent close

## Steps

1. **Agenda Development (T-21 days)**
   - CEO and Chair align on agenda topics and time allocation
   - Identify decision items vs. discussion items vs. information items
   - Assign presenters and time limits for each agenda item
   - Draft agenda using board-agenda.md template
   - Chair approves final agenda

2. **Material Solicitation (T-18 days)**
   - Send material requests to all presenters with deadlines and format requirements
   - Provide relevant templates (briefs, memos) to presenters
   - Share the pre-read pack index template so presenters understand how their material fits
   - Remind presenters: pre-read materials replace presentations — write for reading, not slides

3. **Material Collection and Quality Review (T-14 to T-10 days)**
   - Collect all materials from presenters by T-14
   - Review each document for: completeness, clarity, data accuracy, alignment with template standards
   - Cross-check financial figures across documents for consistency
   - Flag any missing sections, unsupported claims, or stale data
   - Return materials to authors with feedback by T-12
   - Collect revised materials by T-10

4. **Package Assembly (T-10 to T-7 days)**
   - Compile all materials into the pre-read package
   - Create pre-read pack index with reading priorities and time estimates
   - Include prior meeting minutes (for approval), action item tracker, board scorecard
   - Add page numbers and consistent formatting
   - Chair reviews final package by T-7

5. **Distribution (T-5 days minimum)**
   - Upload package to board portal
   - Send distribution email with: package link, key questions for each decision item, logistics reminder
   - Flag any materials that are new or significantly changed from typical package
   - Confirm receipt from all board members within 48 hours

6. **Logistics Confirmation (T-3 days)**
   - Confirm attendance and quorum
   - Verify video conferencing setup (test link, backup plan)
   - Arrange physical room if in-person (AV, catering, access)
   - Brief minute-taker on format and expectations
   - Prepare decision tracking sheet and action item template
   - Send final logistics email to all attendees

7. **Pre-Meeting Briefings (T-2 to T-1 days)**
   - CEO conducts brief pre-call with Chair to align on discussion flow
   - Board Ops addresses any pre-read questions from board members
   - Presenters confirm they are prepared and within time limits
   - Any late-breaking updates are communicated to the full board

8. **Day-of Preparation**
   - Arrive early / open video room 15 minutes before start
   - Verify all technology works (screen sharing, recording if applicable)
   - Have printed copies of key documents available (if in-person)
   - Prepare timer for agenda management
   - Have emergency contact information for all attendees

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Pre-Read Quality Standard:** Every document must be self-contained (readable without a presentation), sourced (data points have origins), and actionable (clear ask of the board)
- **Meeting Design:** Decision items get 2x the time of information items; discussion before decision

## Checklists

### Material Quality Gate
- [ ] All documents follow standard templates
- [ ] Financial figures are internally consistent across all documents
- [ ] Data is less than 90 days old (or staleness is explicitly noted)
- [ ] Each decision item has clear options with trade-offs
- [ ] No document exceeds recommended length for its template type
- [ ] Confidential markings applied where appropriate

### Logistics Quality Gate
- [ ] Quorum confirmed
- [ ] All attendees confirmed (attendance and mode: in-person/remote)
- [ ] Technology tested (video, screen share, recording)
- [ ] Pre-read distributed minimum 5 business days before meeting
- [ ] Minute-taker briefed
- [ ] Chair has approved agenda and package

## Output
- Final approved agenda (distributed)
- Complete pre-read package (distributed)
- Pre-read pack index (distributed)
- Logistics confirmation (sent)
- Meeting room / technology (ready)

## Handoffs
- Chair takes over meeting facilitation (see: meeting-run.md)
- Board Ops transitions to minute-taking and action tracking during the meeting
- Post-meeting: Board Ops begins minutes-and-followups.md task

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-read distribution timing | >=5 business days before meeting | Calendar tracking |
| Material revision cycles | <=2 rounds of feedback | Tracking |
| Board member pre-read completion | >80% read materials before meeting | Self-report or portal analytics |
| Quorum confirmation | >=3 days before meeting | Confirmation tracking |
| Total prep time | <15 hours per regular meeting | Time tracking |
