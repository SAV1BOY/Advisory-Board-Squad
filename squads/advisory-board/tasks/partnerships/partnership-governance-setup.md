# Task: Partnership Governance Setup

## Objective
Establish the governance framework for a new partnership including joint decision-making structures, performance management, communication cadence, and escalation procedures.

## Trigger
- Partnership agreement signed and implementation beginning
- Existing partnership governance needs to be formalized or improved
- Board directs governance establishment for a strategic relationship

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Partnership Owner or Business Development Lead
- **Support:** Legal for governance documentation, Product or Engineering for technical governance, Finance for financial governance
- **Advisory:** Board sponsor for the partnership


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → partnership-evaluation`

- **Lead:** partnership-broker (Reid Hoffman)
- **Support:** governance-clerk (Simon Sinek), board-chair (Board Chair)
- **Review:** ethics-compass (Yvon Chouinard)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Partnership agreement signed
- [ ] Key contacts identified on both sides
- [ ] Partnership objectives and KPIs defined
- [ ] Initial integration or collaboration plan agreed

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Define the governance structure including joint steering committee membership, meeting cadence, decision rights, and voting or consensus mechanisms

2. **Step 2:** Establish operational cadence including regular working group meetings, status reporting, and issue tracking

3. **Step 3:** Define the performance framework including KPIs, measurement methodology, review frequency, and targets

4. **Step 4:** Create the escalation framework including issue categories, escalation levels from operational through executive to board, and response time expectations

5. **Step 5:** Document the communication protocol including authorized spokespersons, information sharing boundaries, confidentiality requirements, and external communication approval

6. **Step 6:** Set up the review and renewal process including quarterly health checks, annual strategic reviews, and renewal or exit decision timeline

7. **Step 7:** Document everything in a Partnership Governance Charter signed by both parties

8. **Step 8:** Partnership Governance Charter; KPI dashboard; Meeting cadence and calendar; Escalation framework; Communication protocol

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Partnership Health Scorecard:** Assess relationship health, value delivery, dependency balance, and strategic alignment
- **RACI for Partnerships:** Clear responsibility assignment across both organizations
- **Value Chain Analysis:** Map where each partner adds value and where dependencies exist

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Both parties' interests and value contribution are clearly documented
- [ ] Risks are identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled and understood
- [ ] Legal review completed for all material terms
- [ ] Exit strategy and costs are understood
- [ ] Governance structure ensures accountability without bureaucracy
- [ ] Board has appropriate visibility into material partnerships

## Output
- Ongoing performance feeds into quarterly board review
- Issues escalate through the defined framework
- Annual review informs partner-eval for renewal decision
- Governance effectiveness feeds into board-effectiveness-review for partnership oversight
