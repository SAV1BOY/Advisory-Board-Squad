# E-Commerce Industry Benchmarks

## Key Findings

1. **Customer acquisition cost inflation is the dominant challenge.** Paid digital acquisition costs have risen 45-60% since 2022 across major channels (Meta, Google, TikTok). Profitable e-commerce now requires a diversified acquisition strategy with organic, referral, and owned-media channels contributing at least 40% of new customers.
2. **Repeat purchase rate separates winners from losers.** E-commerce companies with 30%+ repeat purchase rates within 12 months achieve profitability 2.3x faster than those below 20%. The board should prioritize retention metrics alongside growth metrics.
3. **Gross margin compression from logistics is accelerating.** Consumer expectations for free, fast shipping have raised fulfillment costs to 15-25% of revenue for most DTC brands. Companies that have not optimized their logistics network by $10M revenue face structural margin challenges.
4. **Subscription models are losing favor with consumers.** Subscription fatigue has pushed churn rates for non-consumable subscription boxes to 60-75% annually. Replenishment subscriptions (consumables) maintain healthier retention at 35-50% annual churn.
5. **Omnichannel is no longer optional at scale.** DTC brands that expanded into wholesale or retail partnerships after $20M revenue grew 1.7x faster than those that remained online-only. However, channel conflict management becomes a board-level concern.

## Implications for Board

- **Unit economics by channel.** The board should require unit economics reporting broken down by acquisition channel. Blended CAC masks unprofitable channels being subsidized by organic traffic.
- **Retention investment.** If the repeat purchase rate is below 25%, the board should challenge any proposal to increase acquisition spend. Fixing retention before scaling acquisition is the capital-efficient path.
- **Fulfillment strategy review.** At $5M+ revenue, the board should review the fulfillment strategy annually — 3PL vs. in-house, regional fulfillment centers, and shipping cost pass-through decisions.
- **Channel diversification.** Over-dependence on a single acquisition channel (>40% of revenue) creates platform risk. The board should monitor channel concentration quarterly.
- **Return rate management.** High return rates destroy unit economics silently. The board should track return rates by product category and require a return reduction plan if rates exceed industry medians.
