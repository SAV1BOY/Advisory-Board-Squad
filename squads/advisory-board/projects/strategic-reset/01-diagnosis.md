# Strategic Reset: Diagnosis

## Objective
Identify the root causes of strategic failure or misalignment before proposing new directions. Diagnosis must precede prescription — a strategy built on an incorrect diagnosis will fail regardless of its elegance.

## Inputs
- Strategic reset brief (00-brief.md).
- Historical performance data: revenue, growth rate, market share, customer metrics.
- Customer feedback: churn reasons, NPS trends, feature requests, win/loss analysis.
- Competitive analysis: who is winning and why.
- Internal assessment: team capabilities, culture health, operational effectiveness.
- Previous strategic plans and their outcomes.

## Process
1. Assemble evidence across five domains: market, customer, product, team, and financial.
2. Identify the core challenge in one paragraph using strategy-blocks.md Block 2.
3. Conduct root cause analysis: use 5 Whys on each major symptom to reach the underlying cause.
4. Document competing diagnoses: what are alternative explanations for the same evidence?
5. Validate diagnosis with external perspectives: board advisors, customers, industry experts.
6. Stress-test: present the diagnosis to the team most invested in the current strategy and listen to their pushback.
7. Finalize the diagnosis with explicit acknowledgment of what was wrong about previous strategic assumptions.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` (Block 2: Diagnosis)
- `lib/patterns/postmortem-pattern.md` (root cause analysis methodology)
- `lib/utilities/scenario-table.md` (testing diagnosis against multiple futures)
- `data/registries/lessons-learned-registry.yaml` (past lessons relevant to current diagnosis)

## Output
- Diagnosis document: core challenge, root causes, evidence base, competing diagnoses and why they were rejected, what we got wrong before.
- Validated and stress-tested diagnosis ready to inform strategic choice development.
