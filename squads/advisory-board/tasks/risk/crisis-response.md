# Task: Crisis Response

## Objective
Execute a structured crisis response when a significant adverse event occurs. Ensure rapid containment, clear communication, board involvement at the appropriate level, and systematic post-crisis learning.

## Trigger
- Any event classified as Level 1 Critical or Level 2 Severe per the crisis brief urgency framework
- Data breach or cybersecurity incident
- Material legal action or regulatory investigation
- Executive misconduct allegation
- Major operational failure affecting customers
- Significant reputational event

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Incident Commander designated by CEO or Board Chair
- **Support:** Communications Lead, Legal Counsel, relevant functional leaders, external advisors as needed
- **Advisory:** Board Chair and full board per urgency level communication protocol

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Crisis brief template available
- [ ] Emergency contact list current
- [ ] Crisis communication protocol established
- [ ] Legal counsel on standby
- [ ] Insurance policies reviewed for coverage

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Activate the crisis response team and designate the Incident Commander within 1 hour of event detection

2. **Step 2:** Assess and classify the crisis using the urgency framework in the crisis-brief template

3. **Step 3:** Complete the crisis brief and distribute to the board at the appropriate urgency level

4. **Step 4:** Contain the immediate threat taking all necessary actions to stop ongoing damage

5. **Step 5:** Develop and execute the communication plan for internal, external, and regulatory stakeholders

6. **Step 6:** Manage the ongoing response with regular status updates to the board per the communication protocol

7. **Step 7:** Once contained, transition from response mode to recovery mode restoring normal operations

8. **Step 8:** Conduct post-crisis review and prepare post-mortem report within 14 days of crisis resolution

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Risk Scoring Matrix:** Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5) for consistent risk quantification
- **Swiss Cheese Model:** Multiple layers of defense with the understanding that each layer has holes
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis:** Assume the worst outcome and work backward to identify causes and prevention

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All risk dimensions assessed (financial, operational, strategic, people, legal, technology, reputational)
- [ ] Risk scoring is consistent and calibrated across assessors
- [ ] Mitigation plans are specific with owners and timelines
- [ ] Board reporting is clear, concise, and highlights material changes
- [ ] Risk appetite thresholds are respected and breaches escalated
- [ ] Historical risk data preserved for trend analysis

## Output
- Crisis Brief completed and distributed
- Communication log
- Incident timeline
- Post-mortem report
- Updated risk register
- Lessons learned document

## Handoffs
- Post-mortem feeds into postmortem-report template
- Risk lessons feed into risk-register-update
- Process improvements feed into relevant operating procedures
- Board debrief scheduled within 30 days

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Risk register currency | Updated within agreed cadence | Last updated timestamp |
| Risk coverage | All material risk categories represented | Category audit |
| Mitigation plan quality | 100% of high-severity risks have active mitigation | Register audit |
| Board reporting timeliness | Risk update in every quarterly board package | Board package review |
| Risk prediction accuracy | >60% of materialized risks were previously identified | Incident vs. register comparison |
