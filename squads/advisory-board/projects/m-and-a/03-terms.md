# M&A: Terms

## Objective
Negotiate acquisition terms that protect the acquiring company's interests while creating a fair deal for the target.

## Inputs
- Diligence findings (02-diligence.md). Valuation model. Legal counsel. Board guidance on acceptable terms.

## Process
1. Set valuation range based on diligence findings and financial modeling.
2. Structure the deal: asset purchase vs. stock purchase, cash vs. equity, earnout provisions.
3. Negotiate key terms: purchase price, representations and warranties, indemnification, escrow, earnout metrics.
4. Address retention: key employee retention packages, non-compete agreements.
5. Define transition and integration provisions in the agreement.
6. Legal review and board approval of final terms.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/capital-blocks.md` Block 3 (Investment Decision Card)
- `lib/components/decision-memo-blocks.md` (full decision memo for board)

## Output
Negotiated term sheet or letter of intent. Board-approved terms. Definitive agreement draft.
