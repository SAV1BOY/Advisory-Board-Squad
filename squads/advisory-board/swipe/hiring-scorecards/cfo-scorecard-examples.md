# CFO Scorecard Examples

## CFO Evaluation Dimensions

### Financial Management (Weight: 30%)
- Accuracy and timeliness of financial reporting
- Cash flow management and forecasting precision
- Budget management (actual vs. plan variance)
- Capital structure optimization
- **Rating scale:** 1 (frequent errors, poor forecasting) to 5 (institutional-quality financial management)

### Strategic Partnership with CEO (Weight: 20%)
- Acts as a strategic thought partner to the CEO, not just a bean counter
- Provides data-driven insights that improve strategic decision-making
- Challenges assumptions constructively with analytical rigor
- Translates business strategy into financial models and scenarios
- **Rating scale:** 1 (purely transactional) to 5 (CEO's most valued strategic partner)

### Capital Allocation and Fundraising (Weight: 15%)
- Advises on optimal capital allocation across initiatives
- Manages fundraising processes efficiently (equity, debt)
- Builds and maintains relationships with investors and lenders
- Evaluates M&A opportunities with rigorous financial analysis
- **Rating scale:** 1 (no fundraising capability) to 5 (independently leads capital markets strategy)

### Risk Management (Weight: 15%)
- Identifies and quantifies financial, operational, and compliance risks
- Builds appropriate insurance, hedging, and risk mitigation programs
- Ensures compliance with tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements
- Establishes internal controls that prevent fraud and error
- **Rating scale:** 1 (reactive, compliance-only) to 5 (proactive, enterprise-wide risk management)

### Team Building and Infrastructure (Weight: 10%)
- Builds a capable finance, accounting, and analytics team
- Implements systems (ERP, FP&A tools, BI) that scale with the company
- Creates self-service data and analytics for the organization
- Develops next-generation finance talent
- **Rating scale:** 1 (everything depends on the CFO) to 5 (self-sufficient, scalable finance organization)

### Board and Investor Communication (Weight: 10%)
- Prepares clear, accurate board financial packages
- Communicates financial results with appropriate context and narrative
- Manages investor expectations proactively
- Presents complex financial information accessibly
- **Rating scale:** 1 (data dump without narrative) to 5 (investor-quality communication and credibility)

## Stage-Specific CFO Criteria

### Early-Stage CFO / VP Finance
- Hands-on bookkeeping and reporting capability
- Fundraising process management
- Unit economics analysis and financial modeling
- Lean team management

### Growth-Stage CFO
- GAAP compliance and audit readiness
- Multi-entity, multi-currency management
- FP&A and scenario planning sophistication
- Board and investor communication maturity

### Pre-IPO / Public CFO
- SOX compliance readiness
- Public market investor relations
- Complex capital structure management
- Earnings guidance and management
