# Task: Board Effectiveness Review

## Objective
Assess how well the board is functioning across governance, strategy, oversight, and dynamics. Identify strengths and improvement areas. Produce an actionable effectiveness report.

## Trigger
- Annually (scheduled in Q4 or aligned with fiscal year end)
- After significant board composition changes
- When board performance concerns are raised by any member

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Chair or independent facilitator
- **Support:** Board Ops (survey distribution, data compilation)
- **Participants:** All board members, CEO, CFO (management perspective)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Board has been operating for at least 6 months (sufficient history to assess)
- [ ] Survey instrument prepared or external facilitator engaged
- [ ] Board members committed to participate candidly
- [ ] Prior year effectiveness report available for comparison (if not first year)

## Steps

1. **Design the Assessment (T-4 weeks)**
   - Select methodology: self-assessment survey, 1:1 interviews, facilitated group discussion, or combination
   - Prepare or customize the assessment questionnaire covering: strategic oversight, meeting effectiveness, risk oversight, board-management relationship, board dynamics
   - Determine whether to use an external facilitator (recommended every 2-3 years)
   - Set timeline and communicate to all participants

2. **Distribute Survey (T-3 weeks)**
   - Send assessment survey to all board members and management participants
   - Survey should be anonymous (except for external facilitator interviews)
   - Allow 2 weeks for completion
   - Board Ops sends one reminder at the midpoint

3. **Conduct 1:1 Interviews (T-3 to T-1 weeks)**
   - Chair or external facilitator conducts 30-minute interviews with each board member
   - Use semi-structured format: survey themes plus open-ended exploration
   - Ask each member: "What is the single most impactful change we could make to board effectiveness?"
   - Document themes without individual attribution

4. **Compile and Analyze Results (T-1 week)**
   - Aggregate survey results and identify patterns
   - Compare to prior year results (if available)
   - Identify the top 3 strengths and top 3 improvement areas
   - Benchmark against best practices (industry standards, governance frameworks)
   - Prepare the board-effectiveness-report using the template

5. **Present and Discuss (Board Meeting — Executive Session)**
   - Present findings during executive session (management excused)
   - Facilitate discussion of results — validate, challenge, and prioritize
   - Agree on 3-5 specific improvement actions with owners and timelines
   - Decide what (if anything) to communicate to management

6. **Implement Improvements**
   - Chair assigns owners for each improvement action
   - Board Ops tracks implementation
   - Progress reviewed at the next board effectiveness assessment
   - Quick wins implemented within 30 days; structural changes within 90 days

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Board Effectiveness Dimensions:** Strategic oversight, meeting effectiveness, risk oversight, board-management relationship, board dynamics, composition adequacy
- **Maturity Model:** Assess against governance maturity levels (reactive, structured, proactive, leading)

## Checklists

### Assessment Quality Gate
- [ ] All board members participated (survey and/or interview)
- [ ] Management perspective included (CEO, CFO survey)
- [ ] Results compared to prior year
- [ ] Both quantitative scores and qualitative themes captured
- [ ] Specific, actionable recommendations (not generic advice)
- [ ] Report reviewed by Chair before presentation

## Output
- Board Effectiveness Report (using board-effectiveness-report.md template)
- Improvement action plan with owners and timelines
- Updated skills matrix (if composition changes recommended)

## Handoffs
- Improvement actions tracked in action item tracker
- Composition recommendations feed into board-composition-review.md
- Results inform the annual planning session agenda

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Participation rate | 100% of board members | Survey/interview tracking |
| Overall effectiveness score | Improvement over prior year | Survey comparison |
| Improvement actions completed | >80% within agreed timelines | Action tracking |
| Year-over-year trend | Positive across all dimensions | Annual comparison |
