# Task: Forecast Accuracy Review

## Objective
Assess how accurate the company's financial and operational forecasts have been. Identify systematic biases and improve forecasting methodology for better board and management decision-making.

## Trigger
- Quarterly as part of the financial review
- Annually for a comprehensive forecast accuracy assessment
- When a major forecast miss occurs requiring explanation
- Before setting next year's plan to calibrate assumptions

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CFO
- **Support:** CEO, functional leaders who own forecasted metrics, data or analytics team
- **Advisory:** Board members with financial expertise


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → decision-quality-review`

- **Lead:** capital-allocator (Charlie Munger)
- **Support:** strategic-advisor (Peter Thiel), board-chair (Board Chair)
- **Review:** governance-clerk (Simon Sinek)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Historical forecast data available for comparison
- [ ] Actual results finalized for the review period
- [ ] Forecasting methodology documented
- [ ] Prior accuracy reviews available

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Compile all forecasts made during the review period including revenue, expenses, cash flow, customer metrics, and operational KPIs

2. **Step 2:** Compare each forecast to actual results calculating the variance percentage for every metric

3. **Step 3:** Identify systematic biases by analyzing whether forecasts consistently over- or under-estimate and by how much

4. **Step 4:** Assess forecast accuracy by time horizon noting whether 30-day, 90-day, and 12-month forecasts have different accuracy profiles

5. **Step 5:** Identify the root causes of major forecast misses distinguishing between model errors, assumption errors, and external shocks

6. **Step 6:** Develop recommendations for improving forecast accuracy including methodology changes, assumption validation processes, and confidence intervals

7. **Step 7:** Present findings to the board as part of the quarterly or annual financial review

8. **Step 8:** Forecast Accuracy Report; Systematic bias identification; Methodology improvement recommendations; Updated forecasting confidence intervals

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Retrospective Analysis:** Structured look-back that separates process quality from outcome quality
- **Continuous Improvement Cycle:** Plan-Do-Check-Act applied to governance and management processes
- **Balanced Scorecard:** Multi-dimensional assessment that prevents over-indexing on any single metric

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Review is evidence-based with specific data points, not impressions
- [ ] Both positive and negative findings are presented honestly
- [ ] Root causes identified, not just symptoms
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Historical comparison included to show trends
- [ ] Board has clear insight into what needs to change

## Output
- Improved forecasting feeds into all financial planning
- Bias corrections improve runway-scenarios
- Lessons inform next year's annual planning
- Confidence levels inform board-level decision-making
