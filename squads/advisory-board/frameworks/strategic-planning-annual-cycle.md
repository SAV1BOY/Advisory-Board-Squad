# Strategic Planning Annual Cycle

## Purpose

This framework provides a 12-month calendar for strategic planning activities that ensures the organization is continuously aligning strategy, execution, and learning. Most organizations treat strategic planning as an annual event (usually a painful offsite). This framework distributes strategic thinking across the year in a structured cadence that keeps strategy alive and adaptive rather than static and stale.

## When to Use

- When establishing or improving the organization's planning rhythm
- When strategy disconnects from execution because it is only revisited annually
- When the board wants a structured governance calendar
- When scaling a company and needing to formalize previously ad-hoc planning
- When the organization needs to balance long-term thinking with quarterly execution

## Core Model

### The Annual Cycle (Quarterly Rhythm)

**Q1: Strategy Review and Annual Planning (January-March)**
- Annual retrospective: What did we learn last year?
- Strategy Choice Cascade refresh: Is our Where to Play / How to Win still right?
- Annual goals and OKRs for the year
- Budget allocation aligned with strategy
- Board strategy review session

**Q2: Execution and Early Course Correction (April-June)**
- Quarterly business review: Are we on track?
- Early signal detection: What's changing in the market?
- Mid-year scenario refresh: Are our assumptions still valid?
- Team-level OKR progress reviews
- Board operational review session

**Q3: Mid-Year Strategy Check and H2 Planning (July-September)**
- Half-year retrospective: What's working, what isn't?
- Strategy stress-test against new data
- H2 priority adjustment: What should we change?
- Competitive landscape refresh
- Board strategy check-in session

**Q4: Year-End Assessment and Next Year Prep (October-December)**
- Full-year performance assessment
- Strategy options for next year
- Pre-planning for Q1 strategy session
- Scenario planning for next year's environment
- Board annual effectiveness review

### Monthly Activities
- Leadership team meeting with strategic agenda items
- Key metric review and trend analysis
- One strategic conversation per month (not operational — strategic)

### Continuous Activities
- Decision logging
- Market signal monitoring
- Competitive intelligence gathering
- Stakeholder feedback collection

## Steps

1. **Map the annual calendar.** Place each quarterly activity on the organizational calendar.
2. **Assign ownership.** Who facilitates each session? Who prepares the materials?
3. **Define inputs and outputs for each session.** What pre-reads are needed? What decisions should result?
4. **Integrate with board calendar.** Board meetings should align with the planning cycle.
5. **Protect the time.** Strategic planning sessions should not be cancelled for operational emergencies.
6. **Build feedback loops.** Each quarter's learning should inform the next quarter's focus.

## Application Guidelines

### For Board Advisors
- The board calendar should synchronize with the company's strategic planning cycle.
- Push for quarterly strategy check-ins, not just annual reviews. Annual planning alone allows too much drift.
- Participate in the Q1 annual planning and Q3 mid-year check as a minimum.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Annual-only planning.** Strategy set once a year becomes stale within months.
2. **No retrospective.** Planning without learning from the past year repeats mistakes.
3. **Operational crowding.** Allowing operational discussions to consume strategic planning time.
4. **No connection to execution.** Strategy documents that don't connect to quarterly OKRs are fiction.

## Related Frameworks

- **Operating System of Meetings** — The planning cycle defines the meeting rhythm
