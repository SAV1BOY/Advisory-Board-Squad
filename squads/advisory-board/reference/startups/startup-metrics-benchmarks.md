## Burn and Efficiency Metrics

### Burn Multiple
- **Formula:** Net burn / Net new ARR
- **Interpretation:** How much are you burning to generate each dollar of new ARR?
- **< 1x:** Exceptional efficiency (rare in growth phase)
- **1-2x:** Good efficiency
- **2-3x:** Acceptable in growth phase
- **> 3x:** Burning too fast relative to growth

### Runway
- **Formula:** Cash / Monthly burn rate
- **Minimum comfort:** 12 months
- **Fundraising trigger:** Start raising at 9 months of runway
- **Danger zone:** < 6 months without funded plan

### Magic Number
- **Formula:** (Current quarter ARR - Previous quarter ARR) x 4 / Previous quarter sales & marketing spend
- **> 1.0:** Very efficient; increase spend
- **0.5-1.0:** Efficient; maintain or increase
- **< 0.5:** Inefficient; diagnose before spending more

## Board Application
- Use these benchmarks to contextualize the company's performance relative to peers
- Track metrics over time to identify trends, not just snapshots
- Benchmarks vary by market, geography, and business model—use as guidelines, not absolute standards
- When metrics deteriorate, investigate root causes before prescribing solutions
