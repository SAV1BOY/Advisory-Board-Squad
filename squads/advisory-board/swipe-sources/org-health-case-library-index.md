# Org Health Case Library Index

## Overview
An index of organizational health cases—both exemplary and cautionary—that illustrate how culture, leadership, and team dynamics determine organizational outcomes.

## Exemplary Cases

### Pixar — Creative Confidence
- **Theme:** Building a culture where creative criticism is constructive, not destructive
- **Key mechanism:** Braintrust meetings where candid feedback is given without authority to direct changes
- **Source:** "Creativity, Inc." by Ed Catmull
- **Board lesson:** Honest feedback systems require explicit separation of feedback from authority

### Toyota — The Toyota Way
- **Theme:** Continuous improvement (kaizen) embedded in daily operations
- **Key mechanism:** Any employee can stop the production line to address a quality issue
- **Source:** "The Toyota Way" by Jeffrey Liker
- **Board lesson:** Empowering frontline workers to stop and fix problems prevents small issues from becoming crises

### Costco — Employee Investment
- **Theme:** Paying above-market wages and investing in employee development
- **Key mechanism:** Starting wages significantly above industry average, internal promotion pipeline
- **Board lesson:** Employee investment produces lower turnover, higher productivity, and stronger customer experience

### Bridgewater — Radical Transparency
- **Theme:** Complete transparency in communication and decision-making
- **Key mechanism:** Recorded meetings, real-time feedback tools, believability-weighted decisions
- **Source:** "Principles" by Ray Dalio
- **Board lesson:** Transparency reduces politics but requires psychological safety to function

## Cautionary Cases

### Wells Fargo — Incentive-Driven Fraud (2016)
- **Theme:** Aggressive sales quotas created incentives for employees to open millions of fake accounts
- **Root cause:** Incentive structure that rewarded volume without adequate controls
- **Board lesson:** Incentive design is a board responsibility. Show me the incentive, and I'll show you the outcome.

### Theranos — Culture of Fear
- **Theme:** Employees who raised concerns were threatened, silenced, or terminated
- **Root cause:** CEO who prioritized narrative over reality and punished truth-telling
- **Board lesson:** Organizations that punish dissent accumulate hidden risks until they become catastrophic

### GE under Welch/Immelt — Performance Culture Run Amok
- **Theme:** Stack ranking and "rank and yank" culture created destructive internal competition
- **Root cause:** Over-indexing on individual performance metrics at the expense of collaboration and innovation
- **Board lesson:** Performance cultures must balance individual accountability with team cohesion

## Board Application
- Use exemplary cases to illustrate what good org health practices look like in action
- Use cautionary cases to identify patterns the company should avoid
- Assign 1-2 case studies per quarter for board-level discussion
- When organizational issues arise, reference relevant cases for pattern-matching and solution inspiration
