# Fundraising: Pipeline

## Objective
Build and manage the investor pipeline with discipline and urgency, targeting investors who align with the company's stage, sector, and values.

## Inputs
- Fundraising narrative and materials (01-narrative.md).
- Target investor criteria: stage focus, sector expertise, check size, value-add profile.
- Network map: warm introductions available from board, advisors, and existing investors.

## Process
1. Build a target list of 40-60 investors segmented by tier (Tier 1: ideal fit, Tier 2: good fit, Tier 3: acceptable).
2. Map warm introduction paths for each target. Cold outreach is last resort.
3. Sequence outreach: start with Tier 2 to practice the pitch, then move to Tier 1.
4. Track pipeline in a CRM: stage (intro, first meeting, partner meeting, diligence, term sheet), next action, and notes.
5. Maintain a 2-week sprint cadence: batch meetings, maintain momentum, create urgency.
6. Debrief after every meeting: what went well, what questions were hardest, what to improve.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `data/registries/stakeholder-registry.yaml` (investor relationship tracking)
- `lib/components/partnership-blocks.md` (adapted for investor relationship evaluation)
- `lib/utilities/incentive-map.md` (understanding investor incentives)

## Output
- Active investor pipeline with status tracking.
- Meeting debriefs and pitch iteration log.
- Weekly pipeline report to the board.
