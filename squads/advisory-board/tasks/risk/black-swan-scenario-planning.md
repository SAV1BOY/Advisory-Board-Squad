# Task: Black Swan Scenario Planning

## Objective
Identify and prepare for low-probability, high-impact events that could fundamentally threaten the company. Develop response playbooks for scenarios that most companies fail to prepare for.

## Trigger
- Annually as part of strategic risk management
- After a black swan event affects a peer company or the broader market
- When entering new markets or geographies with unfamiliar risk profiles
- When the company reaches a scale where black swan impact becomes existential

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Strategy Lead
- **Support:** CFO for financial stress testing, CTO for technology scenarios, Legal for regulatory scenarios, CHRO for people scenarios
- **Advisory:** Board members who bring diverse perspectives and pattern recognition from prior crises

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Risk register current
- [ ] Financial model available for stress testing
- [ ] Business continuity plan current if one exists
- [ ] Insurance policies reviewed
- [ ] Key person and key vendor dependencies identified

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Identify potential black swan scenarios across categories including market collapse, technology disruption, regulatory upheaval, pandemic or natural disaster, key person loss, cybersecurity catastrophe, and supply chain failure

2. **Step 2:** For each scenario model the first-order and second-order impacts on revenue, cash, customers, team, and operations

3. **Step 3:** Stress test the financial model against each scenario to determine survivability

4. **Step 4:** Develop response playbooks for the top 5 most impactful scenarios including first 48-hour actions, 30-day response plan, and recovery approach

5. **Step 5:** Assess current insurance coverage against identified scenarios and close gaps

6. **Step 6:** Identify early warning indicators for each scenario that could provide advance notice

7. **Step 7:** Present scenarios and playbooks to the board for review, endorsement, and any preauthorized responses

8. **Step 8:** Review and update annually or when a scenario partially materializes

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Risk Scoring Matrix:** Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5) for consistent risk quantification
- **Swiss Cheese Model:** Multiple layers of defense with the understanding that each layer has holes
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis:** Assume the worst outcome and work backward to identify causes and prevention

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All risk dimensions assessed (financial, operational, strategic, people, legal, technology, reputational)
- [ ] Risk scoring is consistent and calibrated across assessors
- [ ] Mitigation plans are specific with owners and timelines
- [ ] Board reporting is clear, concise, and highlights material changes
- [ ] Risk appetite thresholds are respected and breaches escalated
- [ ] Historical risk data preserved for trend analysis

## Output
- Black Swan Scenario Library
- Response Playbooks for top 5 scenarios
- Financial stress test results
- Insurance gap analysis
- Early warning indicator dashboard

## Handoffs
- Playbooks inform crisis-response procedures
- Insurance gaps feed into procurement actions
- Financial stress tests inform runway-scenarios
- Early warning indicators feed into risk-register-update
- Board-preauthorized responses reduce response time in actual events

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Risk register currency | Updated within agreed cadence | Last updated timestamp |
| Risk coverage | All material risk categories represented | Category audit |
| Mitigation plan quality | 100% of high-severity risks have active mitigation | Register audit |
| Board reporting timeliness | Risk update in every quarterly board package | Board package review |
| Risk prediction accuracy | >60% of materialized risks were previously identified | Incident vs. register comparison |
