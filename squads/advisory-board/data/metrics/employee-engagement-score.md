# Employee Engagement Score

## Definition

A composite metric measuring the degree to which employees are emotionally invested in, motivated by, and committed to their work and the organization. Employee engagement is a leading indicator of retention, productivity, and customer satisfaction. It is measured through periodic surveys combining quantitative scores with qualitative sentiment analysis. The board should treat engagement as a strategic asset — high engagement compounds through better hiring (referrals), better execution (discretionary effort), and better customer outcomes.

## Formula

```
Employee Engagement Score = Weighted average of survey dimensions:
  - Purpose & Meaning (20%): "I understand how my work contributes to the company's mission"
  - Manager Effectiveness (20%): "My manager helps me grow and removes obstacles"
  - Growth & Development (15%): "I have opportunities to learn and advance"
  - Psychological Safety (15%): "I feel safe raising concerns and taking risks"
  - Recognition (10%): "My contributions are recognized and valued"
  - Autonomy (10%): "I have the freedom to do my best work"
  - Team Collaboration (10%): "My team works well together and supports each other"

Each dimension scored 1-5 by respondents. Composite scaled to 0-100.

eNPS (Employee Net Promoter Score) = % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6)
  on the question: "How likely are you to recommend this company as a place to work?"
```

## Target Range

| Rating | Score | eNPS | Status |
|--------|-------|------|--------|
| Exceptional | 80-100 | >50 | Top-decile employer; protect and celebrate |
| Strong | 65-79 | 25-50 | Healthy; address specific dimension weaknesses |
| Moderate | 50-64 | 0-24 | Improvement plan needed; leadership attention required |
| Weak | 35-49 | -20 to -1 | Urgent intervention; board-level discussion |
| Critical | <35 | <-20 | Organizational health crisis; cultural overhaul required |

**Target: Maintain composite score above 65 with no individual dimension below 55. Quarter-over-quarter improvement on the weakest dimension.**

## Data Sources

- Engagement survey platform (Culture Amp, Lattice, 15Five, or equivalent)
- Pulse survey responses (monthly or bi-weekly micro-surveys)
- Exit interview data (departing employee feedback themes)
- Glassdoor and Blind reviews (external sentiment monitoring)
- Referral rate data (employee willingness to recruit peers)
- Voluntary turnover data by team and tenure cohort

## Frequency

- **Full engagement survey:** Semi-annually (recommended Q1 and Q3)
- **Pulse surveys (3-5 questions):** Monthly or bi-weekly
- **Board reporting:** Quarterly — present composite score, dimension breakdown, and trend
- **Deep-dive analysis:** Semi-annually — segment by team, tenure, level, and location
- **Action plan review:** Quarterly — review progress on improvement initiatives from prior survey

## Owner

- **Primary:** VP of People or Chief People Officer
- **Board sponsor:** Board member with people/culture oversight
- **Data steward:** People Analytics or HR Operations
