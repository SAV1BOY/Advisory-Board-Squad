# Risk Register Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the risk register is a living, useful tool for identifying, assessing, and managing organizational risks, not a compliance artifact that gathers dust. A quality risk register surfaces the risks that matter, tracks mitigation progress, and informs decision-making.

## When to Use
- Quarterly during risk review sessions.
- When new material risks are identified.
- During board reporting on risk.
- When evaluating the organization's risk posture.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The risk register includes strategic, operational, financial, compliance, and reputational risks.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each risk has a named owner accountable for monitoring and mitigation.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each risk is assessed for likelihood (high/medium/low) and impact (high/medium/low).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Mitigation actions are specific, actionable, and have deadlines.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The register is updated at least quarterly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Emerging risks are added proactively, not only after they materialize.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The register is reviewed by the leadership team and shared with the board.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Risk tolerance thresholds are defined for each risk category.

## Red Flags
- The risk register has not been updated in over six months.
- Risks are described generically with no specific mitigation actions.
- No one owns the risk register maintenance.
- The register was created for compliance and has never been used for decision-making.
- New risks from the last quarter are not reflected.

## Escalation Triggers
- A material risk materializes that was not on the register.
- Mitigation actions have been overdue for more than 90 days.
- The board requests risk information and the register is inadequate.
- A risk owner is unable to provide a status update on their risks.
