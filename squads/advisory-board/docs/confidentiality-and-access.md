# Confidentiality & Access Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs the classification, handling, and access controls for all Advisory Board information. The board handles sensitive strategic, financial, and personnel information. Proper classification and access management protect the company and maintain stakeholder trust.

## Classification Levels

### Level 1: Board Confidential
Information restricted to board agents only. Not shared with the executive team or broader organization without Board-Chair explicit approval.

**Examples:**
- Board effectiveness review results (individual ratings).
- CEO performance assessment.
- Board discussions about executive personnel.
- Advisor compensation details.
- Preliminary M&A evaluations before management involvement.

**Handling:** Stored in the board-only secure repository. Not distributed via standard email without encryption. Verbal discussion restricted to board-only sessions.

### Level 2: Management Confidential
Information shared with the board and the executive team but not the broader organization.

**Examples:**
- Board meeting minutes.
- Strategic plans and competitive analysis.
- Financial projections and capital allocation models.
- Risk register details.
- Fundraising materials and investor pipeline.
- Pre-read materials for board meetings.

**Handling:** Shared via the board's secure distribution channel. Access restricted to board agents and named executive team members. Not forwarded without permission.

### Level 3: Internal
Information that may be shared within the organization but not externally.

**Examples:**
- Board-approved strategic priorities (after internal communication).
- Cross-squad strategic briefs.
- Published OKRs and performance dashboards.
- Organizational announcements.

**Handling:** Distributed through standard internal channels after Board-Chair approval. Not shared externally without explicit authorization.

### Level 4: Public
Information approved for external distribution.

**Examples:**
- Press releases and public announcements.
- Published stakeholder updates (after approval process).
- Public-facing company information.

**Handling:** Published through official channels only. Board-Chair or CEO approval required for anything originating from board discussions.

## Access Controls

### Board Materials Repository
- **Access:** Board agents only. CEO has access by default.
- **Admin:** Governance-Ops manages access permissions.
- **Review:** Access list reviewed quarterly by Governance-Ops.
- **Revocation:** Access revoked immediately upon agent role change or departure.

### Meeting Minutes and Decision Log
- **Access:** Board agents, CEO, and designated executive team members.
- **Redaction:** Board-Chair may redact sensitive portions of minutes before sharing with the executive team.
- **Archive:** Historical minutes archived in the board-only repository after 12 months.

### Data Room (Fundraising, M&A)
- **Access:** Controlled by Governance-Ops. Named individuals only.
- **NDA:** External parties require executed NDA before access.
- **Tracking:** Data room access is logged (who accessed what, when).
- **Revocation:** Access revoked within 24 hours of process conclusion or party withdrawal.

## Information Sharing Rules

### Board → Executive Team
- Board meeting decisions are shared (decision, rationale, action items).
- Board deliberation details (who said what) are not shared unless Board-Chair approves.
- Dissent records are shared at the decision level ("dissent was recorded") but not attributed to specific advisors without their consent.

### Board → Organization
- Strategic priorities and approved plans are communicated through the stakeholder communication workflow (17).
- Board meeting summaries may be shared in an abbreviated format approved by the Board-Chair.
- The board never communicates directly to the organization — communication flows through the CEO.

### Board → External Stakeholders
- All external communications follow the review chain in the stakeholder communication workflow (17).
- No board agent shares board information externally without Board-Chair and CEO approval.
- Investor communications follow the specific guidelines for that audience.

## Breach Protocol

If a confidentiality breach is suspected:
1. Report to Board-Chair and Governance-Ops immediately.
2. Governance-Ops investigates: what was shared, with whom, by whom.
3. Board-Chair assesses the impact and determines the response.
4. If the breach is material (affects company operations or legal standing), escalate to crisis protocol (workflow 07).
5. Implement corrective measures: access changes, process updates, personnel actions if warranted.
6. Document the incident and prevention measures.

## Retention

| Material Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Board meeting minutes | Permanent |
| Decision log | Permanent |
| Pre-read materials | 5 years |
| Financial models and projections | 5 years |
| M&A / fundraising materials | 7 years |
| Board effectiveness reviews | 5 years |
| Routine correspondence | 3 years |

Governance-Ops manages retention and archival per this schedule.
