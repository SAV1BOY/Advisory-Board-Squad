## Session Preparation Guide

### For Board Members
1. Review this brief and all attachments (estimated 45-60 minutes reading time).
2. Submit questions or requests for additional data to [Board Ops contact] by YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Prepare your perspective on the Key Question — come ready to share a point of view.
4. Review the competitive landscape summary and note any intelligence you can contribute.

### Discussion Framework
The session will follow this structure:
1. **Context Setting** (15 min) — CEO presents state of play.
2. **Data Walk-Through** (20 min) — Strategy lead reviews performance data.
3. **Structured Debate** (45 min) — Board discusses options using pre-mortem and red-team frameworks.
4. **Decision Capture** (15 min) — Chair summarizes decisions and assigns owners.
5. **Next Steps** (10 min) — Agree on communication plan and checkpoints.

### Pre-Mortem Prompt
Before endorsing any strategic direction, the board should answer: "Assume it is 12 months from now and this strategy has failed. What went wrong?" Document the top 3 failure modes and ensure mitigation plans exist for each.
