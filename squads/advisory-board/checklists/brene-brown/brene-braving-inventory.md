# Brene Brown BRAVING Inventory Checklist

## Purpose
Applies Brene Brown's BRAVING framework for assessing trust systematically. BRAVING stands for Boundaries, Reliability, Accountability, Vault (confidentiality), Integrity, Non-judgment, and Generosity. This inventory provides a structured way to assess trust across its seven dimensions, making abstract trust conversations concrete and actionable.

## When to Use
- During self-assessment of leadership trustworthiness.
- In 360-degree feedback processes.
- When diagnosing trust breakdowns in teams or relationships.
- During team-building exercises focused on trust.
- When coaching leaders on building trust.

## Gate Criteria

### Boundaries
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People respect others' boundaries and clearly communicate their own.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: When boundaries are unclear, people ask rather than assume.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization has clear policies about boundaries (work hours, communication, personal space).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Leaders model boundary-setting without stigma.

### Reliability
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People do what they say they will do, consistently.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Commitments are only made when they can be kept.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: When a commitment cannot be met, it is communicated early with an alternative.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Reliability is tracked and patterns of unreliability are addressed.

### Accountability
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People own their mistakes, apologize, and make amends.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Others are held accountable with kindness and clarity.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Accountability is a two-way street: leaders are accountable to their teams, not just the reverse.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization has mechanisms for accountability that do not rely on hierarchy alone.

### Vault
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Confidential information is kept confidential.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People do not share others' stories or information that is not theirs to share.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization defines what is confidential and ensures the definition is known.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Violations of confidentiality are treated seriously and addressed promptly.

### Integrity
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People choose courage over comfort when doing the right thing.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Actions align with stated values, even when it is costly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Practicing values is prioritized over professing values.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization creates conditions where integrity is possible, not just expected.

### Non-Judgment
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People can ask for help without being judged as weak or incompetent.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People can express feelings and uncertainty without being dismissed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Help is offered reciprocally; the relationship is not defined by one person always needing and the other always giving.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Differences in approach or perspective are explored rather than judged.

### Generosity
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People assume positive intent before jumping to negative conclusions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The most generous interpretation of others' words and actions is the starting point.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: When assumptions are checked, the conversation reveals understanding rather than accusation.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The culture discourages cynicism and encourages good faith interpretation.

## Red Flags
- People regularly fail on one or more BRAVING dimensions with no self-awareness.
- The organization has never explicitly discussed what trust looks like in behavioral terms.
- Accountability is one-directional: leaders hold teams accountable but are not accountable themselves.
- Gossip and confidentiality breaches are common and unaddressed.
- People are judged harshly for asking for help or admitting uncertainty.
- The culture assumes negative intent by default.

## Escalation Triggers
- A BRAVING assessment reveals that a leader scores poorly on multiple dimensions with no improvement plan.
- A specific BRAVING dimension (e.g., Vault, Reliability) is systemically weak across the organization.
- Trust breakdown traced to a BRAVING failure causes material harm (talent loss, deal failure, crisis).
- Team trust scores decline across multiple BRAVING dimensions simultaneously.
- A leader is unable to accept feedback about their BRAVING behaviors.
