# Task: Partnership Evaluation

## Objective
Evaluate a potential strategic partner for strategic fit, value creation potential, dependency risk, and terms. Provide the board with a rigorous assessment to support a go or no-go decision.

## Trigger
- Inbound partnership proposal received
- Business development identifies a strategic opportunity
- Board member recommends a partnership connection
- Competitive pressure makes a partnership strategically necessary
- Build vs buy vs partner analysis favors partnership for a capability

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Business Development Lead
- **Support:** Product for technical fit assessment, Legal for terms review, Finance for economic modeling
- **Advisory:** Board members with relevant industry connections or partnership experience

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Initial partner conversations have occurred and mutual interest confirmed
- [ ] NDA signed if needed
- [ ] Preliminary partnership scope defined
- [ ] Board aware of the opportunity

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Assess strategic fit by mapping what each party brings and what mutual value is created that neither can create alone

2. **Step 2:** Conduct partner due diligence covering financial health, reputation, technical capability, cultural compatibility, and reference checks with other partners

3. **Step 3:** Evaluate partnership economics modeling revenue potential, cost structure, and resource requirements for both parties

4. **Step 4:** Assess dependency risk including revenue concentration, technology lock-in, data portability, and partner acquisition or pivot risk

5. **Step 5:** Review and negotiate terms focusing on the key issues of exclusivity, IP ownership, data rights, termination, and governance

6. **Step 6:** Develop the partnership governance framework including joint steering committee, escalation path, KPIs, and review cadence

7. **Step 7:** Prepare partnership-eval-brief and partnership-memo for board review and decision

8. **Step 8:** Partnership Evaluation Brief; Partnership Memo; Due diligence summary; Draft term sheet; Governance framework

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Partnership Health Scorecard:** Assess relationship health, value delivery, dependency balance, and strategic alignment
- **RACI for Partnerships:** Clear responsibility assignment across both organizations
- **Value Chain Analysis:** Map where each partner adds value and where dependencies exist

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Both parties' interests and value contribution are clearly documented
- [ ] Risks are identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled and understood
- [ ] Legal review completed for all material terms
- [ ] Exit strategy and costs are understood
- [ ] Governance structure ensures accountability without bureaucracy
- [ ] Board has appropriate visibility into material partnerships

## Output
- Approved partnership feeds into partnership-governance-setup
- Dependency risks feed into dependency-risk-check
- Economic projections feed into capital-allocation-review
- Rejected partnerships documented in decision log for future reference
