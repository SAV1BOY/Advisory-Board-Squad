# Task: Innovation Portfolio Review

## Objective
Review the company's portfolio of innovation investments across core business improvements, adjacent opportunities, and transformational bets. Ensure appropriate balance and expected returns.

## Trigger
- Quarterly or semi-annually as part of the strategy review
- When total R&D spend exceeds budget by more than 15 percent
- When innovation pipeline is not producing expected results
- Before annual planning to inform resource allocation

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CTO or VP Product
- **Support:** CEO, CFO, Engineering leadership
- **Advisory:** Board members with technology or product expertise

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current R&D spend breakdown available
- [ ] Project status for all innovation initiatives current
- [ ] Outcome data for completed innovation projects available
- [ ] Competitive innovation intelligence gathered

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Inventory all innovation investments categorizing each as core improvement, adjacent exploration, or transformational bet

2. **Step 2:** Assess performance of each investment against its original hypothesis and success criteria

3. **Step 3:** Evaluate the portfolio balance using the 70-20-10 framework or similar allocation model

4. **Step 4:** Identify projects to continue, pivot, or kill based on evidence and strategic alignment

5. **Step 5:** Model the expected return profile of the portfolio under current allocation vs proposed reallocation

6. **Step 6:** Develop recommendations for portfolio rebalancing with specific actions

7. **Step 7:** Present to the board with clear portfolio map, performance data, and rebalancing recommendations

8. **Step 8:** Innovation Portfolio Map; Project performance assessments; Rebalancing recommendations; Updated R&D allocation plan

## Frameworks to Apply
- **First Principles Analysis:** Break the problem down to fundamental truths before building solutions
- **Pre-Mortem:** Assume the initiative fails and work backward to identify likely causes
- **Reversibility Assessment:** Evaluate how reversible each option is — irreversible decisions deserve more scrutiny

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Analysis uses primary data sources, not assumptions
- [ ] Multiple options presented with quantified trade-offs
- [ ] Risks identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Success metrics defined with kill criteria
- [ ] Board has a clear decision or direction to give
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled

## Output
- Killed projects free up resources for reallocation
- New bets feed into capital-allocation-review
- Portfolio decisions inform product-strategy-review
