# Hoffman Learning Loop Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the organization maintains a tight learning loop: the cycle of hypothesis, experiment, measurement, and adaptation that drives continuous improvement and innovation. Hoffman emphasizes that startups and growing companies must learn faster than competitors to survive. This checklist verifies that learning is systematic, not accidental, and that insights are converted into action rather than being filed and forgotten.

## When to Use
- During product development cycles and sprint reviews.
- When evaluating the pace of organizational learning.
- When initiatives are not producing expected results and diagnosis is needed.
- When building or assessing innovation processes.
- During quarterly reviews of strategic hypothesis validation.

## Gate Criteria

### Hypothesis Discipline
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Key initiatives are framed as hypotheses with explicit predictions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Hypotheses are specific and falsifiable, not vague.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Success and failure criteria are defined before the experiment begins.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The riskiest assumptions are tested first.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Hypotheses are documented for later review against outcomes.

### Experiment Design
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Experiments are designed to produce actionable learning in the shortest time.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Experiment scope is minimized to test the hypothesis without over-building.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Control conditions or comparisons exist where feasible.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team commits to a decision framework before seeing results.

### Measurement and Analysis
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Metrics are defined and instrumented before the experiment runs.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Results are analyzed promptly after the experiment concludes.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Analysis distinguishes between signal and noise.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Unexpected results are investigated, not just dismissed.

### Action and Adaptation
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Learning is translated into specific decisions (continue, pivot, kill, expand).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The time from learning to action is measured and minimized.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Failed experiments generate reusable knowledge for the organization.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The learning loop's cycle time is tracked and improving.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Learnings are shared across teams, not siloed within the experimenting team.

## Red Flags
- Initiatives proceed without explicit hypotheses or success criteria.
- Experiments run indefinitely with no defined evaluation point.
- Results are interpreted to confirm the existing belief regardless of data.
- Learning is generated but not converted into action or decision changes.
- The same experiment is repeated without incorporating prior learnings.
- The organization's learning cycle time is longer than competitors'.

## Escalation Triggers
- The organization has not validated or invalidated any strategic hypothesis in the last quarter.
- Multiple experiments produce negative results but no strategic adjustment is made.
- The time from experiment to decision exceeds the defined threshold.
- Learning is siloed and other teams repeat mistakes that were already identified elsewhere.
- The learning culture deteriorates: teams stop experimenting because failure is punished.
