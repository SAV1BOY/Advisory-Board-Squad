# Annual Strategic Planning: Retrospective

## Objective
Conduct an honest review of the previous year's strategic performance before beginning the new planning cycle. Without understanding what worked and what failed, the new plan will repeat the same errors.

## Inputs
- Previous year's strategic plan and OKRs. Financial performance vs. plan. Initiative outcomes. Decision registry data. Market changes observed. Lessons learned registry.

## Process
1. Review each strategic initiative from the previous year: did it achieve its objectives?
2. Assess overall strategy execution: strategic alignment score, execution throughput, decision quality.
3. Identify what worked well and should be continued or expanded.
4. Identify what failed and conduct brief root cause analysis for each major failure.
5. Identify what changed in the market, competitive landscape, or internal capabilities that was not anticipated.
6. Assess forecast accuracy: were our predictions about the year correct?
7. Synthesize the top 5 lessons from the year.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `scripts/analysis/quarterly-review-builder.md` (aggregate quarterly reviews into annual view)
- `data/metrics/` (all metrics for year-over-year comparison)
- `lib/patterns/postmortem-pattern.md` (for initiative-level retrospectives)

## Output
Annual retrospective document. Top 5 lessons. Performance scorecard.
