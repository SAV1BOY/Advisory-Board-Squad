## Template Guidelines
**Word Limit:** 1,500 words maximum (excluding headers and tables)
**Reading Time:** Under 10 minutes
**Use When:** The company is preparing to raise external capital (equity, debt, or alternative financing) and needs board alignment on strategy, terms, timing, and process.

#### Timing Rationale
Address each of these factors:
1. **Runway:** How much runway remains and what is the minimum acceptable runway post-raise.
2. **Momentum:** Are current growth metrics at a local maximum that supports valuation?
3. **Market Conditions:** Is the fundraising environment favorable (interest rates, sector sentiment, comparable valuations)?
4. **Milestones:** What recent achievements strengthen the fundraising narrative?
5. **Competitive Pressure:** Are competitors raising or acquiring, creating urgency?

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Raising too early or too late:** Too early — you give up too much equity at a low valuation. Too late — you negotiate from weakness with depleting runway.
2. **Vanity valuation:** Optimizing for headline valuation over terms. A lower valuation with clean terms often creates more founder value than a high valuation with punitive preferences.
3. **Neglecting the business:** The business must continue to perform during the raise. Shield the operating team and maintain execution.
4. **Narrow funnel:** Running a process with fewer than 10 qualified investors reduces competitive tension and weakens negotiating position.
5. **Ignoring existing investors:** Existing investor participation (or lack thereof) sends a strong signal. Align with them before going external.
