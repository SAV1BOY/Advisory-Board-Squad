# Task: Culture Audit

## Objective
Conduct a comprehensive assessment of the company's culture against its stated values, strategic requirements, and employee experience. Identify gaps between aspired culture and lived culture.

## Trigger
- Annually as part of the organizational health review
- When engagement scores drop significantly
- After major organizational changes such as rapid growth, layoffs, or acquisition
- When the board raises concerns about cultural integrity
- Pre-fundraise or pre-acquisition cultural readiness assessment

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CHRO or People Lead
- **Support:** CEO, functional leaders, external culture consultant if needed
- **Advisory:** Board members particularly those with people and culture expertise

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Engagement survey data current
- [ ] Exit interview data compiled
- [ ] Glassdoor and external reputation data gathered
- [ ] Company values documented and published
- [ ] Prior culture assessment if available

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Design the assessment methodology combining quantitative surveys, qualitative interviews, behavioral observation, and artifact analysis

2. **Step 2:** Deploy culture survey covering values alignment, psychological safety, trust in leadership, belonging, communication, accountability, and innovation

3. **Step 3:** Conduct focus groups across different levels, tenures, and functions to gather rich qualitative data

4. **Step 4:** Analyze artifacts such as decision-making patterns, meeting behaviors, communication styles, recognition patterns, and hiring criteria for cultural signals

5. **Step 5:** Synthesize quantitative and qualitative findings into a culture gap analysis mapping aspired culture vs lived culture

6. **Step 6:** Identify root causes for gaps distinguishing between leadership behavior, structural incentives, communication failures, and environmental factors

7. **Step 7:** Develop prioritized recommendations with specific interventions, owners, timelines, and success metrics

8. **Step 8:** Present findings to board using culture-health-report template

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Competing Values Framework:** Assess culture across clan, adhocracy, market, and hierarchy dimensions
- **Edgar Schein's Three Levels:** Analyze culture at the artifact, espoused values, and underlying assumptions levels
- **Behavioral Indicators:** Ground all cultural assessment in observable behaviors, not aspirational statements

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment combines quantitative data and qualitative insights
- [ ] Multiple organizational levels and functions represented in data collection
- [ ] Findings distinguish between symptoms and root causes
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Success metrics defined with measurement plan
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (culture change takes quarters, not weeks)
- [ ] Leadership commitment secured before proceeding

## Output
- Culture Audit Report
- Gap analysis aspired vs lived culture
- Prioritized recommendations
- Intervention plan with metrics

## Handoffs
- Recommendations feed into culture-evolution-plan
- Leadership gaps feed into leadership-coaching
- Structural issues feed into org-design-review
- Values violations may require trust-repair-plan

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Assessment completion | Within agreed timeline | Calendar tracking |
| Employee participation | >70% for surveys, representative sample for qualitative | Participation rates |
| Recommendation adoption | >80% of recommendations actioned | Action tracking |
| Culture score improvement | Measurable improvement within 6-12 months | Pulse surveys |
| Board confidence in culture trajectory | >4/5 | Board survey |
