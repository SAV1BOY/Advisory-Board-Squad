# Task: Market Expansion Review

## Objective
Evaluate and plan expansion into a new market segment, geography, or customer tier. Ensure the board has full visibility into the opportunity, risks, and resource requirements.

## Trigger
- Annual planning identifies new market as a strategic priority
- Customer pull signals from an adjacent market reach critical mass
- Competitive pressure requires market expansion for defensibility
- Partnership opportunity opens access to a new market

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or VP Sales or Growth Lead
- **Support:** Product for market-product fit, Finance for unit economics, Legal for regulatory
- **Advisory:** Board members with relevant market experience


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → strategic-diagnosis`

- **Lead:** strategic-advisor (Peter Thiel)
- **Support:** growth-navigator (Naval Ravikant), partnership-broker (Reid Hoffman), risk-sentinel (Ray Dalio)
- **Review:** board-chair (Board Chair)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current market performance data available
- [ ] Target market research conducted
- [ ] Unit economics for current market understood
- [ ] Resource requirements estimated
- [ ] Legal and regulatory requirements identified

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Conduct market opportunity sizing with bottom-up methodology including TAM, SAM, SOM, and growth rates for the target market

2. **Step 2:** Assess market-product fit by evaluating how well the current product serves the new market and what adaptations are needed

3. **Step 3:** Model the unit economics for the new market including customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, and payback period

4. **Step 4:** Evaluate go-to-market options including direct sales, partnerships, channel, and self-serve with cost and timeline for each

5. **Step 5:** Assess competitive landscape in the target market identifying incumbents, their strengths, and our right to win

6. **Step 6:** Develop a phased expansion plan with stage gates and kill criteria

7. **Step 7:** Present to the board using the strategy-review-brief template with clear investment ask and expected returns

8. **Step 8:** Market Expansion Brief; Financial model for new market; Go-to-market plan; Phase-gated execution plan

## Frameworks to Apply
- **First Principles Analysis:** Break the problem down to fundamental truths before building solutions
- **Pre-Mortem:** Assume the initiative fails and work backward to identify likely causes
- **Reversibility Assessment:** Evaluate how reversible each option is — irreversible decisions deserve more scrutiny

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Analysis uses primary data sources, not assumptions
- [ ] Multiple options presented with quantified trade-offs
- [ ] Risks identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Success metrics defined with kill criteria
- [ ] Board has a clear decision or direction to give
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled

## Output
- Approved expansion feeds into capital-allocation-review
- Partnership needs feed into partner-eval
- Hiring needs feed into exec-hiring
