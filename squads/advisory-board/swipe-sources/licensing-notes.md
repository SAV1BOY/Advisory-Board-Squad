# Licensing and Attribution Notes

## Overview
This file documents the licensing status, attribution requirements, and fair use considerations for materials referenced in the Advisory Board Squad's reference and swipe files.

## General Principles

### Fair Use Doctrine
- Short quotations from published works for the purpose of commentary, criticism, education, and analysis generally qualify as fair use
- All quotations in these files are used for educational and commentary purposes
- Quotations are attributed to their original authors
- No full texts are reproduced; only brief excerpts that constitute a small fraction of the original works

### Attribution Standard
- Every quotation includes author attribution
- Book references include title and author
- Framework references include the originator's name when known
- When a framework has been adapted (not reproduced verbatim), this is noted

## Specific Licensing Notes

### Public Domain Materials
- Warren Buffett's shareholder letters: Published on Berkshire Hathaway's website for public access
- Jeff Bezos's shareholder letters: Published on Amazon's website for public access
- Howard Marks's memos: Published on Oaktree Capital's website for public access
- SEC filings (S-1 documents, annual reports): Public record
- Jamie Dimon's shareholder letters: Published in JPMorgan annual reports

### Published Books (Fair Use Excerpts)
- All book summaries and quotations constitute fair use commentary and criticism
- Summaries are original analyses, not reproductions of the works
- Frameworks are described based on public knowledge and original analysis
- Readers are encouraged to purchase and read the original works for full context

### Proprietary Frameworks
- Some frameworks referenced (e.g., Bridgewater's specific tools) may be proprietary
- Our references describe the concepts at a high level based on publicly available information
- For implementation of specific proprietary tools, readers should consult the original sources

## Recommended Actions

### For Commercial Use
- If using these reference materials in a commercial advisory context, ensure quotations remain within fair use boundaries
- For extensive use of any single author's work, consider obtaining permissions
- When in doubt, cite the source and provide a reference to the original work

### For Internal Use
- These materials are designed for internal advisory board use
- Distribution should be limited to the advisory board team and their clients
- Do not republish these materials publicly without additional licensing review

## Disclaimer
These reference materials are educational summaries and analyses. They do not substitute for reading the original works. All intellectual property belongs to the respective authors and publishers. The Advisory Board Squad encourages purchasing and reading the original sources.
