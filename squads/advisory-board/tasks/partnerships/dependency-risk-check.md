# Task: Dependency Risk Check

## Objective
Assess and monitor the company's dependency on key partners, vendors, platforms, and external relationships. Ensure no single dependency creates an existential or severe risk.

## Trigger
- Quarterly as part of the risk review cycle
- When a key partner or vendor experiences financial distress, leadership change, or strategic shift
- When revenue from a single partner exceeds 15% of total revenue
- When a platform dependency is identified that could disrupt operations
- Pre-fundraise dependency assessment for investor due diligence

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CFO or Strategy Lead
- **Support:** Product for technology dependencies, Sales for revenue dependencies, Engineering for infrastructure dependencies
- **Advisory:** Board members for risk assessment perspective

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current partner and vendor inventory
- [ ] Revenue breakdown by partner and channel
- [ ] Technology stack and infrastructure dependencies mapped
- [ ] Contractual terms for key relationships available

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Inventory all significant external dependencies across revenue, technology, data, distribution, and operational categories

2. **Step 2:** For each dependency assess concentration level, switching cost, time-to-replace, and impact of sudden loss

3. **Step 3:** Score each dependency on severity using the risk register methodology of likelihood times impact

4. **Step 4:** Identify dependencies that exceed risk appetite thresholds requiring mitigation

5. **Step 5:** Develop mitigation plans for high-severity dependencies including diversification strategies, contractual protections, and contingency plans

6. **Step 6:** Update the risk register with current dependency risk assessments

7. **Step 7:** Report to the board any dependencies that breach or approach threshold levels

8. **Step 8:** Dependency Risk Assessment Report; Updated risk register; Mitigation plans for high-severity dependencies; Dependency monitoring dashboard

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Partnership Health Scorecard:** Assess relationship health, value delivery, dependency balance, and strategic alignment
- **RACI for Partnerships:** Clear responsibility assignment across both organizations
- **Value Chain Analysis:** Map where each partner adds value and where dependencies exist

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Both parties' interests and value contribution are clearly documented
- [ ] Risks are identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled and understood
- [ ] Legal review completed for all material terms
- [ ] Exit strategy and costs are understood
- [ ] Governance structure ensures accountability without bureaucracy
- [ ] Board has appropriate visibility into material partnerships

## Output
- High-risk dependencies feed into risk-register-update
- Revenue concentration risks inform sales strategy diversification
- Technology dependencies inform product-strategy-review
- Contractual gaps feed into deal-terms-review renewals
