# Team Health Assessment Survey Template

## Purpose

This survey measures the health of a team across dimensions that predict sustained high performance. It should be conducted quarterly to identify emerging issues before they become crises and to track improvement over time.

## Administration Guidelines

- **Anonymity:** All responses are anonymous. Minimum team size of 5 for anonymity protection.
- **Frequency:** Quarterly recommended; monthly for teams undergoing significant change.
- **Scale:** 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) unless otherwise noted.
- **Open-ended questions:** Include at least 2-3 to capture nuance that scores miss.
- **Response target:** Aim for 80%+ participation for statistically meaningful results.

## Open-Ended Questions

1. **What is the one thing this team does best that we should protect?**
2. **What is the single biggest thing we could change to improve how this team works?**
3. **Is there anything important that leadership is not seeing or talking about?**

---

## Scoring and Interpretation

| Average Score | Health Rating | Recommended Action |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| 4.5 - 5.0 | Exceptional | Celebrate and maintain; share practices with other teams |
| 3.5 - 4.4 | Healthy | Address any section below 3.5 with targeted action |
| 2.5 - 3.4 | At Risk | Facilitate team discussion; develop improvement plan |
| Below 2.5 | Critical | Immediate leadership intervention; consider external facilitation |

**Trend matters more than absolute score.** A team at 3.8 and improving is healthier than a team at 4.2 and declining.

## Action Planning

After each survey:
1. Share aggregate results with the team within 1 week
2. Identify the 1-2 lowest-scoring areas for focused improvement
3. Co-create specific action items with the team (not top-down mandates)
4. Set measurable targets for next quarter's survey
5. Follow up on previous quarter's action items before introducing new ones
