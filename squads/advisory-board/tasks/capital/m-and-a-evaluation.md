# Task: M&A Evaluation

## Objective
Conduct a rigorous evaluation of a potential acquisition target to determine strategic fit, fair valuation, integration feasibility, and board recommendation. Prevent value-destroying acquisitions through disciplined analysis.

## Trigger
- M&A opportunity identified by management, board member, or banker
- Competitive dynamics make acquisition strategically urgent
- Build vs. buy analysis favors acquisition for a critical capability
- Inbound approach from a potential target

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Corp Dev Lead
- **Support:** CFO (valuation), CTO (technical DD), CHRO (people DD), Legal (legal DD)
- **Advisory:** Board members with M&A experience, external M&A advisor (if engaged)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] NDA signed with target (if discussions are bilateral)
- [ ] Initial information package received from target
- [ ] Strategic rationale articulated and validated
- [ ] Budget for external advisors approved (legal, financial, technical DD)
- [ ] Board aware of the opportunity (at minimum, Chair and relevant committee)

## Steps

1. **Strategic Screening (Week 1)**
   - Validate strategic rationale: does this acquisition address a real strategic need?
   - Complete build vs. buy analysis: is acquisition truly better than building internally?
   - Assess cultural compatibility at a high level
   - Go/no-go decision: proceed to due diligence or decline

2. **Due Diligence (Weeks 2-5)**
   - Financial DD: audit financials, verify revenue quality, assess liabilities, review projections
   - Technical DD: assess technology stack, code quality, scalability, security, technical debt
   - People DD: assess key talent, compensation, cultural fit, retention risk
   - Legal DD: contracts, IP ownership, litigation, regulatory compliance
   - Customer DD: customer concentration, satisfaction, churn risk, contract terms
   - Commercial DD: market validation, competitive position, growth assumptions

3. **Valuation (Weeks 3-5, parallel with DD)**
   - Apply multiple valuation methodologies: revenue multiple, EBITDA multiple, DCF, replacement cost, comparable transactions
   - Set the valuation range and walk-away price
   - Determine deal structure: cash, stock, earnout, retention, escrow
   - Model the acquisition impact on our financials: dilution, cash, P&L

4. **Integration Planning (Weeks 4-6)**
   - Develop integration plan across: technology, people, customers, brand, operations
   - Identify the integration lead and dedicate resources
   - Estimate integration cost (separate from acquisition price)
   - Identify key retention targets and design retention packages
   - Define Day 1, Day 30, Day 90 integration milestones

5. **Board Decision (Week 6-7)**
   - Prepare the M&A evaluation memo using the m-and-a-memo template
   - Present to the board: strategic rationale, DD findings, valuation, deal structure, integration plan, risks
   - Board deliberates and decides: proceed to LOI, decline, or request additional DD
   - If approved: board authorizes negotiation parameters

6. **Negotiation and Close (Weeks 7-14)**
   - Negotiate LOI/term sheet within board-authorized parameters
   - Finalize definitive agreements with legal counsel
   - Complete any remaining DD items
   - Board approves final terms and authorizes signing
   - Execute close and begin integration

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Strategic Fit Scorecard:** Score the target across product, technology, customer, team, culture, and revenue dimensions
- **Synergy Realism Check:** Discount projected synergies by 50-70% to account for typical overestimation
- **Integration Complexity Assessment:** Rate complexity across each integration dimension

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Build vs. buy analysis completed with honest assessment
- [ ] All DD workstreams completed (financial, technical, people, legal, customer, commercial)
- [ ] Multiple valuation methods applied with comparable transactions
- [ ] Walk-away price set before negotiations begin
- [ ] Integration plan and cost estimate developed
- [ ] Key retention targets identified with retention packages designed
- [ ] Maximum downside exposure quantified
- [ ] Board has full transparency into risks and DD findings

## Output
- M&A Evaluation Memo for the board
- Due diligence report (all workstreams)
- Valuation analysis
- Integration plan
- Deal structure recommendation
- Board decision (proceed/decline)

## Handoffs
- If approved: integration execution begins per integration plan
- Financial impact updates the runway model and budget
- People integration feeds into org-design-review and onboarding tasks
- Post-acquisition review scheduled at 90 days and 12 months

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| DD completion | All workstreams completed before board decision | Checklist |
| Valuation discipline | Final price within board-approved range | Price vs. authorization |
| Integration milestones | >80% of Day 90 milestones met | Milestone tracking |
| Key talent retention | >80% of identified key talent retained at 12 months | Retention tracking |
| Synergy realization | >50% of projected synergies realized at 24 months | Financial analysis |
