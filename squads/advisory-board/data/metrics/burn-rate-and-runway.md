# Burn Rate and Runway

## Definition

Burn rate measures the net cash consumed per month. Runway measures the number of months until cash reaches zero at the current burn rate. Together, they form the most critical survival metric for venture-backed companies. The board has a fiduciary responsibility to monitor these metrics and to ensure that management takes action when runway drops below safety thresholds. Burn rate should be measured on a cash basis (not accrual), as cash is what keeps the company alive.

## Formula

```
Gross Burn Rate = Total cash operating expenses per month (excluding one-time items)

Net Burn Rate = Gross Burn Rate - Monthly Cash Revenue
             = Net cash decrease per month

Runway (months) = Current Cash Balance / Net Burn Rate

Burn Multiple = Net Burn / Net New ARR
             (measures dollars burned per dollar of new ARR — lower is better)

Implied Runway at Fundraise = Months of runway remaining when fundraising begins
                             (should be 10-14 months to allow 4-6 months for process)
```

## Target Range

| Metric | Healthy | Caution | Critical |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| Runway (months) | >18 months | 10-18 months | <10 months |
| Net burn rate trend | Declining or stable | Increasing <10% MoM | Increasing >10% MoM |
| Burn multiple | <1.5x | 1.5-2.5x | >2.5x |
| Cash balance vs. plan | Within 10% of plan | 10-25% below plan | >25% below plan |

**Board action triggers:**
- **18 months runway:** Begin fundraising preparation (data room, investor targeting)
- **12 months runway:** Actively fundraising or implementing cost reduction plan
- **9 months runway:** Emergency board session required — reduce burn to extend to 15+ months or secure bridge financing
- **6 months runway:** Survival mode — explore all options including M&A, bridge, or wind-down

## Data Sources

- Bank statements and cash position (verified monthly by finance team)
- Accounts payable and receivable aging reports
- Payroll system (largest component of burn for most startups)
- Financial model with actuals-vs-plan variance analysis
- Revenue collection data (actual cash received, not invoiced revenue)

## Frequency

- **Cash position check:** Weekly (CFO/controller)
- **Burn rate and runway calculation:** Monthly (finance team)
- **Board reporting:** Monthly via written update; detailed review quarterly
- **Scenario modeling:** Quarterly — model runway under base, upside, and downside revenue scenarios
- **Emergency review:** Immediately upon any event that materially changes cash position or burn trajectory

## Owner

- **Primary:** CFO or VP Finance (or CEO if no finance executive)
- **Board sponsor:** Lead investor or board treasurer
- **Data steward:** Controller or FP&A lead
