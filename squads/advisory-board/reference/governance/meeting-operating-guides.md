# Meeting Operating Guides

## Pre-Meeting Preparation

### For the CEO/Chair
- Distribute board package 72 hours in advance (minimum 48 hours)
- Include: agenda, financial dashboard, management commentary, decision memos, consent agenda items
- Flag items requiring decision vs. discussion vs. information only
- Brief the board chair on sensitive topics before the meeting

### For Board Members
- Read all materials before the meeting. Take notes. Formulate questions.
- If you need clarification on data, contact the CFO or relevant executive before the meeting
- Identify the 2-3 most important issues and prepare your perspective

## Meeting Structure Template

### Opening (10 minutes)
- Call to order, quorum confirmation
- Approve previous meeting minutes (consent agenda)
- CEO sets context: the one thing that matters most since last meeting

### Consent Agenda (5 minutes)
- Routine approvals bundled: previous minutes, committee reports, standard resolutions
- Any member can pull an item from consent to full discussion
- Remaining items approved by single motion

### Financial Review (20-30 minutes)
- CFO presents: actuals vs. plan, cash position, key metrics
- Focus on variances and their causes, not restating the numbers
- Board questions on financial health and trajectory

### Strategic Discussion (60-90 minutes)
- The heart of the meeting. 1-2 topics maximum.
- Each topic: management presents context and recommendation (15 min), board discusses (30-45 min), decision or next steps (10 min)
- Use decision memos (written, pre-read) rather than slide decks
- Explicitly invite dissenting views before concluding

### Operational Updates (15-20 minutes)
- Product, sales, people, and technology highlights
- Focus on changes since last meeting, not comprehensive reviews
- Board members ask questions, provide input

### Executive Session (15-30 minutes)
- Board members only, no management present
- Discuss CEO performance, board dynamics, sensitive concerns
- Chair communicates relevant feedback to CEO after the session

### Closing (10 minutes)
- Summarize decisions made and action items assigned
- Confirm next meeting date and preliminary agenda topics
- Adjourn

## Meeting Facilitation Rules

- **The 2-minute rule:** No individual speaks for more than 2 minutes without a pause for others to respond
- **The question-first rule:** Board members should ask questions before making statements
- **The devil's advocate rule:** For every major decision, assign one member to argue against
- **The silence rule:** After a recommendation is presented, allow 60 seconds of silence before discussion begins
- **The parking lot:** Topics that arise but aren't on the agenda go to a "parking lot" for future meetings

## Between-Meeting Communication

- Monthly CEO update email: 1 page, key metrics, top issues, help needed
- Ad hoc communication: for time-sensitive issues that can't wait for the next meeting
- Committee meetings: as needed, focused on specific domains (audit, compensation, governance)
- Board portal: centralized document repository, meeting schedule, contact information

## Annual Meeting Calendar

| Quarter | Focus Areas | Key Decisions |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Q1 | Annual strategy review, CEO evaluation | Strategy approval, CEO compensation |
| Q2 | Mid-year performance review, risk update | Budget adjustments, risk mitigation |
| Q3 | Succession planning, governance review | Board composition changes |
| Q4 | Annual planning, budget | Next-year budget, board slate |
