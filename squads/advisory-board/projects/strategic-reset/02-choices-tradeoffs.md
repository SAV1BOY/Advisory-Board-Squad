# Strategic Reset: Choices and Tradeoffs

## Objective
Generate distinct strategic options, evaluate each against the diagnosis, and make explicit choices including what the company will NOT do. This is the hardest phase because it requires committing to one path and explicitly rejecting attractive alternatives.

## Inputs
- Validated diagnosis (01-diagnosis.md).
- Market opportunity analysis.
- Capability assessment: what can we realistically execute.
- Financial constraints: what can we afford.
- Board and stakeholder input on acceptable risk levels.

## Process
1. Generate 4-6 distinct strategic directions. Each must include: where to play, how to win, and what we will NOT do.
2. Evaluate each direction using: scenario table (robustness across futures), EV calculator (expected value), incentive map (stakeholder alignment), pre-mortem (failure modes).
3. Assess resource requirements for each direction: people, capital, time, capabilities needed vs. available.
4. Identify the tradeoffs explicitly: what we gain and what we sacrifice with each choice.
5. Present options to the board with analysis and recommendation.
6. Make the choice using the appropriate decision method (consensus-vs-conviction-pattern.md).
7. Record dissent and define review triggers for the dissenting view.

## Output
- Strategic choice document: guiding policy, where to play, how to win, what we will NOT do, sequencing.
- Decision memo with full analysis, recommendation, dissent record, and kill criteria.
- Board-approved strategic direction.
