# Org Health Turnaround: Rituals

## Objective
Design, implement, or redesign organizational rituals that structurally reinforce the desired culture changes.

## Inputs
- Alignment priorities (02-alignment.md). Current ritual inventory. Culture-blocks.md Block 3.

## Process
1. Audit existing rituals: which reinforce desired culture, which are performative, which reinforce old culture?
2. Design 2-3 new rituals targeting the highest-priority cultural gaps.
3. Retire rituals that reinforce behaviors the turnaround is trying to change.
4. Define success and decay signals for each ritual.
5. Assign ritual owners and launch schedule.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 3 (Ritual Design)

## Output
Ritual plan with new rituals, retired rituals, owners, and measurement criteria.
