# Emergency Board Session Protocol

## Purpose
Defines the process for convening an emergency board session when a crisis or urgent matter requires board-level decision-making outside the regular meeting cadence. Ensures rapid mobilization while maintaining governance standards.

---

## Activation Criteria

An emergency session may be convened when any of the following conditions are met:

- **Level 1 or Level 2 crisis** as defined in the crisis-brief template
- **Existential threat** to the organization's survival or independence
- **Material legal action** — lawsuit, regulatory investigation, or enforcement action requiring immediate board direction
- **Executive misconduct** or sudden departure of CEO or other critical executive
- **Cybersecurity breach** with material data exposure or operational impact
- **M&A opportunity or hostile action** requiring time-sensitive response
- **Financial emergency** — liquidity crisis, covenant breach, fraud discovery
- **Reputational emergency** — imminent or active media crisis of material scale

---

## Activation Authority

| Who Can Activate | Condition |
|---|---|
| Board Chair | Any activation criteria met — sole discretion |
| CEO | Any activation criteria met — must notify Chair simultaneously |
| Any two board members jointly | If Chair and CEO are unavailable or conflicted |
| General Counsel | Material legal action requiring immediate board awareness |

---

## Convening Process

### Step 1: Activation (Within 30 Minutes)

1. Activating party contacts the Board Secretary or Board Ops lead
2. Board Secretary initiates the emergency communication tree
3. All board members contacted via primary and secondary channels within 30 minutes
4. Target session time set: within 2-4 hours of activation for Level 1; within 24 hours for Level 2

### Step 3: Pre-Session Preparation (Concurrent with Convening)

1. Crisis brief prepared using the crisis-brief template (target: completed within 60 minutes)
2. Relevant supporting documents gathered (legal opinion, financial impact, incident report)
3. External advisors engaged as needed (legal counsel, PR firm, forensics)
4. Brief distributed to all board members via secure channel as soon as available

|---|---|
| Quorum | [N] of [N] board members ([%]) required for decisions |
| Reduced Quorum (Emergency) | [N] of [N] if full quorum cannot be achieved within the required timeframe — requires Chair approval |
| Voting | Simple majority for operational decisions; [specified majority] for material decisions (M&A, executive termination, >$[X] commitments) |
| Absent Member Ratification | Decisions made under reduced quorum must be ratified at the next full board meeting |
| Conflict Recusal | Any conflicted member must recuse; quorum adjusted accordingly |

## Emergency Session Agenda

| Item | Duration | Lead | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Call to Order and Quorum | 2 min | Chair | Confirm quorum and declare emergency session |
| 2. Situation Briefing | 10-15 min | Incident Commander / CEO | Present crisis brief — facts, impact, urgency |
| 3. Legal Assessment | 5-10 min | General Counsel | Legal obligations, constraints, liability exposure |
| 4. Clarifying Questions | 10 min | All | Ensure all members have the same understanding |
| 5. Options and Recommendation | 10 min | CEO / Incident Commander | Present decision options with trade-offs |
| 6. Board Discussion | 15-20 min | Chair (facilitates) | Structured debate on options |
| 7. Decision | 5 min | Chair | Formal vote or consensus decision |
| 8. Communication Plan | 5 min | Communications Lead | Agree on internal and external messaging |
| 9. Next Steps and Follow-Up | 5 min | Chair | Action items, next update cadence, next session if needed |
| **Total** | **~70-80 min** | | |

---

## Documentation Requirements

All emergency sessions must be documented with the following, regardless of urgency:

- [ ] **Attendance record** — who was present, who was absent, who was recused
- [ ] **Crisis brief** — the document presented to the board
- [ ] **Decision record** — exact decision made, voting record, any dissents
- [ ] **Action items** — owner, description, deadline
- [ ] **Communication approvals** — approved messaging for internal and external use
- [ ] **Minutes** — drafted within 24 hours, approved at next regular meeting

---

## Post-Emergency Session

| Action | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Distribute decision record to all board members | Board Secretary | Within 4 hours |
| Execute approved communication plan | Communications Lead | Per agreed timeline |
| File incident documentation securely | Board Secretary | Within 24 hours |
| Schedule follow-up session (if needed) | Chair | Within 48 hours |
| Absent member briefing and ratification | Chair | Within 72 hours |
| Full post-mortem scheduled | CEO | Within 14 days |
| Board debrief on emergency process effectiveness | Chair | Within 30 days |

---

## Protocol Review

This protocol is reviewed and tested annually:
- [ ] Contact information verified quarterly
- [ ] Emergency bridge line tested semi-annually
- [ ] Tabletop exercise conducted annually
- [ ] Protocol updated based on lessons learned from any activation
- [ ] New board members briefed on this protocol during onboarding
