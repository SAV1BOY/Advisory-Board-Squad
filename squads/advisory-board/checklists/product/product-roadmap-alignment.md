# Product Roadmap Alignment Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the product roadmap is aligned with company strategy, customer needs, and resource reality. A misaligned roadmap builds things that do not serve the strategy, satisfy customers, or fit the budget. This checklist prevents roadmap drift and ensures every roadmap item can justify its place.

## When to Use
- During quarterly product planning.
- When evaluating roadmap changes or additions.
- When engineering capacity is insufficient for the planned roadmap.
- When stakeholders complain about roadmap priorities.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Every roadmap item maps to a specific strategic priority or customer need with documented rationale.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Roadmap items are prioritized using a consistent framework (RICE, weighted scoring, or equivalent).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The roadmap is feasible given current engineering capacity and timeline.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Customer-facing items are validated with customer evidence, not just internal opinion.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Technical debt and infrastructure items receive appropriate allocation (minimum 20%).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The roadmap includes explicit NOT-doing items: what was considered and deprioritized.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Dependencies between roadmap items are identified and sequenced.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The roadmap has a clear 90-day committed plan and a directional 12-month view.

## Red Flags
- The roadmap changes every sprint based on the loudest voice.
- No roadmap items are informed by direct customer research.
- Technical debt is never addressed because customer features always win.
- The roadmap is a wish list with no connection to capacity.
- Sales drives the roadmap by promising unreleased features to close deals.

## Escalation Triggers
- Roadmap commitments are missed by more than 30% in a quarter.
- Key customers churn citing product deficiencies that were deprioritized on the roadmap.
- Engineering capacity is consumed by unplanned work for more than 40% of the quarter.
- The roadmap diverges from company strategy without board awareness.
