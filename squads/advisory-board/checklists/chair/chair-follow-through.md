# Chair Follow-Through Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that decisions made by the advisory board are tracked to completion and that the board maintains accountability for follow-through. The gap between decision and implementation is where most governance value is lost. This checklist prevents the pattern of boards that make great decisions that are never executed.

## When to Use
- At the start of every board meeting when reviewing previous action items.
- Monthly when the chair reviews the status of outstanding commitments.
- When a pattern of incomplete follow-through is identified.
- During board effectiveness reviews.

## Gate Criteria

### Action Item Tracking
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Every board decision generates explicit action items with owners and deadlines.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A centralized action item tracker is maintained and accessible to all board members.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Action items are reviewed at the beginning of every board meeting.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Status updates on action items are provided between meetings for high-priority items.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Overdue items are flagged with escalation plans.

### Accountability Enforcement
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Action item owners report on their progress, not the chair or secretary.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Incomplete items are addressed directly: what is the obstacle and what help is needed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Chronic non-completion by the same owner is addressed as a performance issue.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The board holds itself accountable for its own commitments, not just management's.

### Decision-to-Outcome Tracking
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Major board decisions are tracked from decision through implementation to outcome.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The expected outcome is compared to the actual outcome for significant decisions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Lessons learned from decision outcomes are captured and used to improve future decisions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The decision log records both the decision and its subsequent outcome.

### Systemic Follow-Through
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization's follow-through rate (completed items / total items) is tracked.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Root causes of systematic non-completion are diagnosed (resource constraints, unclear ownership, changing priorities).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Process improvements are implemented to address root causes of non-completion.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Follow-through quality is part of the annual board effectiveness assessment.

## Red Flags
- Action items from previous meetings are not reviewed at the current meeting.
- The same action items appear on the tracker for more than 90 days without progress.
- No one can locate the action item tracker or it is not maintained.
- Action items are vague ("look into X") rather than specific ("deliver analysis of X by date Y").
- The board makes decisions but never checks whether they were implemented.
- Management presents new proposals while previous commitments remain unfulfilled.

## Escalation Triggers
- Follow-through rate drops below 70% for two consecutive review periods.
- A critical board decision is not implemented and the delay causes material harm.
- An action item owner fails to complete commitments for three consecutive meetings.
- The gap between board decisions and management action widens, suggesting governance disconnection.
- The board itself does not fulfill its own commitments (due diligence, introductions, feedback).
