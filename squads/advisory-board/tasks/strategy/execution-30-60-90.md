# Task: Execution 30-60-90 Day Plan

## Objective
Translate a board-approved strategic decision into a structured 30-60-90 day execution plan with clear milestones, owners, and checkpoints that the board can monitor.

## Trigger
- After any major strategic decision is approved by the board
- After a pivot decision
- When a new executive joins and needs to operationalize their mandate
- After the annual planning session sets new strategic priorities

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Execution Owner (whoever the board assigned to implement the decision)
- **Support:** Functional leaders contributing to execution, Board Ops (tracking)
- **Oversight:** CEO, Board sponsor for the initiative

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Board decision documented in decision log with clear parameters
- [ ] Budget and resources approved
- [ ] Execution owner identified and committed
- [ ] Success metrics and kill criteria defined during board approval

## Steps

1. **Plan Development (Days 1-3)**
   - Break the strategic initiative into discrete workstreams
   - For each workstream: define the owner, key activities, dependencies, and deliverables
   - Map the critical path: which activities must happen sequentially and which can be parallel
   - Identify the top 5 risks to execution and mitigation plans for each
   - Define the 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day milestones with measurable criteria

2. **Day 1-30 Plan: Foundation**
   - Quick wins: identify 2-3 visible early wins that build momentum and credibility
   - Team assembly: confirm all required people are assigned and available
   - Infrastructure: set up tools, processes, and communication channels for the initiative
   - Stakeholder alignment: brief all affected teams and gather input
   - Baseline measurement: establish current state for all success metrics
   - Gate criteria: what must be true at Day 30 to continue?

3. **Day 31-60 Plan: Build**
   - Core execution: primary value-creating activities underway
   - Integration: connect the initiative to existing workflows and systems
   - Feedback loops: establish mechanisms for rapid learning and course correction
   - External dependencies: vendor engagements, partnerships, or customer interactions
   - Gate criteria: what must be true at Day 60 to continue?

4. **Day 61-90 Plan: Deliver and Validate**
   - Deliver the primary outcome or first major milestone
   - Measure results against the success metrics defined at board approval
   - Conduct retrospective: what worked, what did not, what we learned
   - Prepare board update with results, learnings, and forward plan
   - Gate criteria: has the initiative met the threshold to continue investment?

5. **Board Checkpoint (Day 90)**
   - Present 90-day results to the board
   - Compare actual results to predictions
   - Recommend: continue as planned, adjust, expand, or discontinue
   - If continuing: present the next 90-day plan

## Frameworks to Apply
- **SMART Milestones:** Every milestone must be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
- **RACI:** Clear role assignments for every workstream
- **Critical Path Analysis:** Identify the longest sequence of dependent tasks

## Checklists

### Plan Quality Gate
- [ ] Every milestone has a measurable success criterion
- [ ] Every workstream has a single owner
- [ ] Dependencies between workstreams are mapped
- [ ] Risks are identified with mitigation plans
- [ ] Kill criteria from board approval are incorporated into gate reviews
- [ ] Resource requirements are confirmed (not assumed)
- [ ] Communication plan exists for stakeholders

## Output
- 30-60-90 day execution plan document
- Weekly status reports to CEO
- Day 30 gate review report
- Day 60 gate review report
- Day 90 board update with results and forward recommendation

## Handoffs
- Day 90 results feed into the quarterly board review
- If initiative continues: next 90-day plan prepared
- Learnings documented and shared across squads
- Updated metrics feed into the board scorecard

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Plan completion by day 3 | 100% of milestones defined | Plan review |
| Milestone hit rate (30-day) | >80% of Day 30 milestones met | Milestone tracking |
| Milestone hit rate (60-day) | >80% of Day 60 milestones met | Milestone tracking |
| Milestone hit rate (90-day) | >70% of Day 90 milestones met | Milestone tracking |
| Board reporting timeliness | Day 90 report delivered on time | Calendar tracking |
