# Chouinard Do No Harm Audit Checklist

## Purpose
Provides a systematic assessment of whether the organization's operations cause unnecessary harm to people, communities, or the environment. Chouinard's "do no unnecessary harm" principle acknowledges that all business creates some impact but demands that harm is minimized, acknowledged, and actively mitigated. This audit is not about perfection; it is about honest assessment and continuous reduction of negative impact.

## When to Use
- Annually as a comprehensive impact audit.
- Before launching products, entering markets, or changing operations that could increase harm.
- When stakeholders raise concerns about the organization's impact.
- During supply chain reviews and vendor selection.
- When regulatory requirements change and compliance needs reassessment.

## Gate Criteria

### Environmental Impact
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Carbon footprint is measured and reduction targets are set.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Waste generation is tracked and reduction or recycling programs are in place.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Water usage and pollution impact are assessed where relevant.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Supply chain environmental impact is assessed, not just direct operations.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization has a plan for year-over-year environmental impact reduction.

### Human Impact
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Labor practices across the supply chain are assessed for fairness and safety.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Employee wellbeing (physical, mental, financial) is monitored and supported.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Community impact of operations (positive and negative) is assessed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Product safety and customer wellbeing are prioritized in design and operations.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Diversity, equity, and inclusion impacts are measured and improved.

### Economic Impact
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization pays fair wages across its operations and supply chain.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Economic impact on local communities (employment, tax contribution, local sourcing) is assessed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization does not externalize costs onto communities or future generations.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Pricing practices are fair and do not exploit vulnerable customers.

### Governance and Transparency
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Harm assessment findings are reported transparently to stakeholders.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: An independent review of impact data is conducted periodically.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Corrective actions from previous audits are tracked to completion.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization publicly acknowledges areas where it causes harm and what it is doing about it.

## Red Flags
- The organization has never conducted a comprehensive harm assessment.
- Known sources of harm are tolerated because addressing them is costly or inconvenient.
- Impact data is collected but not shared with stakeholders.
- The supply chain has not been audited for labor or environmental practices.
- The organization lobbies against regulations designed to reduce harm in its industry.
- "Doing less harm" is used to avoid the harder work of "doing good."

## Escalation Triggers
- A regulatory investigation is opened related to the organization's impact.
- A credible third party (NGO, journalist, whistleblower) documents harm the organization failed to identify.
- Environmental or social impact worsens year over year without a corrective plan.
- A product safety issue causes customer harm.
- Employee health or safety incidents exceed industry benchmarks.
