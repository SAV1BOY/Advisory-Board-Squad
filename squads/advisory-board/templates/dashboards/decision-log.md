## ID Convention
- Format: `D-[YEAR]-[SEQUENTIAL NUMBER]`
- Example: D-2026-001, D-2026-042
- IDs are never reused
- Reset annually on January 1
- Async decisions use the date the decision was confirmed, not the date discussion started

## Archival Policy
- Active decisions remain in the main log
- Decisions older than 24 months with status "Superseded," "Reversed," or "Completed" are moved to the archive section
- All decisions are retained indefinitely — nothing is deleted from the log
