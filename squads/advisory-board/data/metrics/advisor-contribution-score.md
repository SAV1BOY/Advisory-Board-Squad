# Advisor Contribution Score

## Definition

A metric measuring the tangible value each advisory board member contributes across key dimensions: strategic insight quality, network leverage, time commitment, follow-through on commitments, and impact on company outcomes. This score enables the board to objectively assess whether its composition is delivering value commensurate with the equity, compensation, and access provided to advisors. It also informs decisions about advisor renewals, seat additions, and composition changes.

## Formula

```
Advisor Contribution Score = (Strategic Input Quality x 0.30) + (Network Value x 0.20) + (Time & Availability x 0.20) + (Follow-Through x 0.15) + (Outcome Impact x 0.15)

Where:
- Strategic Input Quality (0-100): Rated by CEO and fellow board members
    - Quality of questions raised in meetings (depth, relevance, challenge level)
    - Value of strategic advice provided between meetings
    - Pattern recognition and risk identification accuracy
- Network Value (0-100): Measured by introductions and connections
    - Number of actionable introductions made (customer, talent, investor, partner)
    - Conversion rate of introductions to meaningful outcomes
    - Reputation lift from association
- Time & Availability (0-100): Measured by engagement
    - Meeting attendance rate (target: 90%+)
    - Responsiveness to between-meeting requests (<48 hours)
    - Pre-read completion (arrives prepared)
- Follow-Through (0-100): Measured by commitment completion
    - % of committed actions completed by agreed deadline
    - Quality of deliverables when commitments involve work product
- Outcome Impact (0-100): Measured by attributable results
    - Revenue influenced by advisor introductions or advice
    - Hires made through advisor referrals
    - Strategic decisions materially shaped by advisor input
```

## Target Range

| Rating | Score | Action |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Exceptional | 80-100 | Retain, deepen engagement, consider expanding scope |
| Strong | 60-79 | Retain; acknowledge contributions; address any gaps |
| Adequate | 40-59 | Candid conversation about expectations and engagement level |
| Underperforming | 20-39 | Improvement plan with 90-day review; consider transition |
| Disengaged | 0-19 | Initiate respectful off-boarding conversation |

**Portfolio target: Average advisor score above 60. No more than one advisor below 40 at any given time.**

## Data Sources

- Board meeting attendance records
- Action item tracker (commitments made and completed)
- CEO and management team feedback (structured quarterly input)
- Peer advisor feedback (anonymous annual assessment)
- CRM data (introductions tracked and outcome-linked)
- Decision registry (decisions where advisor input was material)

## Frequency

- **Informal assessment:** Quarterly (CEO reflection)
- **Structured score calculation:** Semi-annually
- **Board-level discussion:** Annually — present aggregate scores (anonymized) and composition recommendations
- **Individual feedback:** Annually — board chair provides private feedback to each advisor

## Owner

- **Primary:** Board Chair or Lead Independent Director
- **Assessment facilitator:** CEO (provides management perspective on advisor value)
- **Data steward:** Board Secretary or Chief of Staff
