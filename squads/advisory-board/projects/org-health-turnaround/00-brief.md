# Org Health Turnaround: Brief

## Objective
Define the scope and urgency of an organizational health turnaround initiative triggered by declining culture health scores, rising attrition, or leadership dysfunction.

## Inputs
- Culture health assessment showing decline (below 2.5 or two consecutive quarters of decline).
- Attrition data, engagement survey results, exit interview themes.
- Board or leadership mandate for intervention.

## Process
1. Document the trigger: what specific evidence prompted this initiative?
2. Scope the turnaround: is this organization-wide or localized to specific teams?
3. Assess urgency: how quickly is the situation deteriorating?
4. Identify the turnaround leader and team.
5. Set timeline and success criteria.
6. Secure board commitment and resources.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/utilities/culture-health-rubric.md`, `lib/components/culture-blocks.md`

## Output
Turnaround brief with scope, urgency assessment, team, timeline, and success criteria.
