# Innovation Portfolio Balance Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the company maintains a balanced portfolio of innovation investments across the risk/return spectrum. Prevents both over-concentration in incremental improvements (stagnation) and over-investment in moonshots (cash drain). Based on the "three horizons" framework adapted for board governance.

## Portfolio Allocation Gates

### Horizon 1: Core Innovation (Target: 60-70% of innovation budget)
- [ ] Incremental improvements to existing products and services are funded and staffed
- [ ] Innovation investments in H1 have clear revenue impact within 0-12 months
- [ ] Each H1 initiative has defined KPIs tied to customer satisfaction, efficiency, or revenue growth
- [ ] H1 projects are not consuming so much budget that H2 and H3 are starved

### Horizon 2: Adjacent Innovation (Target: 20-30% of innovation budget)
- [ ] The company is investing in expanding into adjacent markets, customer segments, or capabilities
- [ ] H2 initiatives have a 12-36 month expected impact timeline
- [ ] Each H2 initiative has a clear hypothesis and measurable validation milestones
- [ ] H2 projects have dedicated teams (not side projects from H1 teams)
- [ ] At least one H2 initiative is showing early traction signals

### Horizon 3: Transformational Innovation (Target: 5-15% of innovation budget)
- [ ] The company is investing in exploratory bets that could create entirely new businesses or categories
- [ ] H3 initiatives have a 36+ month expected impact timeline
- [ ] H3 investments are sized for optionality: small bets with asymmetric upside
- [ ] H3 projects are protected from short-term performance pressure
- [ ] The company has a process for graduating H3 experiments to H2 (or killing them)

## Portfolio Health Checks

### Balance Assessment
- [ ] Current allocation percentages are documented and reviewed quarterly
- [ ] No single horizon is receiving less than its minimum target allocation
- [ ] Innovation budget is not being raided to cover operational shortfalls
- [ ] Portfolio includes both technology innovation and business model innovation

### Pipeline Quality
- [ ] There are at least 3 active H2 initiatives in development
- [ ] There is at least 1 active H3 exploration underway
- [ ] Failed experiments are being studied for lessons, not punished
- [ ] There is a clear process for evaluating and killing underperforming initiatives

### Organizational Capability
- [ ] Innovation teams have the autonomy and resources to operate at appropriate speed
- [ ] There is a clear owner (person or team) for innovation portfolio management
- [ ] Innovation metrics are tracked separately from operational KPIs
- [ ] The company has mechanisms for internal ideas to surface (hackathons, idea boards, 20% time, innovation sprints)

### Competitive Context
- [ ] The company monitors competitor innovation activity and adjusts portfolio accordingly
- [ ] Disruptive threats from adjacent industries or new entrants are identified and tracked
- [ ] The innovation portfolio addresses at least one identified disruptive threat

## Pass/Fail Criteria

**Pass:** All three horizons are funded at or near target allocation. At least 3 H2 and 1 H3 initiatives are active. Failed experiments are documented with lessons. Innovation budget is protected from operational raiding.

**Fail (any one triggers):**
- Any horizon receives 0% allocation for two consecutive quarters
- Innovation budget is cut more than 30% to cover operational shortfalls without board approval
- No H3 experiments have been initiated in the past 12 months
- All innovation investment is concentrated in incremental improvements to existing products
- There is no documented process for evaluating, graduating, or killing innovation initiatives

## Board Application
- Review innovation portfolio balance at least semi-annually
- Ask: "What are we not investing in that a disruptive competitor might be?"
- Protect H3 budgets explicitly — they are the first to be cut and the most important for long-term survival
- Evaluate innovation leaders on portfolio learning rate, not just success rate
