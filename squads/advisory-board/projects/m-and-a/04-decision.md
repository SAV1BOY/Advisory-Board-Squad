# M&A: Decision

## Objective
Make the final go/no-go decision on the acquisition with full information, structured analysis, and captured dissent.

## Inputs
- Complete diligence report (02-diligence.md). Negotiated terms (03-terms.md). Financial models. Board input.

## Process
1. Present the complete acquisition case to the board: strategic rationale, financial analysis, diligence findings, terms, and risks.
2. Conduct a pre-mortem: it is 18 months post-acquisition and it has failed. Why?
3. Evaluate the deal against kill criteria: any diligence findings that should stop the deal?
4. Solicit dissent and record all objections with specificity.
5. Make the decision using the appropriate method (typically consensus for irreversible decisions of this magnitude).
6. If approved: authorize signing and set integration timeline.
7. If rejected: document rationale and lessons for future M&A evaluation.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/patterns/pre-mortem-pattern.md`, `lib/patterns/dissent-and-commit-pattern.md`
- `lib/utilities/decision-quality-rubric.md` (score the decision process)
- `data/registries/decision-registry.yaml`

## Output
Board decision record with rationale and dissent. Signing authorization (if approved). Decision registry entry.
