# Growth Sustainability Check Checklist

## Purpose
Evaluates whether current growth is sustainable or if it depends on one-time factors, unsustainable spending, or market conditions that will not persist. Sustainable growth creates compounding value; unsustainable growth creates future crises. This checklist distinguishes between the two.

## When to Use
- During quarterly growth reviews.
- When growth rates change significantly (acceleration or deceleration).
- Before committing additional resources to growth.
- When investors or the board question growth quality.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Organic growth rate is tracked separately from acquisition-driven or promotion-driven growth.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Customer retention rate supports the growth trajectory (growth is not just replacing churned customers).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Unit economics remain positive at the current and projected growth rate.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Growth does not depend on unsustainable levels of spending (marketing, discounting).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Customer quality (engagement, LTV, satisfaction) is stable or improving alongside growth.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team and infrastructure can support projected growth without degradation.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Growth sources are diversified (not dependent on a single channel, customer, or geography).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The competitive landscape supports continued growth (the market is not approaching saturation).

## Red Flags
- Growth is funded entirely by burning cash with no path to organic sustainability.
- Customer churn is increasing at the same rate as customer acquisition.
- Growth depends on a single marketing channel or customer.
- Team burnout is increasing as growth demands exceed capacity.

## Escalation Triggers
- Growth decelerates for two consecutive quarters with no diagnosed cause.
- Unit economics become negative as growth accelerates.
- The primary growth channel deteriorates and no alternative is ready.
- Customer satisfaction declines alongside growth, indicating quality degradation.
