# Task: Cross-Squad Sync

## Objective
Coordinate between the Advisory Board Squad and other operating squads to ensure board guidance reaches operating teams and operating intelligence reaches the board.

## Trigger
- Bi-weekly or after every board meeting
- When board decisions require cross-squad coordination
- When operating squads surface issues needing board input
- When a board action item requires input from multiple squads

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Ops or CEO
- **Support:** Squad leads from all operating squads
- **Advisory:** Board Chair if board-level coordination is needed


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → cross-squad-sync`

- **Lead:** board-chair (Board Chair)
- **Support:** governance-clerk (Simon Sinek), strategic-advisor (Peter Thiel), growth-navigator (Naval Ravikant)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Board meeting decisions and action items documented
- [ ] Squad status updates available
- [ ] Cross-squad dependencies mapped
- [ ] Escalation criteria defined

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Review board decisions from the most recent meeting and identify items requiring cross-squad awareness or action

2. **Step 2:** Collect input from operating squads on topics that may need board visibility or guidance

3. **Step 3:** Identify and resolve cross-squad dependencies related to board initiatives

4. **Step 4:** Flag any operating squad issues that are trending toward board-level escalation

5. **Step 5:** Align on the information flow ensuring board materials incorporate operating reality and operating teams receive board context

6. **Step 6:** Document cross-squad action items with owners and deadlines

7. **Step 7:** Cross-Squad Sync Notes; Updated cross-squad action items; Escalation flags for board attention; Input items for next board meeting agenda

8. **Step 8:** Operating intelligence feeds into board meeting preparation; Board decisions cascade to squad operations; Escalation items feed into board agenda; Cross-squad coordination feeds into board scorecard updates

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Operational Excellence:** Standardized processes with defined cadence and quality standards
- **Continuous Improvement:** Each cycle identifies and implements at least one process improvement
- **Single Source of Truth:** Every governance artifact has one canonical location and one owner
