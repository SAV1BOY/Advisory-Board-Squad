# Task: Executive Performance Review

## Objective
Conduct rigorous performance reviews for all executives using a structured framework that assesses results, leadership, culture stewardship, and strategic contribution.

## Trigger
- Quarterly for lightweight check-ins and annually for comprehensive reviews
- Triggered by performance concerns at any time
- Required before compensation decisions
- Part of succession planning assessment

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO for direct reports, Board Chair for CEO
- **Support:** CHRO for process design, 360 feedback coordination, and calibration facilitation
- **Advisory:** Board compensation committee for CEO review

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Performance period data collected
- [ ] OKR and KPI results finalized
- [ ] 360 feedback collected if applicable
- [ ] Self-assessment completed by executive
- [ ] Peer and stakeholder input gathered

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Collect quantitative results data comparing actual performance against objectives set at period start

2. **Step 2:** Gather qualitative input from peers, direct reports, and cross-functional stakeholders

3. **Step 3:** Executive completes self-assessment using the performance-review-template

4. **Step 4:** Reviewer prepares assessment across all dimensions including results, competencies, culture, and collaboration

5. **Step 5:** Conduct calibration session with CEO and CHRO to ensure consistency across all executive reviews

6. **Step 6:** Deliver review in a face-to-face conversation focusing on specific examples and development

7. **Step 7:** Set objectives for the next period with measurable key results and development goals

8. **Step 8:** Completed performance review document; Calibrated ratings for all executives; Development plans updated; Next period objectives set; Compensation recommendations if applicable

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Structured Assessment:** Use consistent frameworks and rubrics to reduce bias and increase rigor
- **Data-Driven People Decisions:** Ground all people decisions in observable evidence, not gut feeling
- **Stakeholder Triangulation:** Gather input from multiple perspectives (manager, peers, reports, cross-functional) for balanced assessment

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment is grounded in specific, observable evidence
- [ ] Multiple perspectives gathered (not single-source input)
- [ ] Outcomes and recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Confidentiality maintained throughout the process
- [ ] Legal and compliance requirements met
- [ ] Timeline and owners assigned for all actions

## Output
- Ratings inform compensation decisions
- Development gaps feed into leadership-coaching
- Underperformance may trigger performance improvement or succession planning
- Outstanding performance informs succession readiness
