# Task: Stakeholder Communication Review

## Objective
Assess the quality, cadence, and effectiveness of the company's communications to all stakeholders including investors, customers, employees, partners, and the public. Identify gaps and improve the communication strategy.

## Trigger
- Semi-annually as part of governance review
- After receiving feedback that stakeholders feel uninformed or surprised
- Before a major milestone such as fundraise, launch, or acquisition
- When stakeholder relationships show strain
- After a communication failure during a crisis

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Communications Lead
- **Support:** CFO for investor communications, CHRO for employee communications, VP Sales for customer communications
- **Advisory:** Board members for investor communication perspective

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Stakeholder map and communication inventory available
- [ ] Feedback from stakeholders collected
- [ ] Communication materials from the review period available
- [ ] Crisis communication post-mortems if applicable

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Inventory all stakeholder communication touchpoints including frequency, channel, content type, and owner for each stakeholder group

2. **Step 2:** Assess communication quality against standards of timeliness, transparency, accuracy, consistency, and audience-appropriateness

3. **Step 3:** Gather stakeholder feedback through surveys, interviews, or proxy indicators such as investor questions, employee engagement, customer NPS

4. **Step 4:** Identify communication gaps where stakeholders are surprised by information they should have received proactively

5. **Step 5:** Evaluate crisis communication readiness including prepared holding statements, spokesperson designation, and channel activation procedures

6. **Step 6:** Develop recommendations for communication improvements organized by stakeholder group with specific actions and owners

7. **Step 7:** Present findings to the board with emphasis on investor and board communication effectiveness

8. **Step 8:** Communication Review Report; Stakeholder communication map; Gap analysis; Improvement recommendations; Updated communication calendar

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Retrospective Analysis:** Structured look-back that separates process quality from outcome quality
- **Continuous Improvement Cycle:** Plan-Do-Check-Act applied to governance and management processes
- **Balanced Scorecard:** Multi-dimensional assessment that prevents over-indexing on any single metric

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Review is evidence-based with specific data points, not impressions
- [ ] Both positive and negative findings are presented honestly
- [ ] Root causes identified, not just symptoms
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Historical comparison included to show trends
- [ ] Board has clear insight into what needs to change

## Output
- Investor communication improvements feed into stakeholder-update-report template
- Employee communication feeds into culture-health-tracker
- Customer communication feeds into customer success strategy
- Crisis communication readiness feeds into crisis-response
