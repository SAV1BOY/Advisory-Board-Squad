# Task: Values Integrity Check

## Objective
Verify that the company's stated values are consistently reflected in decisions, behaviors, policies, and incentives. Identify hypocrisy gaps where stated values conflict with actual practices.

## Trigger
- Annually as part of the culture audit
- When a values violation is reported or suspected
- After a decision that appears to conflict with stated values
- When new values are adopted or existing values are revised
- When employee feedback highlights values-behavior disconnect

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or CHRO
- **Support:** Functional leaders, People team, legal counsel
- **Advisory:** Board members as values stewards

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Company values clearly documented and communicated
- [ ] Recent decision log available for analysis
- [ ] Employee feedback data available
- [ ] Policy documents current

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Review the stated values and their behavioral definitions ensuring they are specific enough to be observable

2. **Step 2:** Audit recent major decisions against values checking whether values were actually considered in the decision process or only referenced retroactively

3. **Step 3:** Survey employees on values alignment asking for specific examples of values lived and values violated

4. **Step 4:** Review HR policies, incentive structures, and promotion criteria for alignment with stated values

5. **Step 5:** Identify hypocrisy gaps where the organization says one thing but rewards another

6. **Step 6:** Develop recommendations to close gaps including policy changes, incentive realignment, leadership behavior changes, and communication improvements

7. **Step 7:** Present findings to the executive team and board with specific actions

8. **Step 8:** Values Integrity Report; Hypocrisy gap analysis; Recommendations for policy and incentive alignment; Updated values behavioral definitions if needed

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Competing Values Framework:** Assess culture across clan, adhocracy, market, and hierarchy dimensions
- **Edgar Schein's Three Levels:** Analyze culture at the artifact, espoused values, and underlying assumptions levels
- **Behavioral Indicators:** Ground all cultural assessment in observable behaviors, not aspirational statements

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment combines quantitative data and qualitative insights
- [ ] Multiple organizational levels and functions represented in data collection
- [ ] Findings distinguish between symptoms and root causes
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Success metrics defined with measurement plan
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (culture change takes quarters, not weeks)
- [ ] Leadership commitment secured before proceeding

## Output
- Gap remediation feeds into culture-evolution-plan
- Policy changes feed into relevant operating processes
- Leadership gaps feed into leadership-coaching
- Significant violations may trigger trust-repair-plan
