## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Winner's curse:** The excitement of winning a deal leads to overpayment. The walk-away price must be set before negotiations heat up.
2. **Synergy fantasy:** Most acquirers overestimate synergies by 50-70%. Discount projected synergies aggressively and do not pay for unproven synergies.
3. **Integration neglect:** 70% of acquisitions fail to deliver expected value, primarily due to poor integration. The integration plan should be as rigorous as the valuation.
4. **Cultural blindness:** Technology and customers can be integrated. Culture cannot be forced. Assess cultural compatibility during DD, not after close.
5. **Distraction underestimation:** Even a small acquisition consumes 20-30% of CEO time for 3-6 months. Budget for this explicitly.
