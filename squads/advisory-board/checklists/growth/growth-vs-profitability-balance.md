# Growth vs Profitability Balance Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the organization maintains an intentional balance between investing in growth and generating profits. The growth-profitability tradeoff is one of the most important strategic decisions. This checklist prevents both excessive caution (under-investing in growth) and reckless growth (destroying value with every new customer).

## When to Use
- During annual budgeting and strategic planning.
- When investors or the board question the balance.
- When profitability targets are missed due to growth spending.
- When growth targets are missed due to profitability focus.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The target balance between growth and profitability is explicitly stated and approved by the board.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The Rule of 40 (revenue growth rate + profit margin >= 40%) or equivalent metric is tracked.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Growth spending is quantified separately from base operating expenses.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each growth investment has an expected return timeline.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization can articulate why it prioritizes growth or profitability at the current stage.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Break-even analysis shows a credible path to profitability at the planned growth rate.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The board receives regular reporting on the growth-profitability balance.

## Red Flags
- Growth is pursued at any cost with no profitability considerations.
- Profitability is achieved by starving growth investment.
- The balance has not been explicitly discussed or approved.
- The company cannot articulate when it expects to be profitable.

## Escalation Triggers
- Actual growth-profitability balance deviates from plan by more than 20%.
- The path to profitability extends beyond the capital runway.
- Growth spending increases without proportional growth results.
- The board and management disagree on the appropriate balance.
