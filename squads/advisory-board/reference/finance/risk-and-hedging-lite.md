# Risk and Hedging — Practical Guide

## Risk Categories

### Strategic Risk
- Market shifts, competitive disruption, technology obsolescence
- **Mitigation:** Scenario planning, diversification, innovation investment, monitoring leading indicators

### Operational Risk
- Process failures, system outages, supply chain disruption, key person loss
- **Mitigation:** Redundancy, documentation, cross-training, business continuity planning

### Financial Risk
- Cash flow shortfalls, currency exposure, interest rate changes, counterparty default
- **Mitigation:** Cash reserves, hedging instruments, diversified revenue streams, credit monitoring

### Compliance/Legal Risk
- Regulatory changes, litigation, data privacy violations, IP disputes
- **Mitigation:** Proactive compliance programs, legal counsel, regulatory monitoring, insurance

### Reputational Risk
- PR crises, customer trust erosion, employee scandals, social media events
- **Mitigation:** Crisis communication plans, values-driven culture, monitoring, rapid response capability

## Risk Assessment Framework

### Probability x Impact Matrix
| | Low Impact | Medium Impact | High Impact |
|---|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| High Probability | Monitor | Mitigate | Urgent |
| Medium Probability | Accept | Mitigate | Plan |
| Low Probability | Accept | Monitor | Contingency plan |

### The Risk Register
For each identified risk:
1. **Description** — What could happen?
2. **Probability** — How likely (1-5)?
3. **Impact** — How severe (1-5)?
4. **Risk score** — Probability x Impact
5. **Current mitigation** — What are we doing about it now?
6. **Additional mitigation** — What more could we do?
7. **Owner** — Who is responsible for monitoring and responding?
8. **Trigger** — What would indicate this risk is materializing?

## Hedging Concepts for Non-Financial Companies

### Currency Hedging
- If you earn revenue in one currency and pay costs in another, currency fluctuations are a real risk
- **Natural hedge:** Match revenue and cost currencies where possible
- **Forward contracts:** Lock in exchange rates for future transactions
- **When to hedge:** When currency exposure > 10% of revenue and volatility is significant

### Revenue Concentration Risk
- If one customer > 20% of revenue, that's a concentration risk
- If one channel > 50% of revenue, that's a platform risk
- **Hedge:** Diversify deliberately; set concentration limits and act when breached

### Key Person Risk
- Identify the 3-5 people whose departure would cause the most damage
- **Hedge:** Retention packages, documentation of institutional knowledge, succession planning, cross-training

## Board Application
- Review the risk register quarterly; update probability and impact assessments
- Ensure each material risk has an owner and a mitigation plan
- Ask: what risks are NOT on the register? The most dangerous risks are the ones not yet identified
- Conduct an annual "black swan" exercise: what could happen that would fundamentally change the business?
