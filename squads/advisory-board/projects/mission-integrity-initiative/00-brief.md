# Mission Integrity Initiative: Brief

## Objective
Define the scope of an initiative to assess and strengthen the alignment between the company's stated mission and its actual practices, decisions, and culture.

## Inputs
- Company mission and values documentation. Culture health assessment. Customer and employee feedback indicating mission drift. Board or leadership concern about integrity gaps.

## Process
1. Define the trigger: what raised concerns about mission integrity?
2. Scope the assessment: full organizational audit or targeted review?
3. Define the assessment criteria: what does mission integrity look like in practice?
4. Identify the assessment team (include external perspective).
5. Set timeline and deliverables.
6. Secure leadership commitment to act on findings.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 1 (Culture Definition) and Block 2 (Health Assessment)
- `authority/agent-summaries/chouinard-mission-summary.md` (mission-driven business reference)

## Output
Mission integrity initiative brief with scope, criteria, team, and timeline.
