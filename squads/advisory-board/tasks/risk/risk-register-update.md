# Task: Risk Register Update

## Objective
Maintain the company's risk register as a living document. Add new risks, update existing risk assessments, close resolved risks, and ensure the board has current visibility into the company's risk posture.

## Trigger
- Monthly for operational updates
- Quarterly for board-level reporting
- Immediately when a new material risk is identified
- After any risk event materializes
- When a risk assessment changes significantly up or down

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CFO or Risk Lead
- **Support:** All functional leaders for their domain risks, Legal for compliance risks, CTO for technology risks
- **Advisory:** Board finance or risk committee

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current risk register available
- [ ] Functional leaders briefed on update process
- [ ] New risk identification inputs collected
- [ ] Materialized risk events documented

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Solicit risk updates from all functional leaders covering new risks, changed assessments, and closed risks

2. **Step 2:** Review each existing risk entry for current accuracy of likelihood, impact, and mitigation status

3. **Step 3:** Add newly identified risks with full documentation including description, category, scoring, owner, and mitigation strategy

4. **Step 4:** Reassess risks where external conditions or internal circumstances have changed

5. **Step 5:** Close risks that have been fully mitigated or are no longer relevant with documented rationale

6. **Step 6:** Update the risk heat map and summary statistics

7. **Step 7:** For board reporting quarters prepare the risk register section of the quarterly board review highlighting material changes

8. **Step 8:** Risk Register Dashboard updated; New risks documented; Changed assessments explained; Closed risks archived; Board reporting package for quarterly meetings

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Risk Scoring Matrix:** Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5) for consistent risk quantification
- **Swiss Cheese Model:** Multiple layers of defense with the understanding that each layer has holes
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis:** Assume the worst outcome and work backward to identify causes and prevention

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All risk dimensions assessed (financial, operational, strategic, people, legal, technology, reputational)
- [ ] Risk scoring is consistent and calibrated across assessors
- [ ] Mitigation plans are specific with owners and timelines
- [ ] Board reporting is clear, concise, and highlights material changes
- [ ] Risk appetite thresholds are respected and breaches escalated
- [ ] Historical risk data preserved for trend analysis

## Output
- Material risk changes feed into board agenda via meeting-prep
- Critical risks feed into crisis-response preparedness
- Risk trends inform strategic-diagnosis
- Compliance risks feed into regulatory planning
- Insurance implications feed into procurement
