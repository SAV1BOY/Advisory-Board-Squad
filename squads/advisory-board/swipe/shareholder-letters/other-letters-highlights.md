# Other Notable Shareholder Letters — Highlights

## Reed Hastings (Netflix)

### On Culture
- "We don't seek to preserve our culture—we seek to improve it. Like all great companies, we strive to hire the best and we value integrity, excellence, respect, and collaboration."
- "The best thing you can do for employees is hire only 'A' players to work alongside them. It's a perk far better than foosball tables."

### On Strategy
- "Companies rarely die from moving too fast, and they frequently die from moving too slow."
- "Our hit ratio is too high right now—we need to take more risks."

## Tobi Lutke (Shopify)

### On Building
- "We're not building a company to flip. We're building something that will serve merchants for decades."
- "The measure of a great tool is whether it disappears into the workflow and lets the user focus on what matters to them."

### On Decision-Making
- "We operate on the principle that decisions should be made by the people closest to the relevant information."

## Mark Leonard (Constellation Software)

### On Capital Allocation
- "We believe in buying businesses at fair prices rather than building them from scratch."
- "Organic growth gets all the glory; disciplined acquisitions create all the value."
- "We track return on invested capital religiously. It is the single most important metric for a diversified acquirer."

### On Decentralization
- "We push decisions down to the operating group level. Headquarters exists to allocate capital and set standards, not to manage operations."

## Daniel Ek (Spotify)

### On Competition
- "In the long run, the best product wins. But in the short run, distribution wins."
- "We don't define success by what our competitors are doing. We define it by whether we're delivering the best experience for listeners and creators."

### On Long-Term Thinking
- "We optimize for the long term, even when it means short-term pain. We believe this will create more value for all our stakeholders."

## Patrick Collison (Stripe)

### On Building Infrastructure
- "The most impactful companies build infrastructure that enables others to build. They increase the GDP of the internet."
- "We think about Stripe on a 30-year time horizon. The internet is still young."

## Swipe Application
- Use Lutke's principle of pushing decisions to those closest to the information when advising on organizational design
- Apply Leonard's capital allocation discipline to M&A evaluation
- Adopt Hastings' "hit ratio too high" thinking to evaluate whether the company is taking enough risk
