# Fiduciary Duties Check Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures board and advisory members understand and fulfill their fiduciary duties: duty of care (informed decision-making), duty of loyalty (acting in the organization's best interest), and duty of obedience (acting within the organization's mission and charter). This checklist surfaces gaps before they become legal liabilities.

## When to Use
- During board onboarding for new members.
- Before any decision involving potential conflicts of interest.
- Annually during governance reviews.
- When a decision may expose the organization to fiduciary risk.

## Gate Criteria

### Duty of Care
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Board members consistently attend meetings and review materials before sessions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Decisions are made after adequate deliberation and with sufficient information.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Board members ask probing questions and do not rubber-stamp management proposals.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: External expertise is engaged when the board lacks competence in a decision domain.

### Duty of Loyalty
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Conflicts of interest are disclosed in writing and updated annually.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Conflicted members recuse themselves from relevant deliberations and votes.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Related-party transactions are reviewed and approved by disinterested board members.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Board members do not use confidential information for personal benefit.

### Duty of Obedience
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Decisions are consistent with the organization's charter, bylaws, and stated mission.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Legal and regulatory requirements are understood and complied with.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The board does not authorize actions that violate applicable law.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Changes to the organization's mission or charter follow proper governance procedures.

### Documentation and Process
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Board deliberations are documented sufficiently to demonstrate compliance with duty of care.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Conflict of interest disclosures are maintained on file.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Legal counsel is accessible and consulted for governance questions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: D&O insurance is in place and adequate for the organization's risk profile.

## Red Flags
- Board members frequently miss meetings or arrive unprepared.
- No conflict of interest policy exists or it is not enforced.
- Related-party transactions are approved without independent review.
- The board does not have access to independent legal counsel.
- Decisions are made without documentation of the deliberative process.

## Escalation Triggers
- A board member has an undisclosed conflict of interest in a material decision.
- Legal counsel advises that a proposed action may breach fiduciary duties.
- A lawsuit or regulatory action alleges breach of fiduciary duty.
- A board member uses confidential information for personal gain.
- The board approves an action that contradicts the charter without proper amendment.
