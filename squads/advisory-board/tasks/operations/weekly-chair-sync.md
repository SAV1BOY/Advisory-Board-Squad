# Task: Weekly Chair Sync

## Objective
Maintain a structured weekly sync between the CEO and Board Chair to ensure continuous alignment, early issue detection, and efficient use of formal board meeting time.

## Trigger
- Weekly on a set day and time
- Adjusts to more frequent during crises or critical periods
- May reduce to bi-weekly during stable periods by mutual agreement

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO
- **Support:** Board Chair
- **Advisory:** Board Ops may attend for action tracking


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → cross-squad-sync`

- **Lead:** board-chair (Board Chair)
- **Support:** governance-clerk (Simon Sinek)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Week's key developments documented
- [ ] Any urgent board-level items identified
- [ ] Action items status from prior sync available
- [ ] Upcoming board meeting preparation status if within 4 weeks

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** CEO provides a structured 5-minute verbal update covering top win, top concern, and any item needing Chair input

2. **Step 2:** Chair provides external perspective including relevant intelligence from their network, industry developments, or investor feedback

3. **Step 3:** Discuss any items that may need board attention before the next formal meeting

4. **Step 4:** Review progress on open board action items and any items the Chair is helping with

5. **Step 5:** Align on upcoming board meeting preparation if within the planning window

6. **Step 6:** Capture any decisions or action items from the sync

7. **Step 7:** Weekly Sync Notes brief format; Updated action items; Decisions or direction for board-level items

8. **Step 8:** Urgent items may trigger emergency board communication; Board meeting prep alignment feeds into meeting-prep; Chair perspective informs CEO decision-making; Pattern of concerns may trigger formal board agenda items

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Operational Excellence:** Standardized processes with defined cadence and quality standards
- **Continuous Improvement:** Each cycle identifies and implements at least one process improvement
- **Single Source of Truth:** Every governance artifact has one canonical location and one owner

## Checklists

### Quality Gate

- [ ] Data accuracy verified before distribution
- [ ] Stakeholders notified of material changes
- [ ] Process followed consistently (not ad hoc)
- [ ] Feedback collected and incorporated into process improvement
- [ ] Documentation current and accessible
