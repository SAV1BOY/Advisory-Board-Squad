# Task: Succession Planning

## Objective
Ensure continuity of leadership by identifying and developing successors for all critical executive roles. Maintain emergency succession readiness at all times.

## Trigger
- Annual governance calendar triggers succession review
- Key executive signals potential departure
- Board identifies key-person risk
- Company stage transition requires leadership evolution
- Emergency event highlights succession gaps

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Chair for CEO succession, CEO and CHRO for other roles
- **Support:** Board Ops for process coordination
- **Advisory:** Full board participates in CEO succession discussion

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current executive team performance assessments available
- [ ] Internal talent pipeline data available
- [ ] External market benchmarks for key roles available
- [ ] Prior succession plan if one exists

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Review and update the emergency succession plan ensuring every critical role has a named interim

2. **Step 2:** Assess each executive role for succession urgency based on incumbent tenure, flight risk, and performance

3. **Step 3:** Evaluate internal candidates for each role rating readiness as ready now, 1-2 years, or 2 plus years

4. **Step 4:** Develop individualized development plans for succession candidates with stretch assignments, coaching, and mentoring

5. **Step 5:** Assess external talent landscape for roles without viable internal successors

6. **Step 6:** Present succession plan to the board using succession-planning-memo template in executive session

7. **Step 7:** Establish governance cadence for plan review and development tracking

8. **Step 8:** Succession Planning Memo approved by board; Emergency succession plan current; Development plans for internal candidates; External search readiness for gap roles

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Structured Assessment:** Use consistent frameworks and rubrics to reduce bias and increase rigor
- **Data-Driven People Decisions:** Ground all people decisions in observable evidence, not gut feeling
- **Stakeholder Triangulation:** Gather input from multiple perspectives (manager, peers, reports, cross-functional) for balanced assessment

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment is grounded in specific, observable evidence
- [ ] Multiple perspectives gathered (not single-source input)
- [ ] Outcomes and recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Confidentiality maintained throughout the process
- [ ] Legal and compliance requirements met
- [ ] Timeline and owners assigned for all actions

## Output
- Development plans feed into leadership-coaching
- Emergency succession activates if triggered
- External search needs feed into exec-hiring
- Key person risks update the risk-register
