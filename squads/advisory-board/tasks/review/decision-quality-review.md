# Task: Decision Quality Review

## Objective
Retrospectively assess the quality of board and management decisions to improve the decision-making process. Evaluate whether decisions achieved their intended outcomes and what can be learned.

## Trigger
- Annually as part of the board effectiveness review
- After a major decision produces an unexpected outcome positive or negative
- When a pattern of poor decisions is observed
- Pre-annual-planning to incorporate decision lessons

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Chair or CEO
- **Support:** Board Ops for data compilation, functional leaders for outcome data
- **Advisory:** Full board participates in the review discussion

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Decision log current with all decisions tracked
- [ ] Outcome data available for decisions older than 6 months
- [ ] Prior decision quality reviews for comparison if available

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Pull all decisions from the decision log for the review period noting decision type, date, options considered, and stated rationale

2. **Step 2:** For each significant decision compare actual outcome to predicted outcome at the time of decision

3. **Step 3:** Classify each decision outcome as good decision with good outcome, good decision with bad outcome due to unforeseeable factors, bad decision with good outcome due to luck, or bad decision with bad outcome

4. **Step 4:** Identify patterns in decision-making such as common biases, information gaps, time pressure effects, and groupthink indicators

5. **Step 5:** Assess the decision process quality separately from outcome quality because good processes sometimes produce bad outcomes and vice versa

6. **Step 6:** Develop recommendations for improving the decision-making process based on identified patterns

7. **Step 7:** Present findings to the board as part of the effectiveness review

8. **Step 8:** Decision Quality Review Report; Decision outcome classifications; Process improvement recommendations; Updated decision-making framework if changes needed

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Retrospective Analysis:** Structured look-back that separates process quality from outcome quality
- **Continuous Improvement Cycle:** Plan-Do-Check-Act applied to governance and management processes
- **Balanced Scorecard:** Multi-dimensional assessment that prevents over-indexing on any single metric

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Review is evidence-based with specific data points, not impressions
- [ ] Both positive and negative findings are presented honestly
- [ ] Root causes identified, not just symptoms
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Historical comparison included to show trends
- [ ] Board has clear insight into what needs to change

## Output
- Process improvements feed into meeting facilitation approach
- Bias patterns inform board training
- Information gaps inform pre-read quality standards
- Findings inform annual-planning-session
- Lessons incorporated into decision-memo templates

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Review completion | Within agreed timeline | Calendar tracking |
| Finding quality | Specific, evidence-based, and actionable | Peer review |
| Recommendation adoption | >70% of recommendations actioned within agreed timeline | Action tracking |
