# M&A: Diligence

## Objective
Conduct comprehensive due diligence across financial, legal, technical, commercial, and cultural dimensions to validate the acquisition thesis and uncover deal-breaking risks.

## Inputs
- Board-approved target(s) (01-evaluation.md). Access to target's data room. Legal and financial advisors.

## Process
1. Financial diligence: audit financials, validate revenue quality, assess customer concentration, model unit economics.
2. Legal diligence: review contracts, IP ownership, litigation, regulatory compliance, employment agreements.
3. Technical diligence: assess technology quality, technical debt, architecture scalability, security posture.
4. Commercial diligence: interview key customers, assess market position, validate growth assumptions.
5. Cultural diligence: assess leadership quality, team morale, cultural compatibility, retention risk.
6. Synthesize findings into a diligence summary with go/no-go recommendation.
7. Identify deal-breakers and negotiate solutions or walkaway.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/risk-blocks.md` (comprehensive risk assessment)
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 6 (Cultural Due Diligence)
- `lib/components/hiring-blocks.md` (assess key personnel)

## Output
Diligence report covering all dimensions. Risk inventory. Go/No-Go recommendation.
