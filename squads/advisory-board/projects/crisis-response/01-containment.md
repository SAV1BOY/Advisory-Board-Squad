# Crisis Response: Containment

## Objective
Stop the bleeding. Limit damage before fixing the root cause. Containment precedes communication and root cause analysis.

## Inputs
- Situation assessment (00-situation.md). Available resources and authorities.

## Process
1. Execute immediate containment actions prioritized by: people safety > legal exposure > customer impact > financial impact > reputation.
2. Assess containment effectiveness: is the damage contained or still spreading?
3. Identify dependencies: external resources needed for containment.
4. Estimate cost and timeline of containment.
5. Define containment success criteria: how we know the situation is stabilized.
6. Document all actions taken with timestamps.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/crisis-blocks.md` (Block 3: Containment Plan)

## Output
Containment plan executed. Damage assessment. Stabilization confirmed or escalated.
