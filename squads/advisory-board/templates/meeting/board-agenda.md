## Agenda

### 1. Opening (5 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** Chair

- [ ] Call to order and quorum confirmation
- [ ] Approval of agenda (any additions or modifications)
- [ ] Declaration of conflicts of interest for today's agenda items
- [ ] Approval of prior meeting minutes (reference: [date of prior meeting])

---

### 2. CEO Update (20 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** CEO
**Pre-Read:** CEO Report (pages [X-Y] of pre-read pack)
**Format:** 10-minute presentation + 10-minute Q&A

Topics to cover:
- Key wins and misses since last meeting
- Progress against strategic priorities (RAG status)
- Critical risks and emerging issues
- Key decisions made under delegated authority
- Help needed from the board

**Board Preparation:** Review CEO report in pre-read pack. Come with one question and one observation.

---

### 3. Financial Review (20 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** CFO
**Pre-Read:** Financial package (pages [X-Y] of pre-read pack)
**Format:** 10-minute presentation + 10-minute Q&A

Topics to cover:
- P&L performance vs. budget and forecast
- Cash position and runway update
- Key financial metrics and unit economics
- Material variances and explanations
- Forward-looking guidance and scenarios

**Decision Required:** Yes / No — [specify if budget reallocation or spending approval needed]

---

### 4. Strategic Topic: [TITLE] (40 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** [Presenter Name]
**Pre-Read:** [Brief/Memo title] (pages [X-Y] of pre-read pack)
**Format:** 10-minute context setting + 30-minute structured discussion
**Decision Required:** Yes / No

**Discussion Framework:**
1. Presenter sets context (assumes pre-read has been completed)
2. Clarifying questions (5 minutes)
3. Structured debate: Board members share perspectives in round-robin format
4. Decision or direction capture

**Board Preparation:** Review the [brief/memo] and prepare your position on the key question: "[State the key question]"

---

**Pre-Read:** [Document reference]
**Format:** [Presentation / Discussion / Decision]
**Decision Required:** Yes / No

## Logistics

### Pre-Meeting
- [ ] Agenda finalized and distributed: YYYY-MM-DD
- [ ] Pre-read pack distributed: YYYY-MM-DD
- [ ] Board portal updated with all materials
- [ ] Video conferencing link tested
- [ ] Physical room booked and AV confirmed (if applicable)
- [ ] Dietary requirements confirmed for catering (if applicable)
- [ ] Guest access arranged (if applicable)

### During Meeting
- [ ] Recording setup (if applicable and approved)
- [ ] Minute-taker briefed on format and expectations
- [ ] Decision tracking sheet ready
- [ ] Action item template ready

### Post-Meeting
- [ ] Draft minutes distributed within 48 hours
- [ ] Action items entered in tracker within 24 hours
- [ ] Decision log updated within 24 hours
- [ ] Follow-up materials sent to board members as committed
- [ ] Next meeting preparation timeline set
