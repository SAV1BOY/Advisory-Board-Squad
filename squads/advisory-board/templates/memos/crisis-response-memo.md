# Crisis Response Decision Memo

## Template Guidelines
**Word Limit:** 800 words maximum (excluding headers and tables)
**Reading Time:** Under 5 minutes — designed for rapid consumption during active crisis
**Use When:** The board must make a decision during or immediately after a crisis event. This memo accompanies the crisis brief and focuses specifically on the decision, not the full situation analysis.
