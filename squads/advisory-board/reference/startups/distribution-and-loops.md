# Distribution and Loops

## Distribution Channels

### Viral/Organic
- Product inherently spreads through use (Slack, Dropbox, Figma)
- K-factor > 1 means each user brings in more than one new user
- Lowest CAC but hardest to engineer; usually requires product redesign, not just marketing

### Content/SEO
- Create content that attracts organic search traffic
- Long payback period (6-12 months) but compounds over time
- Best for: B2B SaaS, marketplaces, information products

### Paid Acquisition
- Direct response advertising (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok)
- Immediate, measurable, and scalable—but only while you're spending
- Economics must work: LTV/CAC > 3x with < 18 month payback
- Vulnerable to platform changes and competition driving up costs

### Sales-Led
- Inside sales (SDR → AE model) or field sales for enterprise
- Higher CAC but higher ACV; works for complex products and larger customers
- Sales cycle management is critical: track pipeline velocity, conversion rates, and deal size

### Partnerships/Channel
- Distribution through existing platforms, integrations, or reseller networks
- Can provide massive reach but you don't own the relationship
- Platform risk: if the partner changes terms, your distribution evaporates

## Growth Loops

### Content Loop
User creates content → Content attracts new visitors via search/social → Visitors become users → Users create more content

### Data Network Effect Loop
More users → More data → Better product → More users (Waze, Google Maps)

### Marketplace Loop
More supply → More selection → More demand → More revenue for supply → More supply

### Viral Loop
User invites others → Invitees experience product → Some convert → New users invite others

### Paid Acquisition Loop
Revenue → Reinvest in ads → Acquire customers → Revenue (only works if unit economics are positive)

## Channel-Market Fit

| Product Type | Best Channels |
|-------------|---------------|
| SMB SaaS ($50-500/mo) | Self-serve, content, viral, paid |
| Mid-market SaaS ($500-5K/mo) | Inside sales, content, partnerships |
| Enterprise SaaS ($50K+/yr) | Field sales, events, partnerships |
| Consumer app | Viral, paid social, influencer, app store optimization |
| Marketplace | Supply-side sales + demand-side marketing/SEO |
| E-commerce | Paid, SEO, social, email, influencer |

## Board Application
- Evaluate whether the company has channel-market fit, not just product-market fit
- Monitor channel concentration risk: reliance on a single channel is a strategic vulnerability
- Ask about the company's growth loops and whether they're self-reinforcing
- Assess whether paid acquisition is generating profitable customers or just buying growth metrics
