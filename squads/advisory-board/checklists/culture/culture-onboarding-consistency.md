# Culture Onboarding Consistency Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that new employees receive a consistent, intentional cultural onboarding that transmits the organization's values, norms, and operating style. Culture transmission is most effective and most vulnerable during onboarding. This checklist prevents the failure of leaving cultural assimilation to chance.

## When to Use
- When designing or reviewing the onboarding program.
- When new hire feedback indicates cultural confusion.
- When rapid hiring threatens cultural consistency.
- When assessing why new hires struggle to integrate.

## Gate Criteria

### Content Quality
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Onboarding explicitly covers organizational values with behavioral examples, not just definitions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Cultural norms (communication style, meeting behavior, decision-making approach) are taught, not assumed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Stories of the organization's culture in action are shared by leaders, not just by slides.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: New hires understand how culture influences daily decisions and interactions.

### Delivery Consistency
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Every new hire receives the same cultural onboarding regardless of department or level.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Cultural onboarding is delivered by people who embody the culture.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The onboarding program is documented and version-controlled.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Remote and in-person hires receive equivalent cultural onboarding.

### Integration Support
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each new hire has a culture buddy or mentor (distinct from their manager) for the first 90 days.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Check-ins during the first 90 days specifically assess cultural adjustment.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: New hires are introduced to key cross-functional contacts to build relationships early.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The new hire's direct manager is trained on supporting cultural integration.

### Feedback and Improvement
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: New hire surveys at 30, 60, and 90 days assess cultural onboarding effectiveness.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Feedback is used to improve the onboarding program continuously.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Time-to-productivity is tracked as an indicator of onboarding effectiveness.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Early attrition (within first 6 months) is analyzed for cultural onboarding failures.

## Red Flags
- New hires report they did not understand the culture until months after joining.
- Cultural onboarding is a single presentation on the first day and never revisited.
- Different departments have dramatically different onboarding experiences.
- Early attrition cites cultural surprise as a factor.
- The onboarding program has not been updated in over 12 months.

## Escalation Triggers
- Early attrition rate (within 6 months) exceeds 15%.
- New hire survey scores on cultural clarity are below 70%.
- Managers report that new hires are not exhibiting desired cultural behaviors.
- Rapid hiring causes onboarding quality to deteriorate visibly.
