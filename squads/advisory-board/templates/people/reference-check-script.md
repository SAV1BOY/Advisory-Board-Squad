## Opening (2 minutes)

"Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. We are considering [Candidate Name] for the role of [Title] at our company, and your perspective is very valuable to us. This conversation is confidential — I will not share your specific comments with the candidate. I have about 20 minutes of questions. Shall we begin?"

## Performance and Results (5 minutes)

3. **"What were [Candidate]'s most significant accomplishments during your time together?"**
   - Listen for: Specific, quantified achievements that match the candidate's claims
   - Flags: Vague answers, inability to recall specifics, achievements do not match candidate's description
   - Notes: _______________

4. **"How would you rate [Candidate]'s performance relative to peers in similar roles? Top 5%, top 25%, average, or below average?"**
   - Listen for: Conviction level, hesitation, caveats
   - Flags: Lukewarm endorsement, "they were good but not great," significant hesitation
   - Notes: _______________

5. **"What was the most difficult challenge [Candidate] faced, and how did they handle it?"**
   - Listen for: Resilience, problem-solving approach, leadership under pressure
   - Notes: _______________

### Overall Reference Rating
- [ ] **Exceptional** — Glowing, specific, and enthusiastic endorsement with concrete evidence
- [ ] **Strong** — Positive endorsement with good specific examples
- [ ] **Adequate** — Generally positive but lacking enthusiasm or specificity
- [ ] **Mixed** — Some positive, some concerning signals

## Back-Channel Reference Protocol

For back-channel (not candidate-provided) references:
1. Only conduct with hiring manager approval
2. Never reveal the candidate's identity to someone who may not know they are interviewing
3. Use indirect questions: "I understand you worked at [Company] during [period]. Can you tell me about the leadership team?"
4. Do not share with the candidate that back-channels were conducted
5. Document findings with the same rigor as candidate-provided references
6. Weight back-channel insights appropriately — they may lack full context
