# Task: Annual Planning Session

## Objective
Facilitate an extended board session focused on setting strategic direction, priorities, and success metrics for the coming year. This is the board's most important meeting — where long-term direction is debated and set.

## Trigger
- Annually, typically in Q4 (60-90 days before fiscal year start)
- Extraordinary session if a major strategic pivot is being considered mid-year

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Chair (facilitation), CEO (content)
- **Support:** Board Ops (logistics and materials), CFO (financial planning)
- **Optional:** External facilitator for strategic framing

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Annual board review report completed
- [ ] Board effectiveness review completed
- [ ] Management has prepared a draft strategic plan and budget for board input
- [ ] Competitive landscape report current
- [ ] All board members available for extended session (half-day or full-day)

## Steps

1. **Session Design (T-6 weeks)**
   - Chair and CEO agree on session objectives and desired outcomes
   - Design the session flow: retrospective, forward-looking, decision-making
   - Decide on format: in-person strongly preferred for annual planning
   - Identify any external speakers, facilitators, or pre-session homework
   - Set a clear deliverable: what document or set of decisions will emerge from this session

2. **Pre-Session Materials (T-4 weeks)**
   - CEO prepares: year-in-review, strategic options paper, draft strategic priorities for next year
   - CFO prepares: financial review, budget scenarios, capital allocation options
   - Strategy lead prepares: competitive landscape update, market trends analysis
   - Board Ops compiles and distributes the pre-read package (T-2 weeks)

3. **Pre-Session Homework (T-2 weeks)**
   - Each board member receives a structured prompt to prepare their perspective
   - Prompt: "What are the 3 most important things the company should accomplish next year, and why?"
   - Responses collected by Board Ops and synthesized into themes (shared at session start)

4. **Run the Session (Day-of)**

   **Block 1: Look Back (90 minutes)**
   - Year in review: what we achieved vs. what we planned
   - Decision quality review: which decisions were right, which were wrong, what did we learn?
   - Honest assessment: where did we underinvest, overinvest, or miss signals?

   **Block 2: Look Around (60 minutes)**
   - Competitive landscape briefing
   - Market and technology trends impacting our business
   - Customer and stakeholder insights
   - Board members share external intelligence from their networks

   **Block 3: Look Forward (90 minutes)**
   - CEO presents draft strategic priorities for next year with supporting rationale
   - Board members share their pre-session perspectives (synthesized themes)
   - Structured debate on priorities: what is missing, what should be deprioritized, what is the right sequencing?
   - Challenge session: pre-mortem on each proposed priority ("If this fails, why?")

   **Block 4: Decide (60 minutes)**
   - Align on 3-5 strategic priorities for the year
   - Set measurable success criteria for each priority
   - Agree on resource allocation principles
   - Identify the top 3 risks to the plan and agree on mitigation approaches
   - Set the board's own focus areas for the year (what the board should spend its time on)

5. **Post-Session Documentation (Within 1 week)**
   - Board Ops produces session summary with agreed priorities, metrics, and allocations
   - CEO updates the strategic plan to reflect board input
   - Budget is adjusted to align with agreed priorities
   - Decision log updated with strategic direction decisions
   - Communication plan developed for cascading strategy to the organization

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Pre-Mortem:** For each strategic priority, assume failure and work backward to identify root causes
- **MECE Prioritization:** Ensure priorities are mutually exclusive (no overlap) and collectively exhaustive (no gaps)
- **Reversibility Test:** For each priority, assess how reversible the commitment is — irreversible commitments deserve more scrutiny

## Checklists

### Session Quality Gate
- [ ] All board members participated for the full session
- [ ] Both retrospective and forward-looking blocks were completed
- [ ] 3-5 strategic priorities agreed with measurable success criteria
- [ ] Resource allocation principles established
- [ ] Top risks identified with mitigation approaches
- [ ] Board's own focus areas for the year defined
- [ ] Pre-mortem conducted on each major priority
- [ ] All decisions captured with owners and timelines

## Output
- Annual strategic priorities (3-5, with success metrics)
- Resource allocation framework
- Risk priorities and mitigation plan
- Board focus areas for the year
- Updated strategic plan
- Session summary document

## Handoffs
- Strategic priorities cascade to quarterly board reviews for tracking
- Budget finalization incorporates annual planning decisions
- Board focus areas inform meeting agenda planning for the year
- Risk priorities feed into risk-register-update.md

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Board member attendance | 100% for full session | Attendance record |
| Strategic priorities clarity | All priorities have measurable success criteria | Quality check |
| Management alignment | CEO confirms strategic plan reflects board direction | CEO confirmation |
| Cascade speed | Strategy communicated to organization within 2 weeks of session | Communication tracking |
| Priority stability | <20% of priorities changed mid-year (indicates good planning) | Annual review |
