# Term Sheet Library Index

## Overview
An index of term sheet resources, templates, and educational materials for board members advising on fundraising.

## Standard Term Sheet Templates

### NVCA Model Term Sheet
- The industry standard for venture capital term sheets
- Covers all standard economic and governance terms
- Source: nvca.org/model-legal-documents
- **Board note:** Compare any term sheet you review against this standard to identify non-standard terms

### Y Combinator SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)
- The standard for pre-seed and seed-stage investments
- Variations: SAFE with cap, SAFE with discount, MFN SAFE
- Source: ycombinator.com/documents
- **Board note:** SAFEs are not debt. They don't accrue interest. They convert on a future priced round.

### 500 Global KISS (Keep It Simple Security)
- Alternative to SAFE for convertible instruments
- Available in debt and equity versions
- Source: 500.co/kiss

### Series Seed Documents
- Lightweight priced round documents for seed-stage investments
- Simpler than full NVCA documents but with priced round economics
- Source: seriesseed.com

## Educational Resources

### Brad Feld & Jason Mendelson — "Venture Deals"
- The definitive guide to understanding venture capital term sheets
- Covers economics, control, and other key terms in detail
- **Board note:** Every board member advising on fundraising should read this book

### Holloway Guide to Raising Venture Capital
- Comprehensive online guide covering term sheets, negotiation, and process
- Source: holloway.com/g/venture-capital

### Carta Term Sheet Guide
- Visual, interactive guide to term sheet terms and their implications
- Source: carta.com/blog/term-sheet

## Key Terms Quick Reference

| Term | Founder-Friendly | Investor-Friendly |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Liquidation preference | 1x non-participating | 2x+ participating |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average | Full ratchet |
| Board composition | Founder majority | Investor majority |
| Protective provisions | Standard only | Extensive operational veto |
| Option pool | Post-money, right-sized | Pre-money, oversized |
| Drag-along | High threshold (75%+) | Low threshold (50%+) |
| Pay-to-play | Included | Excluded |

## Board Application
- Maintain familiarity with standard terms to identify non-standard (potentially unfavorable) terms quickly
- Use the NVCA model as a baseline for comparison
- Advise founders to seek legal counsel with venture capital experience for any priced round
- Model the cap table through multiple future rounds before agreeing to terms
