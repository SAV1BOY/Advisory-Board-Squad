# Glossary

Definitions of key terms used across the Advisory Board squad. When a term has a specific meaning in this context, this glossary is the authoritative reference.

---

**Action Item**
A specific task assigned to a named owner with a deadline, resulting from a board meeting or decision. Tracked by the Execution-Tracker.

**Action Tracker**
The centralized log of all open, completed, and overdue action items. Maintained by Execution-Tracker and Governance-Ops.

**Agenda**
The structured plan for a board meeting, specifying topics, time allocations, presenters, and decision items. Set by the Board-Chair.

**Believability Weight**
A calibration of how much weight to give an advisor's input on a specific topic, based on relevant experience, track record, and reasoning quality. See `voice/calibration/believability-weights.md`.

**Black Swan Exercise**
A structured brainstorming session to identify catastrophic, low-probability risks that are not captured by standard risk assessment. Conducted annually.

**Board-Chair**
The agent responsible for running the board, setting the agenda, facilitating meetings, and ensuring follow-through. The hub of the agent network.

**Bus Factor**
The number of people who could be suddenly unavailable before a critical function fails. A bus factor of 1 means a single departure causes a critical gap.

**Capital Allocation**
The process of deciding how to deploy financial resources across competing opportunities. See workflow 03.

**Crisis Protocol**
The structured rapid-response process activated during material crises. See workflow 07.

**Culture Audit**
A structured assessment of organizational culture, measuring the gap between stated values and lived behaviors. See workflow 13.

**Culture-Guardian**
The agent responsible for monitoring values alignment, organizational health, and cultural integrity.

**Decision Log**
The authoritative record of all substantive board decisions, including rationale, options considered, dissent, owner, and review date. Maintained by Decision-Tracker.

**Decision-Tracker**
The agent responsible for recording decisions in real time during meetings and maintaining the decision log.

**Decision Quality Review**
A systematic retrospective on past decisions, separating process quality from outcome luck. See workflow 12.

**Devil's-Advocate**
The agent responsible for challenging every recommendation and presenting the strongest counter-case. Structurally independent.

**Disagree-and-Commit**
A protocol where an advisor formally records their dissent but commits to supporting the group decision publicly. See `voice/calibration/dissent-and-commit.md`.

**Escalation**
The process of raising an issue to a higher decision-making level. Defined by levels (L0-L3) in `docs/escalation-policy.md`.

**Execution-Tracker**
The agent responsible for monitoring action items, initiative progress, and follow-through.

**Expected Value (EV)**
The probability-weighted average of all possible outcomes. Calculated as the sum of each outcome's value multiplied by its probability.

**Follow-Through Scorecard**
A report published at T+21 after each meeting showing the status of all action items: on track, completed, blocked, or overdue.

**Governance-Ops**
The agent responsible for board operations: logistics, templates, tracking, archiving, and access management.

**Inversion**
A mental model that approaches a problem by asking "how could we guarantee failure?" instead of "how do we succeed?" See `phrases/inversion-questions.md`.

**Kill Criteria**
Pre-defined conditions that trigger the termination of an initiative or investment. Set at the time of commitment to prevent sunk-cost bias.

**Moat**
A durable competitive advantage that protects a company's market position and is difficult for competitors to replicate.

**One-Way Door / Two-Way Door**
A classification of decisions by reversibility. One-way doors (irreversible) require careful deliberation. Two-way doors (reversible) should be made quickly.

**OKR (Objectives and Key Results)**
A goal-setting framework where qualitative objectives are paired with 3-5 quantitative key results.

**Pre-Mortem**
A technique where the team imagines that a decision has failed and works backward to identify what went wrong. Conducted before committing, not after.

**Pre-Read**
Written materials distributed to the board before a meeting. Must follow the standards in `docs/pre-read-standards.md`.

**Quorum**
The minimum participation required for the board to make substantive decisions. Defined in the charter.

**Risk-Analyst**
The agent responsible for maintaining the risk register, assessing risks, and designing mitigations.

**Risk Register**
The living document cataloging all identified risks with likelihood, impact, velocity, mitigation plans, and owners. See workflow 16.

**Scorecard (Hiring)**
A structured evaluation tool for executive candidates that defines the role's mission, expected outcomes, required competencies, and cultural markers. See workflow 05.

**Second-Order Effect**
The downstream consequence of a direct action. "If we do X, then Y happens (first order), which causes Z (second order)."

**Squad**
An operational unit responsible for a specific domain of the organization's work. The Advisory Board is itself a squad.

**Squad Brief**
A structured document that translates a board decision into actionable direction for a specific squad. See workflow 15.

**Stakeholder Update**
A periodic written communication to external stakeholders (investors, partners) providing a structured view of company progress. See `voice/channels/stakeholder-update.md`.

**Strategy-Analyst**
The agent responsible for producing analysis, building financial models, and preparing strategic briefs for the board.

**Strategy Refresh**
A structured process to re-examine strategic position, stress-test choices, and produce an updated strategy. See workflow 02.

**Stress Test**
An analysis that tests how a strategy, investment, or portfolio performs under extreme scenarios.

**Succession Planning**
The proactive process of identifying, developing, and preparing successors for critical leadership roles. See workflow 11.

**T-N / T+N**
Timing notation relative to a board meeting. T-5 means 5 business days before the meeting. T+21 means 21 days after.

**Type 1 / Type 2 / Type 3 Decision**
Decision classification by impact and reversibility. Type 1 is irreversible and high-impact. Type 2 is reversible and high-impact. Type 3 is operational and delegated. See `docs/decision-policy.md`.
