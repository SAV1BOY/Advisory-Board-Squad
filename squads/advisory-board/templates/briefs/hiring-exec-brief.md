## Purpose
Brief the board on a proposed executive hire, providing full context for board approval or advisory input on the candidate, role design, compensation, and strategic fit.

## Urgency / Priority

**Priority Level:** Critical / High / Medium / Low

**Urgency Rationale:** Explain why this hire is needed now. Reference business impact of the vacancy, competitive pressure for the candidate, or strategic initiatives that depend on this role being filled.

**Decision Deadline:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Consequences of Delay:** What happens if this role remains unfilled for another 30 / 60 / 90 days.

1. **Budget:** Approved headcount budget and any variances this hire creates.

6. **Confidentiality:** Any restrictions on disclosing the search or candidate identity.
