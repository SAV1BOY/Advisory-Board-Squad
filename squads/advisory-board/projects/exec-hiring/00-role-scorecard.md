# Exec Hiring: Role Scorecard

## Objective
Define the executive role with enough precision that evaluation is objective, not subjective. The scorecard is the single most important document in the hiring process — it determines who you look for, how you evaluate, and how you measure success.

## Inputs
- Strategic plan and the specific challenge this role must address.
- Current organizational gaps and capabilities.
- Culture definition and values from culture-blocks.md.
- Compensation benchmarks for the role.

## Process
1. Define the role's mission: one sentence explaining why this role exists.
2. Define 3-5 measurable outcomes the hire must deliver in 12 months.
3. Identify 5-7 competencies required, rated as Must-Have or Nice-to-Have.
4. Define cultural attributes that predict success in this specific organization.
5. Define anti-patterns: characteristics that historically predict failure.
6. Set compensation range with market benchmark source.
7. Quantify hiring urgency: what is the cost of leaving this role unfilled?
8. Get board approval on the scorecard before sourcing begins.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/hiring-blocks.md` (Block 1: Role Scorecard)
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` (Block 6: Culture Due Diligence criteria)
- `data/registries/hiring-registry.yaml` (create registry entry)

## Output
- Approved role scorecard document.
- Hiring registry entry created.
- Board alignment on role definition and evaluation criteria.
