# Task: Reputational Risk Review

## Objective
Proactively assess and manage risks to the company's reputation with customers, employees, investors, regulators, and the public. Prevent reputational crises through early detection and mitigation.

## Trigger
- Quarterly as part of the risk review cycle
- When negative media coverage or social media attention occurs
- When a customer complaint escalates publicly
- When an employee situation could become external
- Before major public announcements or events
- When a competitor suffers a reputational crisis that may affect the industry

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Communications Lead
- **Support:** Legal Counsel, CHRO, Customer Success, Marketing
- **Advisory:** Board members with communications or crisis management experience

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Scan all reputational risk surfaces including media, social media, customer reviews, employee platforms, regulatory filings, and industry forums

2. **Step 2:** Categorize identified risks by source and severity using the risk register framework

3. **Step 3:** For each material risk assess the probability of escalation, potential impact, and available mitigation options

4. **Step 4:** Review current reputational defenses including media relationships, crisis communication plans, spokesperson readiness, and monitoring tools

5. **Step 5:** Develop or update mitigation plans for the top reputational risks

6. **Step 6:** Assess preparedness for rapid response including holding statements, spokesperson availability, and escalation procedures

7. **Step 7:** Update the risk register with reputational risk assessments and present material risks to the board

8. **Step 8:** Reputational Risk Assessment; Updated risk register; Mitigation plans for top risks; Response readiness assessment; Media monitoring summary

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Risk Scoring Matrix:** Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5) for consistent risk quantification
- **Swiss Cheese Model:** Multiple layers of defense with the understanding that each layer has holes
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis:** Assume the worst outcome and work backward to identify causes and prevention

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All risk dimensions assessed (financial, operational, strategic, people, legal, technology, reputational)
- [ ] Risk scoring is consistent and calibrated across assessors
- [ ] Mitigation plans are specific with owners and timelines
- [ ] Board reporting is clear, concise, and highlights material changes
- [ ] Risk appetite thresholds are respected and breaches escalated
- [ ] Historical risk data preserved for trend analysis

## Output
- Material risks feed into risk-register-update
- Spokesperson needs feed into communications planning
- Employee-related risks feed into culture-health-tracker
- Customer-related risks feed into stakeholder-communication-review
