# Mission Integrity Initiative: Implementation

## Objective
Roll out mission integrity policies and practices across the organization with training, structural reinforcement, and visible leadership commitment.

## Inputs
- Approved policies (02-policy.md). Implementation plan. Communication strategy.

## Process
1. Communicate the initiative and policies to the entire organization with CEO leadership.
2. Conduct training on mission integrity criteria and decision-making.
3. Integrate mission integrity checks into existing decision processes.
4. Update incentive structures to reward mission-aligned behavior.
5. Establish rituals that reinforce mission integrity (using culture-blocks.md Block 3).
6. Create safe channels for reporting mission integrity concerns.
7. Lead by example: document and publicize leadership decisions that prioritize mission over short-term gain.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 3 (Rituals) and Block 5 (Culture Change)
- `scripts/generation/stakeholder-update-builder.md` (communication)

## Output
Implementation complete. Training delivered. Structural reinforcements in place. Reporting channels active.
