# Risk Assessment Session Brief

## Session Type
Strategic Risk Assessment

## Purpose
Systematically identify, evaluate, and prioritize risks facing the organization across operational, strategic, financial, regulatory, and reputational dimensions. Produce a ranked risk register with mitigation plans for the top risks.

## Pre-Session Preparation

### For the Facilitator
- [ ] Gather previous risk assessments and audit findings
- [ ] Collect incident reports from the past 12 months
- [ ] Review industry risk reports and competitor incidents
- [ ] Prepare risk category framework and scoring rubric
- [ ] Identify participants from each functional area
- [ ] Distribute pre-read materials 5 business days before session

### For Participants
- [ ] Review the risk category definitions below
- [ ] Prepare a list of the top 3 risks in your functional area
- [ ] Bring data on any near-misses or incidents from the past year
- [ ] Consider risks that span multiple departments or functions
- [ ] Think about risks that are being under-discussed or actively avoided

## Session Agenda (3 Hours)

| Time | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| 0:00 | Opening: objectives, ground rules, and risk categories | 15 min |
| 0:15 | Individual risk brainstorm (silent writing) | 15 min |
| 0:30 | Group risk identification and clustering | 30 min |
| 1:00 | Break | 10 min |
| 1:10 | Risk scoring: likelihood and impact assessment | 40 min |
| 1:50 | Risk prioritization and heat map construction | 20 min |
| 2:10 | Break | 10 min |
| 2:20 | Mitigation planning for top 5 risks | 30 min |
| 2:50 | Assign owners, next steps, and review cadence | 10 min |

## Risk Categories

### Strategic Risk
Threats to the business model, competitive position, or market relevance.
*Examples: market disruption, competitive entry, technology obsolescence, customer concentration*

### Operational Risk
Failures in processes, systems, people, or external events that disrupt operations.
*Examples: key person dependency, system outages, supply chain disruption, quality failures*

### Financial Risk
Threats to financial health, liquidity, or capital structure.
*Examples: revenue concentration, cash runway, currency exposure, credit risk, fraud*

### Regulatory and Legal Risk
Exposure to legal action, regulatory enforcement, or compliance failures.
*Examples: data privacy violations, IP infringement, employment law, industry regulation*

### Reputational Risk
Events that damage brand, trust, or stakeholder confidence.
*Examples: product failures, executive misconduct, security breaches, social media crises*

## Risk Scoring Matrix

### Likelihood Scale
| Score | Level | Definition |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| 5 | Almost Certain | Expected to occur within 12 months |
| 4 | Likely | Will probably occur within 12 months |
| 3 | Possible | Could occur within 12 months |
| 2 | Unlikely | Not expected but plausible |
| 1 | Rare | Would require extraordinary circumstances |

### Impact Scale
| Score | Level | Definition |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| 5 | Catastrophic | Threatens company survival or requires >50% revenue to resolve |
| 4 | Major | Significant financial loss (>20% revenue) or major reputational damage |
| 3 | Moderate | Material impact requiring significant management attention |
| 2 | Minor | Manageable with existing resources; limited external visibility |
| 1 | Negligible | Minimal impact; handled within normal operations |

### Risk Priority = Likelihood x Impact
| Priority | Score Range | Action Required |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| Critical | 15-25 | Immediate mitigation plan; board-level visibility |
| High | 10-14 | Active mitigation within 30 days; executive ownership |
| Medium | 5-9 | Monitor quarterly; develop contingency plans |
| Low | 1-4 | Accept and monitor annually |

## Output Template

### Risk Register Entry
For each identified risk:
- **Risk ID:** [Sequential number]
- **Category:** [Strategic / Operational / Financial / Regulatory / Reputational]
- **Description:** [Clear statement of the risk event and its potential consequences]
- **Likelihood:** [1-5]
- **Impact:** [1-5]
- **Priority Score:** [L x I]
- **Current Controls:** [What is already in place to mitigate this risk?]
- **Recommended Actions:** [What additional mitigation is needed?]
- **Owner:** [Name and role]
- **Review Date:** [Next review milestone]

## Ground Rules for the Session

1. All risks are valid — no dismissing concerns raised by any participant
2. Focus on organizational risks, not personal grievances
3. Quantify where possible, but do not let lack of data prevent identification
4. Discuss the uncomfortable risks — the ones people avoid naming
5. The goal is a prioritized list, not an exhaustive inventory
