# Org Health Turnaround: Scorecard

## Objective
Measure the effectiveness of the turnaround initiative and determine whether the organizational health trajectory has shifted.

## Inputs
- Culture health assessment data (pre and post intervention). Pulse survey trends. Attrition data. Engagement metrics.

## Process
1. Conduct full culture health assessment at 90-day and 180-day marks.
2. Compare scores to pre-turnaround baseline across all six dimensions.
3. Assess whether leadership behavior changes are sustained.
4. Evaluate ritual effectiveness: are they working or becoming performative?
5. Review attrition and engagement trends.
6. Decide: continue current interventions, escalate, or transition to maintenance mode.
7. Capture lessons for the lessons-learned registry.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/utilities/culture-health-rubric.md`, `data/metrics/culture-health-score.md`
- `data/registries/lessons-learned-registry.yaml`

## Output
Turnaround scorecard with before/after comparison, lessons learned, and recommendation for next phase (continue, escalate, or maintain).

## Next Phase
Transition to ongoing culture stewardship through quarterly culture health assessments.
