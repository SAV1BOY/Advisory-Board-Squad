## RAG Criteria

| Dimension | Green (>75) | Amber (50-75) | Red (<50) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Values Alignment | >80% "lived daily" on all values | 60-80% on most values | <60% on any value or declining trend |
| Psychological Safety | >80% positive on all indicators | 60-80% positive | <60% or declining on any indicator |
| Trust in Leadership | eNPS >30, >75% trust leadership | eNPS 10-30, 55-75% trust | eNPS <10, <55% trust |
| Belonging | >80% feel belonging, inclusion index >75 | 60-80%, index 50-75 | <60%, index <50 |
| Communication | >80% understand strategy | 60-80% | <60% |
| Accountability | >75% "people are held accountable" | 50-75% | <50% |
| Innovation | >70% "safe to take risks" | 50-70% | <50% |
