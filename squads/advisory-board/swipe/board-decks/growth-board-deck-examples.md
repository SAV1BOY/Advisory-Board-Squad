# Growth Board Deck Examples

## Overview
Growth-stage board decks (Series B+) reflect a company with proven product-market fit, scaling operations, and increasing organizational complexity. Decks should demonstrate strategic clarity, operational discipline, and proactive risk management. Governance expectations are higher.

## Recommended Structure (20-25 slides)

### Section 1: Executive Summary (2 slides)
- CEO's letter: strategic narrative, key themes, candid assessment
- Dashboard summary: 5-7 key metrics with traffic light status (green/yellow/red)

### Section 2: Financial Performance (4-5 slides)
- P&L with variance analysis (actual vs. plan vs. prior year)
- Revenue breakdown: by product, segment, geography, new vs. expansion
- Cash flow and balance sheet
- Updated full-year forecast with scenario analysis (bear/base/bull)
- Capital allocation review: where is money being deployed and what's the return?

### Section 3: Growth and GTM (3-4 slides)
- Bookings and pipeline analysis
- Sales efficiency metrics: magic number, CAC payback, quota attainment by cohort
- Marketing efficiency: CAC by channel, funnel conversion rates, brand metrics
- Customer success: NRR, logo retention, expansion rates, health scores

### Section 4: Product and Technology (3 slides)
- Product KPIs: engagement, feature adoption, NPS
- Roadmap progress: delivered vs. planned
- Technology infrastructure: scalability, security, technical debt
- AI/ML strategy and competitive technology assessment

### Section 5: People and Organization (3 slides)
- Organizational health dashboard: engagement, attrition, eNPS
- Leadership team assessment: performance and development status
- Talent acquisition: pipeline, time-to-hire, quality of hire
- DEI metrics and progress

### Section 6: Risk and Compliance (2 slides)
- Updated risk register with probability/impact assessment
- Regulatory and compliance status
- Cybersecurity posture
- Litigation and legal matters

### Section 7: Strategic Discussion (3-4 slides)
- Strategy update: progress against the strategic plan
- Market landscape: industry trends, competitive dynamics, emerging threats and opportunities
- Deep-dive topic: one strategic issue requiring board discussion (pre-read memo distributed separately)
- Options analysis for key decisions

### Section 8: Governance (1-2 slides)
- Committee reports: audit, compensation, governance
- Board calendar and upcoming decisions
- Consent agenda items

### Section 9: Appendix
- Detailed financial schedules
- Competitive intelligence
- Customer case studies
- Employee survey detailed results

## Board Application
- Growth-stage boards should spend 50% on strategy, 30% on oversight, 20% on governance
- Require a written pre-read memo for the strategic deep-dive topic—no PowerPoint-only presentations for important decisions
- Track plan vs. actual over multiple quarters; patterns reveal more than any single quarter
- Ensure the CEO is spending board time on decisions where the board adds value, not on operational updates that could be pre-read
