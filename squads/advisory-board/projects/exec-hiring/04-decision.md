# Exec Hiring: Decision

## Objective
Make the hire/no-hire decision using structured evaluation, documented rationale, and explicit acknowledgment of risks.

## Inputs
- Complete candidate evaluations: interview scorecards, reference summaries, cultural assessment.
- Compensation requirements and budget.
- Hiring committee perspectives.

## Process
1. Review each finalist's complete evaluation package.
2. Score against the original scorecard: how does each finalist perform on every dimension?
3. Identify top 3 strengths and top 3 risks for each finalist.
4. For each risk, define a mitigation plan.
5. Make the decision using the appropriate method (typically conviction + advisory: CEO decides with board input).
6. Capture dissent if any committee members disagree.
7. Prepare the offer: compensation, equity, start date, and 90-day success criteria.
8. Record the hiring decision in the decision registry with full rationale.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/hiring-blocks.md` (Block 5: Hiring Decision Record)
- `lib/components/decision-memo-blocks.md` (decision memo format)
- `lib/patterns/dissent-and-commit-pattern.md` (if committee is split)
- `data/registries/decision-registry.yaml` (record the decision)

## Output
- Hiring decision record with rationale, risks, and mitigation plans.
- Offer package approved and ready to extend.
- Decision registry entry created.
