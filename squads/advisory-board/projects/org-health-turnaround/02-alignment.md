# Org Health Turnaround: Alignment

## Objective
Align the leadership team on the diagnosis and the path forward. Without leadership alignment, turnaround interventions will be inconsistent and ineffective.

## Inputs
- Diagnosis document (01-diagnosis.md). Leadership team perspectives.

## Process
1. Present diagnosis to full leadership team in a structured workshop.
2. Create space for disagreement and dissent about the diagnosis.
3. Align on the 2-3 highest-priority cultural issues to address.
4. Define what leadership behavior change looks like for each priority.
5. Each leader commits to specific, observable behavior changes.
6. Address any leadership mismatches identified in the diagnosis.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `authority/workshop-kits/culture-health-workshop.md`
- `lib/patterns/dissent-and-commit-pattern.md`

## Output
Leadership alignment document with agreed priorities, committed behavior changes, and accountability structure.
