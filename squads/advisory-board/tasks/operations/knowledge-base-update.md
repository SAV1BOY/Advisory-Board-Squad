# Task: Board Knowledge Base Update

## Objective
Maintain and update the board's knowledge base — the collection of reference materials, templates, policies, historical decisions, and institutional knowledge that enables effective board governance. Ensure all materials are current, accessible, and organized.

## Trigger
- Quarterly maintenance cycle (aligned with board meeting preparation)
- After any board decision that creates or modifies a policy
- When new templates or frameworks are adopted
- During new board member onboarding (to ensure materials are current)
- After a significant organizational change (restructuring, acquisition, leadership change)
- Annual comprehensive review and refresh

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Secretary or Board Ops lead
- **Support:** CEO, CFO, General Counsel, functional leaders (for content areas they own)
- **Advisory:** Board Chair for governance materials

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current knowledge base inventory and structure documented
- [ ] Access permissions and security protocols defined
- [ ] Content ownership assigned for each section
- [ ] Version control and change log maintained
- [ ] Board member feedback on knowledge base usability collected

## Steps

1. **Inventory and Audit (Days 1-3)**
   - Review the complete knowledge base inventory — all documents, templates, and reference materials
   - Identify documents that are out of date or have not been reviewed within their review cycle
   - Check all links, references, and cross-references for accuracy
   - Identify gaps — topics or materials that should exist but do not
   - Review access logs — which materials are frequently accessed vs. never used
   - Verify that sensitive materials have appropriate access controls

2. **Content Updates (Days 3-10)**
   - Update all documents flagged as out of date:
     - Policy documents — reflect current board-approved policies
     - Templates — incorporate improvements from recent usage
     - Decision log — all decisions from the most recent period entered and categorized
     - Contact information — board members, advisors, external counsel, key management
     - Organizational charts and reporting structures
     - Financial reference data (cap table, key metrics history, benchmarks)
   - Archive superseded documents with clear labeling
   - Ensure version numbers and last-updated dates are current on all documents

3. **Structural Review (Days 8-12)**
   - Assess the knowledge base organization — is the structure intuitive?
   - Evaluate naming conventions for consistency
   - Ensure the index or table of contents is current
   - Review the onboarding section — does it give a new board member what they need?
   - Assess search and navigation — can members find what they need quickly?
   - Evaluate the format — consistency in headers, structure, and style across documents

4. **New Content Development (Days 10-15)**
   - Create any new templates or documents identified in the gap analysis
   - Develop summaries or quick-reference guides for frequently needed information
   - Add recent board decisions and their context to the decision log
   - Update the FAQ or common questions section based on recent board inquiries
   - Incorporate learnings from post-mortems and reviews into relevant reference materials

5. **Distribution and Communication (Days 15-18)**
   - Notify board members of material updates to the knowledge base
   - Distribute a change log summarizing what was added, updated, or archived
   - Confirm all board members have access and can navigate the updated materials
   - Collect feedback on the knowledge base and log improvement suggestions
   - Update the next review date for each section

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Single Source of Truth:** Every governance artifact has one canonical location and one owner
- **Information Architecture:** Organize by user need, not organizational structure
- **Version Control:** Clear versioning with change history for all policy documents
- **Access Control:** Right information to right people with appropriate security

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All documents reviewed within their defined review cycle
- [ ] No broken links or outdated cross-references
- [ ] Decision log current through the most recent board meeting
- [ ] Contact information verified and current
- [ ] Access controls verified — sensitive documents appropriately restricted
- [ ] New board member onboarding materials tested for completeness
- [ ] Change log distributed to all board members

## Output
- Updated knowledge base with all documents current
- Change log for the update period
- Gap analysis and content development plan (if gaps identified)
- Board member notification of updates
- Updated review schedule for next cycle

## Handoffs
- Updated templates feed into meeting-prep and board meeting workflows
- Decision log updates support decision-quality-review and forecast-accuracy-review tasks
- Onboarding materials feed into onboard-new-advisor task
- Policy updates feed into regulatory-compliance-check task
- Historical data supports annual-planning-session and strategic-diagnosis tasks

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Document currency | 100% within review cycle | Staleness audit |
| Knowledge base completeness | Zero identified gaps unfilled | Gap analysis |
| Board member access verification | 100% confirmed access quarterly | Access check |
| Update cycle compliance | Updates completed within 5 days of quarter close | Calendar |
| Board member satisfaction | >4/5 on knowledge base usability | Annual survey |
