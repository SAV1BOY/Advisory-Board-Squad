## 6. Annual Review Checklist

- [ ] All critical roles have identified successors (minimum 2 deep)
- [ ] Development plans are active and progressing
- [ ] Emergency contact information and authorization documents are current
- [ ] Board has met successor candidates in both formal and informal settings
- [ ] Succession plan has been reviewed by legal counsel
- [ ] Plan is stored securely and accessible to board chair and CHRO
