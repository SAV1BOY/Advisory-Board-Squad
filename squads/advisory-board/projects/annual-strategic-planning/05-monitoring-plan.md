# Annual Strategic Planning: Monitoring Plan

## Objective
Define how strategic progress will be monitored throughout the year, including review cadences, escalation triggers, and adaptation protocols.

## Inputs
- OKRs and milestones (04-okrs-and-milestones.md). Risk register. Strategic assumptions.

## Process
1. Define monitoring cadence: weekly operational metrics, monthly OKR scoring, quarterly strategic review, annual planning cycle.
2. Design the strategic dashboard: 7-10 metrics that provide a real-time view of strategic health.
3. Define assumption validation schedule: when and how each critical strategic assumption will be tested.
4. Set pivot triggers: specific metric thresholds that should cause a strategic reassessment.
5. Define escalation protocols: who is notified when metrics breach concern thresholds.
6. Schedule all quarterly strategic reviews for the year.
7. Define the mid-year strategic checkpoint: a structured review of whether the annual plan needs adjustment.
8. Assign monitoring ownership: who watches which metrics and reports to whom.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `data/metrics/` (all metric definitions)
- `scripts/tracking/scorecard-updater.md` (automated monitoring)
- `scripts/analysis/quarterly-review-builder.md` (quarterly review assembly)
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` Block 6 (Strategic Risk & Assumptions)
- `lib/patterns/pivot-pattern.md` (if pivot triggers are hit)

## Output
Monitoring plan with dashboard design, review calendar, pivot triggers, and escalation protocols. All reviews scheduled for the year.

## Next Phase
Execute the strategic plan with monitoring in place. The annual cycle continues with the next year's retrospective.
