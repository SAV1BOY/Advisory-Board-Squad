## Background

### Strategic Alignment
Explain how this capital request connects to board-approved strategic priorities. Reference specific strategic objectives and the decision-log entry for the relevant strategy approval.

### Business Case Summary
In 3-5 paragraphs, describe the investment thesis:
1. What problem or opportunity does this capital address?
2. Why is now the right time to deploy this capital?
3. What alternatives were considered and why were they rejected?
4. What is the expected return and over what timeframe?

## Constraints

1. **Cash Reserves Policy:** Minimum reserve requirements and how this allocation affects compliance.
2. **Covenant Requirements:** Any debt covenants, investor side letters, or board-approved spending limits.
3. **Tax Implications:** Tax treatment of the expenditure and any timing considerations.
4. **Accounting Treatment:** CapEx vs. OpEx classification and P&L impact.
5. **Approval Chain:** Any additional approvals required beyond the board (investor consent, lender approval).
6. **Vendor / Counterparty:** Exclusivity periods, contractual lock-ins, or switching costs.
