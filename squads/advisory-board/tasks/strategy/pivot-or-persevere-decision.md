# Task: Pivot or Persevere Decision

## Objective
Facilitate a rigorous pivot-or-persevere decision when evidence suggests the current strategy may not be working. Apply structured analysis to overcome cognitive biases and reach a clear decision.

## Trigger
- When key metrics miss targets for 2+ consecutive quarters
- When the board or CEO questions fundamental strategy
- When market conditions shift dramatically
- Pre-fundraise if current traction is insufficient

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO, Strategy Lead, Board Chair
- **Support:** CFO for financial analysis, Product and Sales for market evidence
- **Advisory:** Board members for pattern recognition and external perspective

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current performance data vs. original plan available
- [ ] Customer feedback and market data current
- [ ] Financial runway analysis complete
- [ ] Team has had initial internal discussion
- [ ] Prior decision-log entry for original strategy available

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Assemble the evidence base objectively gathering all quantitative signals for and against the current strategy including metrics vs original targets, customer acquisition and retention trends, competitive position changes, and team capacity and morale indicators

2. **Step 2:** Apply the pivot-or-persevere framework from the memo template presenting a balanced view of persevere signals, pivot signals, and ambiguous signals

3. **Step 3:** Conduct cognitive bias audit explicitly testing for sunk cost fallacy, confirmation bias, anchoring, and loss aversion by asking what a new CEO with no history would do

4. **Step 4:** Develop options including persevere with adjustments, pivot to a specific new direction, reduce and explore, and wind down; quantify each option

5. **Step 5:** Facilitate board discussion using the structured pivot-or-persevere-memo template ensuring all options get equal analytical rigor

6. **Step 6:** Capture the decision with clear success criteria, timeline for validation, and conditions for revisiting

7. **Step 7:** Pivot-or-Persevere Memo; Cognitive Bias Audit; Evidence Base; Decision with validation criteria

8. **Step 8:** Decision feeds into execution-30-60-90 for implementation; If pivot: updated strategic plan and communication plan needed; Financial implications feed into runway-scenarios

## Frameworks to Apply
- **First Principles Analysis:** Break the problem down to fundamental truths before building solutions
- **Pre-Mortem:** Assume the initiative fails and work backward to identify likely causes
- **Reversibility Assessment:** Evaluate how reversible each option is — irreversible decisions deserve more scrutiny

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Analysis uses primary data sources, not assumptions
- [ ] Multiple options presented with quantified trade-offs
- [ ] Risks identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Success metrics defined with kill criteria
- [ ] Board has a clear decision or direction to give
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled
