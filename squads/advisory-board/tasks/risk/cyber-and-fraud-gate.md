# Task: Cybersecurity and Fraud Gate Review

## Objective
Assess the company's cybersecurity posture and fraud prevention controls at board level. Ensure adequate protection of company and customer data, systems, and financial assets.

## Trigger
- Quarterly as part of the risk review cycle
- After any security incident or near-miss
- When a significant system change or migration is planned
- When industry peers experience notable breaches
- Before handling new categories of sensitive data
- When regulatory requirements change

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CTO or CISO
- **Support:** Engineering leadership, Finance for fraud controls, Legal for compliance, external security assessors if engaged
- **Advisory:** Board members with technology or security expertise

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Security assessment or audit data available
- [ ] Incident log current
- [ ] Compliance requirements inventory current
- [ ] System architecture documentation current
- [ ] Access control records available

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Review the current threat landscape including industry-specific threats, recent peer incidents, and emerging attack vectors

2. **Step 2:** Assess technical controls including access management, encryption, monitoring, patching, backup, and recovery readiness

3. **Step 3:** Review human controls including security training completion, phishing test results, and access review compliance

4. **Step 4:** Assess fraud prevention controls including financial controls, approval workflows, segregation of duties, and anomaly detection

5. **Step 5:** Review compliance status against all applicable frameworks and regulations

6. **Step 6:** Conduct a tabletop exercise or review the results of a recent penetration test or security audit

7. **Step 7:** Identify gaps and prioritize remediation based on risk severity

8. **Step 8:** Present findings to the board with risk ratings, remediation priorities, and resource requests

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Risk Scoring Matrix:** Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5) for consistent risk quantification
- **Swiss Cheese Model:** Multiple layers of defense with the understanding that each layer has holes
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis:** Assume the worst outcome and work backward to identify causes and prevention

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All risk dimensions assessed (financial, operational, strategic, people, legal, technology, reputational)
- [ ] Risk scoring is consistent and calibrated across assessors
- [ ] Mitigation plans are specific with owners and timelines
- [ ] Board reporting is clear, concise, and highlights material changes
- [ ] Risk appetite thresholds are respected and breaches escalated
- [ ] Historical risk data preserved for trend analysis

## Output
- Cybersecurity Assessment Report
- Fraud Controls Assessment
- Compliance Status Report
- Remediation Priorities with timeline
- Board presentation

## Handoffs
- Remediation actions feed into engineering sprint planning
- Compliance gaps feed into legal and regulatory response
- Training needs feed into people development
- Material risks feed into risk-register-update
- Significant findings may trigger crisis-response preparation

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Risk register currency | Updated within agreed cadence | Last updated timestamp |
| Risk coverage | All material risk categories represented | Category audit |
| Mitigation plan quality | 100% of high-severity risks have active mitigation | Register audit |
| Board reporting timeliness | Risk update in every quarterly board package | Board package review |
| Risk prediction accuracy | >60% of materialized risks were previously identified | Incident vs. register comparison |
