# Risk Legal and Compliance Lite Checklist

## Purpose
Provides a lightweight compliance check for organizations that are not yet large enough for a full compliance function. This checklist covers the essentials that every organization must address to avoid legal exposure, regulatory risk, and reputational damage from compliance failures.

## When to Use
- Quarterly during governance and risk reviews.
- When entering new markets or jurisdictions.
- When launching products that touch regulated areas (data privacy, financial services, healthcare).
- When regulatory changes affect the business.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization is in compliance with its formation documents and applicable corporate law.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Material contracts are reviewed by legal counsel before execution.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Employment law requirements are met (contracts, classification, benefits, workplace safety).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Intellectual property is properly protected (trademarks, patents, trade secrets, copyright).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Data privacy requirements are identified and complied with (GDPR, CCPA, or applicable local law).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Tax obligations are current and filed on time.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Regulatory requirements specific to the industry are identified and complied with.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Legal counsel is accessible and consulted for material decisions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A litigation or dispute tracker is maintained.

## Red Flags
- No legal counsel has reviewed contracts in over 12 months.
- The company operates in regulated industries without compliance awareness.
- Data privacy requirements are unknown or unaddressed.
- Employment practices have not been reviewed for compliance.
- IP is not properly documented or protected.

## Escalation Triggers
- A regulatory notice or inquiry is received.
- A lawsuit or threat of litigation is received.
- A compliance gap is discovered that could result in material penalties.
- An employee raises a concern about legal compliance.
- The company enters a new jurisdiction without legal review.
