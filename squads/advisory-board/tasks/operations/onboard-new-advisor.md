# Task: Onboard New Board Advisor

## Objective
Execute a structured onboarding process for a new advisory board member to maximize their time to contribution and integration with the existing board.

## Trigger
- New advisory board member appointed
- Board composition change approved
- Replacement member joining after a departure

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Ops with CEO
- **Support:** Board Chair for relationship integration, CHRO for administrative setup
- **Advisory:** Existing board members for relationship building

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Board member agreement signed
- [ ] Compensation and equity terms finalized
- [ ] Administrative setup complete covering email, portal access, and calendar
- [ ] Company materials prepared for distribution

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Send a comprehensive welcome package including board charter, member list with bios, meeting calendar, governance documents, recent board materials covering last 2 quarters, and company overview

2. **Step 2:** Schedule 1-on-1 introductory calls between the new member and each existing board member plus key management team members

3. **Step 3:** CEO conducts a 90-minute strategy deep dive covering company history, current strategy, key challenges, and where the new member's expertise will be most valuable

4. **Step 4:** CFO provides a financial overview covering current position, unit economics, cap table, and financial planning

5. **Step 5:** Provide access to the board portal and walk through the document library, decision log, risk register, and scorecard

6. **Step 6:** New member attends first board meeting as an observer or participant with Chair explicitly inviting their contributions during discussion

7. **Step 7:** Board Ops conducts a 30-day check-in to gather feedback on the onboarding experience and identify any additional information needs

8. **Step 8:** Completed welcome package delivery; Introductory calls scheduled and completed; Portal access confirmed; 30-day check-in conducted; Feedback incorporated

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Operational Excellence:** Standardized processes with defined cadence and quality standards
- **Continuous Improvement:** Each cycle identifies and implements at least one process improvement
- **Single Source of Truth:** Every governance artifact has one canonical location and one owner

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All required updates completed within the defined cadence
- [ ] Data accuracy verified before distribution
- [ ] Stakeholders notified of material changes
- [ ] Process followed consistently (not ad hoc)
- [ ] Feedback collected and incorporated into process improvement
- [ ] Documentation current and accessible

## Output
- New member fully integrated into board operations
- Expertise mapped to upcoming agenda items
- Relationship connections established
- Feedback improves onboarding for future members
