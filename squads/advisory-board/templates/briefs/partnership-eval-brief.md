## Background

### How This Opportunity Arose
Describe the origin of the partnership discussion — who initiated, through what channel, and when.

### Strategic Fit
Explain why this partner and why now. Map the partnership to specific strategic priorities:
1. **Our Strategic Need:** What capability, access, or resource gap does this partnership fill?
2. **Partner's Strategic Need:** What does the partner gain from working with us?
3. **Mutual Value Creation:** What value is created together that neither party can create alone?

### Due Diligence Summary
- **Financial Health:** Summary of partner's financial position (revenue, profitability, funding, runway).
- **Reputation:** Market reputation, customer references, any controversies or litigation.
- **Technical Capability:** Assessment of partner's technology, team, and delivery capacity.
- **Cultural Compatibility:** Assessment of working style, values alignment, decision-making speed.
- **References Checked:** [Number] references from current/former partners of this entity.

## Constraints

1. **Exclusivity Trade-offs:** What we give up by entering this partnership (other potential partners, markets, or approaches).
2. **Resource Requirements:** Engineering, sales, or operations resources needed to support the partnership.
3. **Legal / Regulatory:** Any regulatory approvals, data privacy considerations, or antitrust concerns.
4. **Brand Risk:** How association with this partner affects our brand positioning.
5. **Investor Considerations:** Any investor relationships or expectations that interact with this partnership.
6. **Exit Costs:** What it costs us (financially, operationally, relationally) to exit this partnership.

## Governance Framework (Post-Approval)

### Partnership Governance
- **Joint Steering Committee:** Meeting cadence, membership, decision rights.
- **Escalation Path:** How disputes are resolved (operational escalation to executive escalation to board involvement).
- **Performance Reviews:** Quarterly reviews against agreed KPIs.

### Board Reporting
- **Quarterly:** Partnership performance vs. projections, relationship health assessment.
- **Annually:** Full partnership review — renew, renegotiate, or exit recommendation.
- **Trigger-Based:** Immediate board notification if any kill criteria or dependency thresholds are breached.

### Kill Criteria
The partnership will be brought back to the board for exit consideration if:
1. Revenue from partnership falls below $[minimum] for two consecutive quarters.
2. Partner experiences [specific negative event — acquisition, leadership change, competitive conflict].
3. Our dependency on partner exceeds [X]% of [metric].
4. Partner breaches [specific material term].
