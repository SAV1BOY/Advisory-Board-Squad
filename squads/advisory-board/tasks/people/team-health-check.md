# Task: Team Health Check

## Objective
Assess the health and effectiveness of a specific team or the broader organization. Identify dysfunction patterns and recommend interventions before problems become crises.

## Trigger
- Quarterly pulse check across all teams
- When a team misses objectives for 2 consecutive periods
- When attrition spikes in a specific team
- After significant organizational change
- When cross-functional friction is reported

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CHRO or People Lead
- **Support:** Team managers, HR business partners
- **Advisory:** Board sponsor if board-level intervention may be needed

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Survey or assessment instrument prepared
- [ ] Team performance data available
- [ ] Attrition and engagement data current
- [ ] Manager input collected
- [ ] Confidentiality framework communicated to team

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Deploy team health assessment covering psychological safety, clarity of purpose, decision-making effectiveness, accountability, and trust

2. **Step 2:** Analyze results by team, tenure, level, and compared to company benchmarks

3. **Step 3:** Conduct skip-level conversations to validate and deepen quantitative findings

4. **Step 4:** Identify root causes for any concerning patterns distinguishing between manager issues, structural issues, resource issues, and cultural issues

5. **Step 5:** Develop intervention recommendations tailored to each team's specific challenges

6. **Step 6:** Present findings to the relevant executive with specific improvement actions and timeline

7. **Step 7:** Track improvement through follow-up pulse checks at 30 and 90 days

8. **Step 8:** Team health assessment results; Root cause analysis; Intervention plan with timeline; Follow-up measurement plan

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Structured Assessment:** Use consistent frameworks and rubrics to reduce bias and increase rigor
- **Data-Driven People Decisions:** Ground all people decisions in observable evidence, not gut feeling
- **Stakeholder Triangulation:** Gather input from multiple perspectives (manager, peers, reports, cross-functional) for balanced assessment

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment is grounded in specific, observable evidence
- [ ] Multiple perspectives gathered (not single-source input)
- [ ] Outcomes and recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Confidentiality maintained throughout the process
- [ ] Legal and compliance requirements met
- [ ] Timeline and owners assigned for all actions

## Output
- Manager development needs feed into leadership-coaching
- Structural issues feed into org-design-review
- Cultural patterns feed into culture-audit
- Severe dysfunction may require conflict-resolution
