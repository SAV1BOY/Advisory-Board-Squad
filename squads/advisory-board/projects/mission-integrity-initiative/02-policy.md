# Mission Integrity Initiative: Policy

## Objective
Develop policies that institutionalize mission integrity so it persists beyond individual leaders and does not depend on daily heroism.

## Inputs
- Tradeoff analysis (01-tradeoffs.md). Priority gaps to close. Legal and regulatory requirements.

## Process
1. For each unacceptable gap, draft a policy that specifies the expected behavior.
2. For intentional compromises, document the rationale and review cadence.
3. Define mission integrity criteria for key decision types: product, pricing, partnerships, hiring, marketing.
4. Establish a mission integrity review process for major decisions.
5. Define escalation paths for mission integrity concerns.
6. Get board and leadership approval on policies.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 1 (Cultural Non-Negotiables)
- `data/registries/principle-registry.yaml` (formalize mission integrity principles)

## Output
Mission integrity policy document. Updated principle registry. Approved by board.
