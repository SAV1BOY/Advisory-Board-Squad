## Tone and Content Guidelines

### Do
- Lead with data, not adjectives
- Acknowledge challenges directly and share what you are doing about them
- Include specific metrics, not vague directional language
- End with clear asks if you need stakeholder help
- Keep to 2 pages maximum

### Do Not
- Use superlatives without data ("amazing quarter," "incredible growth")
- Hide bad news — stakeholders will find out and trust erodes
- Include metrics without context (a number without a target or trend is meaningless)
- Send without board chair review (for investor updates)
- Include information that has not been shared with the full board first

### Cadence
- **Monthly:** Lightweight update (this template, abbreviated)
- **Quarterly:** Full update aligned with board meeting
- **Ad-Hoc:** Material events (fundraise, acquisition, crisis, key hire/departure)
