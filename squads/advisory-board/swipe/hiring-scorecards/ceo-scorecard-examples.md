# CEO Scorecard Examples

## CEO Evaluation Dimensions

### Strategic Leadership (Weight: 25%)
- Articulates a clear, compelling vision that the organization understands and follows
- Makes decisive strategic choices (where to play, how to win)
- Identifies and responds to industry disruption and competitive threats
- Allocates capital effectively across competing priorities
- **Rating scale:** 1 (reactive, no clear strategy) to 5 (industry-defining strategic vision)

### Operational Execution (Weight: 20%)
- Achieves financial targets (revenue, profitability, cash flow)
- Builds scalable operational systems and processes
- Manages resources efficiently (burn rate, headcount productivity)
- Delivers on committed milestones and timelines
- **Rating scale:** 1 (consistently misses targets) to 5 (exceeds targets while building for the future)

### Team Building and Leadership (Weight: 20%)
- Attracts, develops, and retains top talent
- Builds a high-performing executive team
- Creates a culture of accountability and trust
- Makes difficult people decisions promptly
- **Rating scale:** 1 (high attrition, weak team) to 5 (magnetic talent attractor, world-class team)

### Culture and Values (Weight: 15%)
- Embodies and reinforces company values in daily behavior
- Maintains psychological safety and employee engagement
- Addresses culture issues proactively, not reactively
- Models ethical behavior, especially when it's costly
- **Rating scale:** 1 (culture is deteriorating) to 5 (culture is a recognized competitive advantage)

### External Representation (Weight: 10%)
- Represents the company effectively to investors, partners, and media
- Builds and maintains key external relationships
- Manages the company's reputation proactively
- Communicates transparently with all stakeholders
- **Rating scale:** 1 (avoids external engagement) to 5 (sought-after spokesperson and thought leader)

### Board Relationship (Weight: 10%)
- Communicates proactively and transparently with the board
- Seeks board input on appropriate topics
- Provides timely, accurate information
- Implements board feedback constructively
- **Rating scale:** 1 (adversarial or disengaged) to 5 (productive partnership with appropriate tension)

## Stage-Specific CEO Criteria

### Seed-Stage CEO
- Product vision and technical depth
- Customer obsession and willingness to do things that don't scale
- Speed of learning and pivoting
- Ability to recruit first 10 employees

### Growth-Stage CEO
- Scaling operations without losing culture
- Building management layers and delegating effectively
- Fundraising and investor management
- Navigating increasing organizational complexity

### Late-Stage / Pre-IPO CEO
- Public market readiness and investor relations
- Governance and compliance maturity
- Long-term strategic planning
- Industry leadership and market positioning
