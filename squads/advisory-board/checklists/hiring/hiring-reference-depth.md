# Hiring Reference Depth Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures reference checks go beyond surface-level validation to uncover genuine insights about a candidate's capabilities, working style, and potential fit. Shallow references are a formality; deep references are a decision tool. This checklist prevents the failure mode of investing weeks in interviews only to skip meaningful references.

## When to Use
- For every hire at the senior individual contributor level and above.
- When candidate interviews produce conflicting signals.
- When the candidate has a non-standard career path that requires explanation.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: At least four references are checked, including at least one back-channel (not provided by the candidate).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: References include a former manager, a peer, and a direct report (where applicable).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Specific behavioral questions are asked: "Tell me about a time when..." not just "Would you hire them again?"
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Questions probe for weaknesses and development areas, not just strengths.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Questions assess performance under stress, conflict, and ambiguity.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Reference patterns are analyzed: consistent themes across references carry more weight.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Red flags from references are investigated further, not explained away.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Reference insights are documented and included in the hiring decision discussion.

## Red Flags
- References are all provided by the candidate and are uniformly positive with no nuance.
- The candidate cannot provide direct report references.
- References decline to answer specific behavioral questions.
- A pattern of short tenures is explained differently by the candidate and references.

## Escalation Triggers
- A reference raises a concern about integrity, honesty, or ethical behavior.
- References contradict the candidate's self-representation on material matters.
- Multiple references flag the same weakness that is critical for the role.
- The candidate has no back-channel references available after a decade-long career.
