# Chouinard Stakeholder Impact Check Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that the impact of organizational decisions on all stakeholders (not just shareholders) is assessed before action is taken. Chouinard demonstrated that considering employees, communities, the environment, and future generations alongside financial returns creates more durable value. This checklist prevents the failure mode of optimizing for one stakeholder at the expense of all others.

## When to Use
- Before any major decision that affects multiple stakeholder groups.
- During strategy development to ensure all stakeholder perspectives are considered.
- When evaluating partnerships, supply chain decisions, or market actions.
- When stakeholder complaints or concerns are rising.
- During annual impact assessments.

## Gate Criteria

### Stakeholder Mapping
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: All affected stakeholders are identified (customers, employees, investors, community, environment, suppliers, future generations).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each stakeholder group's interests and concerns are documented.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The power and influence of each stakeholder group is assessed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Stakeholders who cannot advocate for themselves (environment, future generations) have proxy representation.

### Impact Assessment
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Positive and negative impacts on each stakeholder group are assessed for every major decision.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Impacts are assessed in the short term (within 12 months) and long term (3-5 years).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Quantitative measures of impact are used where possible (jobs created, emissions generated, community investment).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Disproportionate negative impact on any single stakeholder group is flagged.

### Balancing and Tradeoffs
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: When stakeholder interests conflict, the tradeoff is made transparently with documented rationale.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Mitigation strategies exist for stakeholders who are negatively affected by a decision.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization does not systematically favor one stakeholder group at the expense of others.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Stakeholder input is sought through appropriate channels before decisions, not just communicated after.

### Accountability and Reporting
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Stakeholder impact is reported to the board or advisory body at least annually.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Commitments to stakeholders are tracked and progress is reported.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Stakeholder satisfaction or trust metrics are monitored for each key group.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization's stakeholder impact improves year over year.

## Red Flags
- Decisions consider only shareholder or financial impact.
- Stakeholder concerns are received but not addressed.
- The organization has no mechanism for hearing from affected communities.
- Environmental impact is not measured or is measured but not disclosed.
- Employee impact is assessed only for layoffs, not for positive investment.
- The organization's supply chain has not been assessed for stakeholder impact.

## Escalation Triggers
- A stakeholder group mounts organized opposition to the company's actions.
- Regulatory action is taken based on negative stakeholder impact.
- A material negative impact is discovered after a decision, that should have been identified before.
- Employee, customer, or community trust metrics show significant decline.
- Media coverage highlights stakeholder harm that the organization failed to address.
