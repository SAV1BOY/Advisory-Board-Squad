# Task: Executive Hiring

## Objective
Run a board-level executive hiring process from role definition through offer and onboarding. Ensure the hire strengthens the leadership team and aligns with company strategy.

## Trigger
- Board approves a new executive role
- Executive departure creates a vacancy
- Performance concerns require an upgrade
- Company stage transition requires new capabilities

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Hiring Manager
- **Support:** CHRO or People Lead, search firm if engaged
- **Advisory:** Board members for interviews and network, Board sponsor

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Role justified and approved by board
- [ ] Compensation framework approved
- [ ] Search budget available
- [ ] Job specification defined

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Define role using exec scorecard and hiring-exec-brief templates with clear 12-month success criteria

2. **Step 2:** Build candidate pipeline through retained search, direct sourcing, board network, and internal candidates

3. **Step 3:** Screen candidates against must-have competencies and anti-patterns

4. **Step 4:** Run structured interview process using interview-kit template with assigned focus areas per interviewer

5. **Step 5:** Conduct reference checks using reference-check-script template for all finalists

6. **Step 6:** Present finalist to board with full hiring-exec-brief for approval

7. **Step 7:** Extend and negotiate offer within approved compensation parameters

8. **Step 8:** Execute onboarding using onboarding-90d-plan template

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Structured Assessment:** Use consistent frameworks and rubrics to reduce bias and increase rigor
- **Data-Driven People Decisions:** Ground all people decisions in observable evidence, not gut feeling
- **Stakeholder Triangulation:** Gather input from multiple perspectives (manager, peers, reports, cross-functional) for balanced assessment

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment is grounded in specific, observable evidence
- [ ] Multiple perspectives gathered (not single-source input)
- [ ] Outcomes and recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Confidentiality maintained throughout the process
- [ ] Legal and compliance requirements met
- [ ] Timeline and owners assigned for all actions

## Output
- Completed hire with signed offer
- Updated org chart
- 90-day onboarding plan
- Board briefed on new executive

## Handoffs
- Onboarding feeds into onboarding-90d-plan execution
- Performance tracking feeds into exec-scorecard
- Org changes feed into org-design-review

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Process completion | Within agreed timeline | Calendar tracking |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | >4/5 from all involved parties | Post-process survey |
| Outcome quality | Actions lead to measurable improvement within 90 days | Follow-up assessment |
| Documentation completeness | All required documents completed and filed | Checklist audit |
| Confidentiality | Zero breaches | Incident reporting |
