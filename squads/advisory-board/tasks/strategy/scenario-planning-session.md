# Task: Scenario Planning Session

## Objective
Facilitate a structured scenario planning session that maps multiple plausible futures, stress-tests current strategy against each, identifies robust moves that work across scenarios, and establishes early warning indicators that signal which future is materializing.

## Trigger
- Annual strategic planning cycle (required)
- Quarterly strategy reviews when macro conditions have shifted significantly
- Before major capital allocation decisions or irreversible commitments
- When the leadership team operates with a single implicit assumption about the future
- After a significant market disruption, regulatory change, or competitive event
- Pre-fundraise to stress-test milestones across market conditions

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Strategy Lead
- **Support:** CFO (financial modeling), CTO (technology scenarios), functional leaders
- **Advisory:** Board members with relevant domain expertise
- **Facilitator:** External facilitator recommended for annual sessions to reduce anchoring

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Focal question defined and circulated to participants 1 week in advance
- [ ] Current strategy document available for reference
- [ ] Market and competitive intelligence updated within 30 days
- [ ] Financial model capable of running multiple scenarios
- [ ] Pre-read distributed: key uncertainties identified by each participant
- [ ] Prior scenario planning outputs reviewed for continuity
- [ ] Room or virtual space set up with visual collaboration tools

## Steps

1. **Frame the focal question (15 min).** State the specific strategic decision or question the session will address. Good focal questions are time-bounded and action-oriented: "What should our geographic expansion strategy be for 2027?" not "What does the future hold?"

2. **Brainstorm driving forces (30 min).** Each participant identifies forces that could shape the future relevant to the focal question. Categories: technology, regulation, competition, customer behavior, macroeconomy, talent market, funding environment. Target 15-20 forces.

3. **Rank forces by impact and uncertainty (20 min).** Plot each force on a 2x2 matrix of impact (high/low) and uncertainty (high/low). High-impact, low-uncertainty forces are planning assumptions. High-impact, high-uncertainty forces are scenario candidates.

4. **Select two critical uncertainties (15 min).** From the high-impact, high-uncertainty quadrant, select the two most consequential and independent forces. These become the axes of the scenario matrix.

5. **Build four scenario narratives (60 min).** For each quadrant of the 2x2, develop a plausible narrative: name the scenario, describe the world in this future, explain the path from today, and identify early signals. Each scenario should be internally consistent and distinct.

6. **Stress-test current strategy (45 min).** Evaluate each element of the current strategy against all four scenarios. Document where the strategy succeeds, fails, or requires adaptation in each future.

7. **Identify robust and contingent moves (30 min).** Robust moves work across all or most scenarios — these are no-regret priorities. Contingent moves are scenario-specific — define the trigger signal that activates each.

8. **Define early warning indicators (20 min).** For each scenario, identify 3-5 leading indicators that would signal this future is materializing. Assign monitoring responsibility and review cadence.

9. **Document outputs and assign actions (20 min).** Capture the scenario matrix, narratives, strategy stress-test results, robust moves, contingent moves, and monitoring dashboard. Assign owners and deadlines.

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Scenario Planning Framework** — Core methodology for the session
- **Second-Order Thinking** — Trace downstream effects within each scenario
- **Pre-Mortem Framework** — Run quick pre-mortems on robust moves
- **Base Rates and Reference Class** — Use base rates to calibrate scenario plausibility
- **Expected Value and Asymmetry** — Evaluate asymmetric payoffs across scenarios

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Two genuinely independent critical uncertainties selected
- [ ] Four distinct, plausible scenarios developed (not just optimistic/pessimistic variants)
- [ ] Current strategy stress-tested against all four scenarios
- [ ] At least 3 robust (no-regret) moves identified
- [ ] Contingent moves have specific trigger signals
- [ ] Early warning indicators are measurable and assigned
- [ ] Financial implications modeled for at least the two most divergent scenarios
- [ ] Outputs documented and distributed within 48 hours

## Output
- Scenario matrix with four named scenarios
- Strategy stress-test assessment
- Prioritized list of robust moves
- Contingent moves with trigger signals
- Early warning monitoring dashboard
- Updated strategic priorities (if warranted)

## Handoffs
- Robust moves feed into execution-30-60-90 and annual-strategy-refresh
- Financial scenarios feed into runway-scenarios and budget-review
- Contingent moves feed into risk-register-update
- Early warning indicators feed into update-board-scorecard
- Scenario narratives inform competitive-response-plan preparation
