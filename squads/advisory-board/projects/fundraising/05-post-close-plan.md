# Fundraising: Post-Close Plan

## Objective
Translate raised capital into execution by activating the use-of-proceeds plan, onboarding the new investor relationship, and setting the cadence for investor communication and accountability.

## Inputs
- Use of proceeds from fundraising brief (00-brief.md).
- New investor's expectations and value-add capabilities.
- Updated financial model with new capital.
- Strategic execution plan.

## Process
1. Activate use of proceeds: begin deploying capital against the defined allocation plan.
2. Onboard the new investor: establish communication cadence, introduce to the team, clarify expectations for board participation.
3. Set up investor reporting: monthly investor update template, quarterly board materials, annual review.
4. Update the strategic plan to reflect new resources and any commitments made during fundraising.
5. Communicate the close to the team: what was raised, what it means for the company, what changes (if any) in priorities.
6. Define the milestone for the next raise: what must be achieved before seeking additional capital.
7. Archive the fundraising process: document lessons learned for the next round.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/capital-blocks.md` (Block 2: Capital Allocation, Block 6: Capital Review Record)
- `scripts/generation/stakeholder-update-builder.md` (investor updates)
- `data/registries/lessons-learned-registry.yaml` (fundraising lessons)

## Output
- Activated capital deployment plan.
- Investor onboarding complete with communication cadence established.
- Updated strategic plan and team communication.
- Fundraising retrospective with lessons for next round.

## Next Phase
Return to regular strategic execution and board cadence. Next fundraising cycle begins when milestone triggers are approached.
