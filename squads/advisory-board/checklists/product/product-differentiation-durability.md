# Product Differentiation Durability Checklist

## Purpose
Assesses whether the product's differentiation is durable or easily replicable. Temporary differentiation creates a window; durable differentiation creates a moat. This checklist evaluates the structural barriers that protect the product's unique value.

## When to Use
- During competitive analysis and product strategy.
- When a competitor replicates a key feature.
- When evaluating whether to invest in deepening differentiation.
- When presenting the competitive advantage thesis to investors.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The product's key differentiators are explicitly identified and ranked by importance.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each differentiator is assessed for replicability: how long would a well-funded competitor take to copy.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Structural barriers (network effects, data advantages, switching costs, patents) protect at least one key differentiator.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Differentiation is perceived by customers, not just by the product team (validated through research).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The product roadmap invests in deepening differentiation, not just adding features.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization monitors competitor product development for convergence on differentiators.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Differentiation contributes to pricing power: customers pay a premium versus alternatives.

## Red Flags
- Differentiation is based solely on features that any competitor could build in 6 months.
- Customers cannot articulate what makes the product different from alternatives.
- Pricing is at parity or below competitors despite claimed differentiation.
- The product team invests in parity features rather than deepening unique advantages.
- Competitors have successfully replicated previous differentiators.

## Escalation Triggers
- A competitor replicates the primary differentiator within a shorter time than anticipated.
- Customer churn increases in segments where differentiation was assumed to be strong.
- Pricing power erodes despite maintained functionality.
- The product team cannot identify any differentiator that would take a competitor more than 12 months to replicate.
