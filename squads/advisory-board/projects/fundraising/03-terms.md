# Fundraising: Terms

## Objective
Evaluate term sheets, negotiate favorable terms, and ensure the company understands the long-term implications of every clause.

## Inputs
- Term sheet(s) received from investors.
- Fundraising brief with walkaway conditions (00-brief.md).
- Legal counsel review.
- Cap table model showing post-investment ownership.

## Process
1. Review each term sheet against the parameters defined in the brief.
2. Map economic terms: valuation, investment amount, liquidation preference, participation, anti-dilution.
3. Map control terms: board seats, protective provisions, information rights, drag-along.
4. Model dilution: current cap table vs. post-investment cap table. Run scenarios for future rounds.
5. Evaluate the investor beyond the terms: track record, portfolio support, references from other founders.
6. Negotiate: focus on the terms that matter long-term (governance, liquidation preferences) rather than optimizing valuation to the dollar.
7. Check walkaway conditions: do any terms violate the pre-defined limits?

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/capital-blocks.md` (Block 4: Fundraising Parameters)
- `lib/components/decision-memo-blocks.md` (decision memo for term sheet acceptance)
- `lib/patterns/dissent-and-commit-pattern.md` (if board disagrees on terms)

## Output
- Term sheet analysis document comparing offers across all dimensions.
- Cap table model with post-investment scenarios.
- Board recommendation on which term sheet to accept.
- Negotiation strategy for remaining open items.
