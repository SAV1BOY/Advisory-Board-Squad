# Crisis Response: Situation Assessment

## Objective
Rapidly classify and assess the crisis to activate the correct response level and assemble the right team.

## Inputs
- Initial incident report or discovery. Available facts (distinguish from speculation). Time pressure assessment.

## Process
1. Classify severity: Level 1 (existential), Level 2 (severe), Level 3 (significant).
2. Categorize: operational, financial, reputational, legal, security, leadership, natural disaster.
3. Assess blast radius: customers, employees, partners, investors, public, regulators.
4. Document what is known, what is suspected, and what is unknown.
5. Activate the crisis team per crisis-blocks.md Block 2.
6. Notify the board per severity level protocols.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/crisis-blocks.md` (Block 1: Classification, Block 2: Team Activation)

## Output
Crisis classification document. Crisis team activated. Board notified.
