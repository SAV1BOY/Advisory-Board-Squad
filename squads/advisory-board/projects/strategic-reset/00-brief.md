# Strategic Reset: Brief

## Objective
Define the scope, trigger, and parameters of a strategic reset initiative. A strategic reset is initiated when the current strategy is no longer producing results and incremental adjustment is insufficient — the company needs to fundamentally rethink its direction.

## Inputs
- Evidence that the current strategy is failing (declining metrics, market shifts, competitive displacement).
- Stakeholder mandate for change (board, CEO, or investor request for strategic review).
- Current strategic plan and its performance against targets.
- Financial position and runway constraints.
- Team capability assessment.

## Process
1. Document the trigger: what specific evidence prompted this reset?
2. Define the scope: full strategic reset or targeted reset of specific elements?
3. Identify participants: who must be involved in the reset process?
4. Set timeline: how long do we have to complete the reset given runway and market conditions?
5. Establish decision authority: who makes the final call on the new direction?
6. Define success criteria: what will a successful reset produce?

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` (Block 1: Strategic Context)

## Output
- Strategic reset brief document (1-2 pages) covering: trigger, scope, participants, timeline, decision authority, and success criteria.
- Approved budget and resource allocation for the reset process.
- Kickoff meeting scheduled with all participants.
