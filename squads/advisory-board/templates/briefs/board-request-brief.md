# Board Request Brief

## Purpose
A general-purpose brief for any matter requiring board-level attention, input, or decision. Use this template when no specialized brief template applies.

## Urgency / Priority

**Priority Level:** Critical / High / Medium / Low

**Urgency Rationale:**
Explain why this matter requires board attention at the stated priority. Reference any deadlines, contractual obligations, competitive windows, or deteriorating conditions that drive the timeline.

**Decision Deadline:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Consequences of Delay:** Describe what happens if the board does not address this within the stated deadline.

---

## Background

Provide 3-5 paragraphs of relevant history. The board should be able to understand the full context without referring to prior documents. Cover:

1. **Origin:** How this matter arose and what triggered the request.
2. **Prior Board Involvement:** Any previous board discussions, decisions, or guidance related to this topic. Reference decision-log entries by ID where applicable.
3. **Current State:** Where things stand today, including any interim actions taken.
4. **Stakeholders Affected:** Internal teams, customers, partners, investors, or regulators with a stake in the outcome.

---

## Key Question

State the single most important question the board must answer. This should be precise and actionable.

> Example: "Should we allocate $2M of reserves to acquire the talent team from [Company X] before their March 30 deadline?"

---

## Decision Needed

Describe the specific decision or output expected from the board:

**Option A:** [Recommended path] — Description, expected outcome, cost, timeline.
**Option B:** [Alternative path] — Description, expected outcome, cost, timeline.
**Option C:** [Status quo / do nothing] — Description, expected outcome, risks of inaction.

**Recommendation:** State which option the requesting party recommends and why.

## Constraints

List all constraints the board should factor into its decision:

1. **Budget:** Available budget and any pre-committed funds.
2. **Legal / Regulatory:** Any legal opinions, regulatory requirements, or compliance obligations.
3. **Technical:** Technical dependencies, feasibility assessments, or architectural constraints.
4. **People:** Headcount limits, skill gaps, or key-person dependencies.
5. **Time:** Hard deadlines, contractual milestones, or seasonal windows.
6. **Political / Relationship:** Partner commitments, investor expectations, or public statements that limit options.

## Appendix

### Pre-Read Checklist (for Board Ops)
- [ ] Brief reviewed for completeness
- [ ] Supporting data verified
- [ ] Financial figures cross-checked
- [ ] Legal review obtained (if applicable)
- [ ] Conflicts of interest flagged
- [ ] Brief added to pre-read package
- [ ] Board members notified

### Quality Standards
- All financial figures must include assumptions and sensitivity ranges
- Customer data must be less than 90 days old unless otherwise noted
- Competitive data must cite primary sources
- Recommendations must include a reversal plan if the decision proves wrong
