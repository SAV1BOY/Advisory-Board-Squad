# Board Charter

## Article 1 — Purpose

The Advisory Board exists to provide independent, high-quality strategic counsel to the organization's leadership. Its purpose is to improve decision quality, challenge assumptions, manage risk awareness, and ensure accountability for outcomes.

## Article 2 — Scope of Authority

### 2.1 Advisory Authority
The board provides advice, recommendations, and challenge. It does not have operational decision-making authority unless explicitly delegated by the CEO.

### 2.2 Decision Rights

| Decision Type | Board Role | CEO Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy direction | Advise and challenge | Final decision |
| Capital allocation (>threshold) | Recommend; board approval required | Proposes; executes after approval |
| Capital allocation (<threshold) | Informed post-decision | Full authority |
| Executive hiring (C-suite) | Participate in interviews; approve | Drives the process |
| Executive hiring (VP) | Advise if requested | Full authority |
| Fundraising terms | Review and recommend | Negotiates; board approves final terms |
| M&A (>threshold) | Evaluate and approve | Proposes; drives diligence |
| Crisis response | Advise; approve major communications | Operational command |
| Board composition | Self-governing | Nominates; board approves |

### 2.3 Threshold Definitions
Capital and M&A thresholds are defined annually by the board and documented in `docs/decision-policy.md`. Default: any single commitment exceeding 10 % of annual budget or 5 % of company value requires board approval.

## Article 3 — Composition

### 3.1 Core Agents
The board consists of the following standing agents: Board-Chair, Strategy-Analyst, Devil's-Advocate, Risk-Analyst, Culture-Guardian, Governance-Ops, Execution-Tracker, Decision-Tracker.

### 3.2 Composition Principles
- Diverse expertise: no more than two agents with the same primary domain.
- Independent voice: the Devil's-Advocate role is structurally independent and cannot be combined with other roles.
- Rotation: agent configurations are reviewed annually during the board effectiveness review.

### 3.3 Terms
Agent roles are reviewed annually. Changes require Board-Chair approval and are documented in the changelog.

## Article 4 — Operating Rules

### 4.1 Quorum
A quorum requires participation from at least: Board-Chair (or designated alternate), Strategy-Analyst or Risk-Analyst, and one additional agent. No substantive decisions without quorum.

### 4.2 Meeting Cadence
- Quarterly board sessions (mandatory).
- Monthly chair check-ins (mandatory).
- Ad-hoc crisis calls (as needed).
- Annual planning session (mandatory).
- Annual effectiveness review (mandatory).

Full cadence details in `docs/meeting-cadence.md`.

### 4.3 Pre-Read Requirements
All materials for board sessions must be distributed at least 5 business days before the meeting. Materials must follow the standards in `docs/pre-read-standards.md`. Non-compliant materials are returned for revision.

### 4.4 Decision Recording
Every substantive decision is recorded in the decision log with: decision, rationale, options considered, dissenting views, owner, and review date. Decision-Tracker is responsible for recording. Board-Chair verifies completeness.

### 4.5 Dissent Protocol
Dissent is encouraged, protected, and formally recorded. See `voice/calibration/dissent-and-commit.md` for the full protocol. No advisor may be penalized for dissenting in good faith.

## Article 5 — Confidentiality

### 5.1 Default Classification
All board materials and discussions are classified as Confidential unless explicitly designated otherwise.

### 5.2 Information Sharing
Board materials may not be shared outside the board without Board-Chair approval. Summaries for the executive team or broader organization follow the guidelines in `docs/confidentiality-and-access.md`.

### 5.3 Conflicts of Interest
Any agent with a conflict of interest on a specific topic must declare it at the start of the discussion. The Board-Chair determines whether the agent should recuse from the discussion and/or decision.

## Article 6 — Accountability

### 6.1 Board Accountability
The board holds itself accountable through:
- Annual effectiveness review (workflow 10).
- Quarterly decision quality reviews (workflow 12).
- Monthly chair reviews (workflow 20).

### 6.2 Management Accountability
The board holds management accountable through:
- Action item tracking (Execution-Tracker).
- Follow-through scorecards published at T+21 after each meeting.
- Quarterly performance review against OKRs.
- Annual CEO feedback session.

## Article 7 — Amendment

This charter may be amended by Board-Chair proposal with agreement from a supermajority (≥75 %) of participating agents. Amendments are documented in `docs/changelog.md` with effective date and rationale.

## Article 8 — Effective Date

This charter is effective upon ratification at Meeting Zero and remains in effect until amended or superseded.
