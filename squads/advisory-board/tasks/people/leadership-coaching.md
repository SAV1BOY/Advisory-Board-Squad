# Task: Leadership Coaching

## Objective
Identify executive development needs and establish coaching engagements to close critical leadership gaps. Board-level oversight ensures coaching investment translates to measurable improvement.

## Trigger
- Performance review identifies development gaps in a key executive
- Board effectiveness review highlights leadership concerns
- Executive self-identifies a coaching need
- New executive onboarding reveals adjustment challenges

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO for direct reports, Board Chair for CEO coaching
- **Support:** CHRO for coach selection and engagement management
- **Advisory:** Board sponsor assigned to monitor progress


## Agent Assignment
> Routing: see `config.yaml → routing → exec-hiring`

- **Lead:** talent-advisor (Patrick Lencioni)
- **Support:** culture-steward (Brene Brown), board-chair (Board Chair)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Development gaps identified and documented
- [ ] Budget for coaching engagement approved
- [ ] Executive is willing to engage in coaching
- [ ] Confidentiality framework established

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Identify specific development areas tied to business outcomes not vague improvement goals

2. **Step 2:** Select an executive coach with relevant expertise through CHRO vetted network

3. **Step 3:** Establish coaching engagement parameters including duration, cadence, confidentiality, and success metrics

4. **Step 4:** Executive and coach establish development plan with measurable milestones

5. **Step 5:** Conduct monthly check-ins between CHRO and executive on progress without violating coaching confidentiality

6. **Step 6:** Conduct 90-day formal review of coaching impact on observable leadership behaviors

7. **Step 7:** Board sponsor receives periodic updates on trajectory without detailed coaching content

8. **Step 8:** Coaching engagement agreement; Development plan with measurable milestones; Quarterly progress assessments; 6-month impact evaluation

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Structured Assessment:** Use consistent frameworks and rubrics to reduce bias and increase rigor
- **Data-Driven People Decisions:** Ground all people decisions in observable evidence, not gut feeling
- **Stakeholder Triangulation:** Gather input from multiple perspectives (manager, peers, reports, cross-functional) for balanced assessment

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment is grounded in specific, observable evidence
- [ ] Multiple perspectives gathered (not single-source input)
- [ ] Outcomes and recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Confidentiality maintained throughout the process
- [ ] Legal and compliance requirements met
- [ ] Timeline and owners assigned for all actions

## Output
- Development progress feeds into performance-review-exec
- Skills improvement may affect succession-planning assessments
- Coaching insights may inform culture initiatives
