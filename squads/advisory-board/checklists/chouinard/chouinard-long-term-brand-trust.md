# Chouinard Long-Term Brand Trust Checklist

## Purpose
Evaluates whether the organization builds brand trust through consistent actions rather than campaigns. Chouinard built one of the most trusted brands by making the brand a byproduct of authentic behavior, not a manufactured image. This checklist ensures that brand trust is earned through operational integrity, not advertising.

## When to Use
- During brand strategy reviews.
- When evaluating marketing campaigns for authenticity.
- When a decision could affect long-term brand perception.
- When brand metrics (NPS, trust scores) show changes.
- After a brand crisis or reputation event.

## Gate Criteria

### Brand-Behavior Alignment
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The brand promise matches the actual customer experience consistently.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Internal culture matches external brand positioning.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The company does not make claims it cannot substantiate.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Brand consistency is maintained across all touchpoints (product, service, communication, hiring).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Employees believe in the brand they represent.

### Trust Building
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization prioritizes actions that build trust over actions that generate short-term awareness.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Mistakes and shortcomings are addressed publicly and honestly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Customer feedback is solicited, taken seriously, and acted upon visibly.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization under-promises and over-delivers rather than the reverse.

### Long-Term Orientation
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Brand decisions are evaluated on a 5-10 year time horizon, not just the current quarter.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Short-term promotional tactics do not undermine long-term brand positioning.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization resists trends that conflict with brand identity, even when popular.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Brand equity is treated as a balance sheet asset that requires ongoing investment.

### Measurement and Protection
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Brand trust metrics are tracked and reviewed regularly (NPS, trust index, brand sentiment).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization has a rapid-response capability for brand-threatening events.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Brand protection is a responsibility of every function, not just marketing.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Partners and vendors are assessed for brand alignment before engagement.

## Red Flags
- Marketing messaging promises what operations cannot deliver.
- Brand trust scores are declining while marketing spend is increasing.
- Customer complaints are addressed privately but systemic issues are not fixed.
- The brand has changed its positioning three or more times in five years.
- Employees are cynical about the brand, indicating internal disconnect.
- Short-term promotions (heavy discounting, clickbait) erode brand positioning.

## Escalation Triggers
- A brand trust metric drops significantly in a single quarter.
- A brand crisis occurs due to behavior inconsistent with brand positioning.
- A major customer publicly challenges the brand's authenticity.
- Employee brand advocacy scores decline, indicating internal trust erosion.
- A competitor's brand surpasses the organization's in a key trust metric.
