# Org Health Turnaround: Diagnosis

## Objective
Identify root causes of organizational health decline through structured assessment across all culture dimensions.

## Inputs
- Culture health assessment data, skip-level interview findings, exit interview themes, engagement data.

## Process
1. Conduct deep-dive assessment: surveys, interviews, artifact analysis across all six culture dimensions.
2. Identify root causes versus symptoms using 5 Whys methodology.
3. Map structural contributors: incentives, processes, leadership behaviors, organizational design.
4. Identify bright spots: where is culture healthy and why?
5. Present findings to leadership with radical honesty.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/utilities/culture-health-rubric.md`, `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 2
- `lib/utilities/incentive-map.md`, `lib/patterns/postmortem-pattern.md`

## Output
Diagnosis document with root causes, structural contributors, bright spots, and severity assessment.
