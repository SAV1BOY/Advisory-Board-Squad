# Task: Competitive Response Plan

## Objective
Develop and execute a response plan when a competitor makes a significant move that threatens our market position, customer base, or strategic direction.

## Trigger
- Competitor launches a product that directly threatens our core offering
- Competitor raises significant funding specifically targeting our market
- Competitor acquires a company that changes competitive dynamics
- Key customer churns to a competitor citing product or pricing reasons
- Win rate drops significantly against a specific competitor

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO or Strategy Lead
- **Support:** Product, Sales, Marketing, Engineering
- **Advisory:** Board members with competitive strategy experience

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Competitive intelligence verified from multiple sources
- [ ] Impact assessment on our customers and pipeline complete
- [ ] Internal team briefed on the competitive development
- [ ] Financial data available to assess response investment options

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Assess the threat by quantifying the actual and potential impact on revenue, pipeline, customer retention, and market positioning

2. **Step 2:** Gather intelligence from customers, partners, and market sources to understand the competitor move depth and sustainability

3. **Step 3:** Classify the response urgency as immediate requiring action within days, short-term within weeks, or strategic within quarters

4. **Step 4:** Develop response options ranging from no response through tactical response to strategic counter-move, each with cost and timeline

5. **Step 5:** Evaluate whether response requires board approval based on investment level or strategic significance

6. **Step 6:** If board-level: prepare a strategy memo with response options; if management-level: brief the board for awareness

7. **Step 7:** Execute the approved response with clear milestones and measurement

8. **Step 8:** Competitive Response Memo; Updated competitive landscape; Response execution plan with milestones

## Frameworks to Apply
- **First Principles Analysis:** Break the problem down to fundamental truths before building solutions
- **Pre-Mortem:** Assume the initiative fails and work backward to identify likely causes
- **Reversibility Assessment:** Evaluate how reversible each option is — irreversible decisions deserve more scrutiny

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Analysis uses primary data sources, not assumptions
- [ ] Multiple options presented with quantified trade-offs
- [ ] Risks identified with specific mitigation plans
- [ ] Success metrics defined with kill criteria
- [ ] Board has a clear decision or direction to give
- [ ] Financial implications are modeled

## Output
- Response investments feed into capital allocation
- Product changes feed into product strategy
- Market intelligence updates competitive landscape report
