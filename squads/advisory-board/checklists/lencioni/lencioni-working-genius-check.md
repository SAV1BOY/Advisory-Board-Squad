# Lencioni Working Genius Check Checklist

## Purpose
Evaluates whether the organization is leveraging Lencioni's Working Genius model to ensure all six types of work required for any successful endeavor are represented and assigned to people who find them energizing. The six geniuses are: Wonder (asking big questions), Invention (creating solutions), Discernment (evaluating ideas), Galvanizing (inspiring action), Enablement (providing support), and Tenacity (completing the work). Teams fail when required geniuses are missing or when people are stuck doing work that drains them.

## When to Use
- When forming new teams for significant initiatives.
- When teams are underperforming despite individual talent.
- When individuals report frustration, burnout, or disengagement.
- During talent reviews and career development conversations.
- When redesigning workflows or team structures.

## Gate Criteria

### Genius Coverage
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: All six types of working genius are represented on the team.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Gaps in genius coverage are identified and addressed through hiring, reassignment, or development.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each phase of work (ideation, activation, implementation) has genius coverage.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team's genius distribution matches the work's requirements.

### Individual Alignment
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each team member's working genius is identified (through assessment or observation).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: People spend the majority of their time on work that aligns with their genius.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Working frustrations (tasks that drain energy) are identified and minimized.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Role design accounts for genius alignment, not just skill and availability.

### Team Dynamics
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team understands and appreciates each member's genius contribution.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Handoffs between genius types are smooth (wonder to invention, discernment to galvanizing, enablement to tenacity).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: No one genius type dominates team dynamics at the expense of others.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The team's genius balance is assessed when new members join or leave.

### Organizational Application
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The working genius framework is used in hiring to identify what the team actually needs.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Performance conversations account for the difference between working genius (energizing) and working competency (capable but draining).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Burnout patterns are analyzed through the lens of genius misalignment.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization values all six geniuses equally, not just the most visible ones.

## Red Flags
- A team has strong inventors but no one with tenacity, leading to brilliant ideas that never ship.
- A team has strong doers but no one with wonder or invention, leading to efficient execution of the wrong things.
- People are stuck doing work that drains them because no one has assessed genius fit.
- The organization values galvanizing and invention (visible geniuses) while undervaluing enablement and tenacity.
- Teams are formed solely based on functional skills without considering genius diversity.
- Burnout is common and traced to chronic genius misalignment.

## Escalation Triggers
- A critical initiative stalls because a required genius type is missing from the team.
- Talent attrition is traced to genius misalignment (people leaving because their work drains them).
- Team performance is consistently below expectations despite individual capability.
- The same type of work failure recurs (ideas without execution, execution without strategy) suggesting a genius gap.
- A leader is unable to adapt their approach despite feedback about genius imbalance in their team.
