# Org Health Turnaround: Execution

## Objective
Implement the structural and behavioral changes defined in the alignment and ritual phases with consistency and accountability.

## Inputs
- Leadership alignment commitments (02-alignment.md). Ritual plan (03-rituals.md). Structural changes (incentive redesigns, process changes).

## Process
1. Launch new rituals with clear communication about purpose and expectations.
2. Implement structural changes (incentive redesigns, reporting changes, process updates).
3. Leadership models new behaviors visibly and consistently.
4. Conduct monthly pulse surveys to track progress.
5. Address resistance directly using conflict-types.md for diagnosis.
6. Celebrate early wins and positive exemplars publicly.
7. Handle persistent resisters through direct conversation and, if necessary, transition.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 5 (Culture Change Plan)
- `lib/taxonomies/conflict-types.md`, `lib/patterns/escalation-pattern.md`

## Output
Monthly execution progress reports. Pulse survey results. Resistance log and resolution actions.
