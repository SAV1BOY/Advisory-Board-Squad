# Strategy Win Condition 30-60-90 Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that every strategic initiative has clear, measurable win conditions at 30, 60, and 90 days. Without near-term milestones, strategy becomes a distant aspiration rather than a disciplined execution commitment. This checklist bridges the gap between strategic intent and operational accountability.

## When to Use
- When translating strategy into execution plans.
- When launching new initiatives, products, or market entries.
- When onboarding a new executive accountable for strategic execution.
- During quarterly reviews to reset 30-60-90 targets.

## Gate Criteria

### 30-Day Win Conditions
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: At least two measurable milestones are defined for the first 30 days.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: 30-day milestones focus on foundation-building: team assembled, plan finalized, quick wins achieved.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: 30-day milestones are achievable and within the team's control.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Success criteria are specific enough that achievement is unambiguous.

### 60-Day Win Conditions
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: 60-day milestones represent meaningful progress toward the strategic objective.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: 60-day milestones include at least one external validation point (customer feedback, market signal).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Dependency risks for 60-day milestones are identified and mitigated.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The 60-day checkpoint includes a go/no-go assessment based on 30-day learnings.

### 90-Day Win Conditions
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: 90-day milestones represent outcomes, not just activities.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: 90-day milestones connect clearly to the longer-term strategic objective.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Success at 90 days is defined in terms that the board or advisory body can evaluate.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Kill or pivot criteria are defined for the 90-day review.

### Tracking and Accountability
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A single owner is accountable for each win condition.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Progress toward win conditions is reviewed weekly during the 90-day period.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Deviations from plan are flagged early with course-correction proposals.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Learnings from the 90-day cycle inform the next 90-day plan.

## Red Flags
- Win conditions are vague ("make progress on X") rather than measurable.
- No 30-day milestones exist; the first checkpoint is at 90 days.
- Win conditions are all internal activities with no external validation.
- The same win conditions are carried forward cycle after cycle without achievement.

## Escalation Triggers
- 30-day milestones are missed without a recovery plan.
- 60-day go/no-go assessment reveals the initiative is fundamentally off track.
- 90-day outcomes are significantly below expectations.
- The accountable owner cannot explain the variance between plan and actual.
