# Cross-Squad Handoff Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that work, decisions, and information flowing between squads or teams are transferred with sufficient context, accountability, and quality that nothing is lost in transition. Handoff failures are among the most common sources of execution breakdowns in organizations. This checklist prevents the gap where one team thinks they communicated clearly and the receiving team does not have what they need.

## When to Use
- When any deliverable, decision, or responsibility transitions from one squad or team to another.
- When a project enters a new phase with different team ownership.
- When advisory board recommendations need to be implemented by operational teams.
- During retrospectives of initiatives that experienced handoff failures.

## Gate Criteria

### Context Transfer
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A handoff document exists that includes: objective, background, current status, and open issues.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The rationale behind key decisions made prior to handoff is documented.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Constraints and non-obvious dependencies are explicitly stated.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Historical context (what was tried before, what failed) is included.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Contact information for subject matter experts is provided.

### Accountability Transfer
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The receiving team has a named owner who formally accepts the handoff.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The outgoing team's accountability end-date and the incoming team's start-date are agreed upon.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Open action items are transferred with status and original deadlines.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Escalation paths for post-handoff questions are defined.

### Quality Verification
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The receiving team has reviewed the handoff materials and confirmed they have what they need.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A handoff meeting has occurred where both teams discuss the work interactively.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The receiving team can articulate the objective and success criteria in their own words.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Any quality issues or incomplete work are flagged and a plan to address them is agreed upon.

### Communication and Stakeholders
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: All stakeholders are notified of the handoff and the new point of contact.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: External parties (customers, partners, vendors) affected by the handoff are informed.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Communication channels and meeting cadences are updated to reflect the new ownership.

## Red Flags
- The handoff is an email with attachments and no interactive discussion.
- The receiving team has not been involved in any prior discussions about the work.
- Critical information exists only in one person's head and has not been documented.
- The handoff is rushed due to the outgoing team's departure or deadline.
- No one explicitly accepts ownership of the handoff on the receiving side.
- Past handoffs between the same teams have resulted in dropped work.

## Escalation Triggers
- The receiving team discovers material information gaps within the first week.
- Stakeholders experience a visible service degradation during the transition.
- The outgoing team is no longer available and the receiving team cannot proceed without their input.
- A critical deadline is missed because the handoff was incomplete.
- Repeated handoff failures between the same teams suggest a systemic process issue.
