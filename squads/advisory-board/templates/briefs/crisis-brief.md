## Urgency / Priority

### Urgency Classification

| Level | Definition | Response Time | Board Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LEVEL 1 — CRITICAL** | Existential threat to company survival, imminent legal liability, or active safety risk | Immediate (within 1 hour) | Emergency session required |
| **LEVEL 2 — SEVERE** | Significant financial, operational, or reputational damage in progress | Within 4 hours | Emergency async decision or session within 24 hours |
| **LEVEL 3 — ELEVATED** | Material issue with potential to escalate if not contained | Within 24 hours | Briefing at next session with interim updates |
| **LEVEL 4 — WATCH** | Emerging situation that may become a crisis | Within 48 hours | Awareness brief, no action required yet |

## Post-Crisis Requirements

Once the immediate crisis is contained:
1. Full post-mortem within 14 days (use postmortem-report.md template).
2. Risk register update within 7 days.
3. Decision log updated with all emergency decisions within 48 hours.
4. Insurance claims filed within policy deadlines.
5. Board debrief session scheduled within 30 days.
6. "Lessons learned" document distributed to all operating squads.
