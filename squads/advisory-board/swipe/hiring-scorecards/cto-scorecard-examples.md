# CTO Scorecard Examples

## CTO Evaluation Dimensions

### Technical Vision and Strategy (Weight: 25%)
- Articulates a technology strategy aligned with business goals
- Makes effective build vs. buy vs. partner decisions
- Anticipates technology trends and positions the company accordingly
- Balances innovation investment with maintenance and stability
- **Rating scale:** 1 (no coherent tech strategy) to 5 (technology is a recognized competitive advantage)

### Product Delivery (Weight: 25%)
- Delivers product roadmap on time and with quality
- Manages technical debt without sacrificing velocity
- Establishes reliable engineering processes (CI/CD, testing, code review)
- Responds effectively to production incidents and outages
- **Rating scale:** 1 (frequent delays and quality issues) to 5 (consistently reliable, high-quality delivery)

### Engineering Team Leadership (Weight: 20%)
- Recruits and retains top engineering talent
- Creates a culture of engineering excellence and continuous improvement
- Develops technical leaders and creates career growth paths
- Manages team scaling through organizational stages (tribe → village → city)
- **Rating scale:** 1 (high attrition, weak team) to 5 (engineering team is a talent magnet)

### Architecture and Scalability (Weight: 15%)
- Designs systems that scale with business growth
- Makes pragmatic architecture decisions (not over-engineering, not under-engineering)
- Ensures security, reliability, and performance meet business requirements
- Manages infrastructure costs effectively
- **Rating scale:** 1 (frequent scaling issues) to 5 (architecture enables business growth seamlessly)

### Cross-Functional Collaboration (Weight: 15%)
- Communicates technical concepts effectively to non-technical stakeholders
- Partners productively with Product, Design, Sales, and Operations
- Translates business requirements into technical solutions
- Manages expectations about what's technically feasible and how long it takes
- **Rating scale:** 1 (engineering is a black box) to 5 (engineering is deeply integrated with the business)

## Stage-Specific CTO Criteria

### Early-Stage CTO
- Hands-on coding capability (still the primary contributor)
- Speed of shipping and iterating
- Technical co-founder credibility with investors and recruits
- Pragmatic architecture decisions (build for now, not for 10x scale you haven't reached)

### Growth-Stage CTO
- Transition from individual contributor to technical leader
- Building and managing engineering managers
- Establishing engineering processes that scale
- Managing increasing technical complexity and team size

### Late-Stage CTO
- Enterprise architecture and security leadership
- Vendor and partner management at scale
- Technology M&A evaluation
- Industry thought leadership and recruiting gravity
