# Task: Strategic Diagnosis

## Objective
Conduct a comprehensive diagnostic of the company's strategic position — market, competitive dynamics, internal capabilities, and strategic fit — to inform board-level strategy discussions and decisions.

## Trigger
- Annually as input to the annual planning session
- When performance significantly deviates from strategic plan (>20% miss on key metrics)
- When market conditions shift materially (new competitor, regulatory change, technology disruption)
- Pre-fundraise or pre-acquisition strategic validation

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO / Strategy Lead
- **Support:** Product, Sales, Finance, Engineering leadership
- **Advisory:** Board members with relevant domain expertise
- **Optional:** External strategy advisor for independent perspective

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current financial data available
- [ ] Customer data current (NPS, churn, feedback)
- [ ] Competitive intelligence collected
- [ ] Access to internal teams for interviews and data

## Steps

1. **Define the Diagnostic Scope (Week 1)**
   - Clarify the central strategic question driving this diagnosis
   - Determine scope: full strategic review or focused diagnostic on specific area
   - Identify the key stakeholders who must be interviewed
   - Set the timeline and deliverable expectations with the board

2. **External Analysis (Weeks 1-3)**
   - Market sizing: update TAM/SAM/SOM with current data and methodology
   - Competitive mapping: profile all direct, indirect, and emerging competitors
   - Customer segmentation analysis: which segments are growing, shrinking, and why
   - Regulatory and technology trend scan: what external forces will shape the next 2-3 years
   - Win/loss analysis: why are we winning and losing deals, and how is this changing

3. **Internal Analysis (Weeks 2-3)**
   - Capability assessment: where are we strong and where do we have gaps relative to our strategy
   - Resource allocation audit: where is money and talent actually going vs. stated priorities
   - Execution velocity assessment: are we shipping fast enough relative to the market
   - Moat assessment: update the moat tracker with current evidence
   - Culture and organizational health check: is the organization healthy enough to execute the strategy

4. **Synthesis (Week 4)**
   - Integrate external and internal findings into a coherent strategic picture
   - Identify the 3-5 most important strategic insights (things that should change how we think)
   - Develop strategic options with trade-offs for each
   - Apply pre-mortem to each strategic option: "If we pursue this and fail, the most likely reason is..."
   - Prepare the strategic diagnosis brief for the board

5. **Board Presentation and Discussion (Week 5)**
   - Present the diagnosis using the strategy-review-brief template
   - Facilitate structured discussion on strategic options
   - Capture board direction and decisions
   - Translate decisions into an updated strategic plan with measurable goals

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Porter's Five Forces:** Assess competitive intensity and attractiveness of the market
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done:** Revalidate the core customer problem we solve
- **Wardley Mapping:** Map the value chain to identify where to invest and where to commoditize
- **7 Powers (Helmer):** Assess which strategic power sources are available and being built
- **SWOT (structured):** Ensure balanced assessment of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats

## Checklists

### Diagnosis Quality Gate
- [ ] External analysis uses primary data (not just secondary reports)
- [ ] Internal analysis includes uncomfortable truths (not just strengths)
- [ ] Customer voice is directly included (not filtered through sales team only)
- [ ] Competitive analysis includes emerging and adjacent threats
- [ ] Strategic options include a "do nothing" baseline
- [ ] Each option has quantified trade-offs and pre-mortem analysis
- [ ] Resource allocation audit compares actual spend to stated priorities

## Output
- Strategic Diagnosis Report (15-25 pages)
- Strategy Review Brief for the board (using template)
- Updated competitive landscape report
- Updated moat tracker
- Strategic options memo with trade-offs

## Handoffs
- Board decisions feed into the strategic plan update
- Competitive findings update the competitive landscape report
- Moat findings update the moat tracker
- Resource allocation findings inform capital allocation review

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis completion time | <5 weeks from trigger | Calendar |
| Stakeholder interviews conducted | >10 internal + >5 external (customers, partners) | Count |
| Strategic options presented | >=3 (including status quo) | Count |
| Board engagement | All members actively contribute to discussion | Chair observation |
| Insight actionability | >80% of insights lead to specific strategic actions | Action tracking |
