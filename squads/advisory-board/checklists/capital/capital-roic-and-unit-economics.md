# Capital ROIC and Unit Economics Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the organization understands and tracks its return on invested capital and unit economics with intellectual honesty. ROIC tells you whether the business creates or destroys value for every dollar invested. Unit economics tell you whether each transaction is profitable at scale. Together they determine whether growth creates or destroys value.

## When to Use
- Monthly as part of financial review.
- Before approving any significant capital investment.
- When evaluating growth strategy (is growth value-creating or value-destroying).
- During fundraising to validate the financial narrative.
- When unit economics appear to be deteriorating.

## Gate Criteria

### Unit Economics Clarity
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Customer acquisition cost (CAC) is calculated accurately, including all related costs.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Customer lifetime value (LTV) is calculated with explicit churn and retention assumptions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: LTV:CAC ratio exceeds 3:1 or there is a credible plan to reach this threshold.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Payback period on CAC is defined and within acceptable range for the business model.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Unit economics are tracked by customer segment, channel, and cohort.

### ROIC Assessment
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Return on invested capital is calculated for the overall business and for major initiatives.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: ROIC is compared to the cost of capital to determine value creation or destruction.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: ROIC trends are tracked over time (improving, stable, declining).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Incremental ROIC (on new investments) is tracked separately from overall ROIC.

### Honesty and Rigor
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Calculations use fully loaded costs, not cherry-picked favorable allocations.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Assumptions underlying LTV calculations (churn rates, expansion revenue, price changes) are validated with data.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The analysis does not rely on future improvements that have not been demonstrated.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Cross-subsidization between segments is identified and understood.

### Decision Integration
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Unit economics inform pricing decisions.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Investments are evaluated against ROIC hurdle rates.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Growth is pursued only in segments where unit economics are positive or demonstrably improving.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The board receives unit economics and ROIC reporting at least quarterly.

## Red Flags
- The company is growing rapidly but unit economics are deteriorating.
- LTV calculations assume churn rates that are lower than actual experience.
- CAC does not include all acquisition-related costs (salaries, overhead, technology).
- ROIC has never been calculated or is calculated only for the whole company, hiding segment variation.
- The company cannot articulate its unit economics for its primary product.

## Escalation Triggers
- LTV:CAC ratio drops below 2:1 without a credible recovery plan.
- ROIC falls below cost of capital for two consecutive quarters.
- Unit economics are negative and the path to positive is more than 12 months away without a funded plan.
- Growth spending is increasing while unit economics are declining.
- The financial narrative to investors differs from internal unit economics reality.
