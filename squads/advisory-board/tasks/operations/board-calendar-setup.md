# Task: Board Meeting Calendar Setup

## Objective
Establish and maintain the annual board meeting calendar, ensuring a well-structured cadence of regular meetings, committee sessions, and strategic offsites that supports effective governance and timely decision-making throughout the year.

## Trigger
- Annual calendar setup (typically Q4 for the following year)
- New board member onboarding requiring calendar orientation
- Material change to meeting cadence or structure
- Post-board-effectiveness-review recommendations requiring calendar adjustments
- Change in company stage (e.g., pre-IPO) requiring enhanced governance cadence

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** Board Secretary or Board Ops lead
- **Support:** Board Chair, CEO, executive assistant to Chair and CEO
- **Advisory:** Board Chair for strategic session planning

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Board member availability collected for the year
- [ ] Key business calendar events identified (fiscal year end, annual planning, audit cycle)
- [ ] Prior year board calendar reviewed for effectiveness
- [ ] Regulatory and compliance meeting requirements identified
- [ ] Committee structure and membership confirmed

## Steps

1. **Requirements Gathering (Days 1-3)**
   - Review the governance charter or bylaws for minimum meeting frequency requirements
   - Identify mandatory agenda items by quarter (annual budget, audit review, compensation review, etc.)
   - Collect board member availability and scheduling constraints
   - Identify key business milestones that drive board timing (quarter close, annual planning, fundraising)
   - Review feedback from the prior year on meeting frequency, duration, and format

2. **Calendar Architecture Design (Days 3-7)**
   - Design the annual meeting cadence — regular meetings, committee meetings, offsites
   - Map mandatory agenda items to specific meetings throughout the year
   - Ensure adequate spacing between meetings for preparation and follow-up
   - Designate one meeting as the annual strategy offsite (typically full-day or multi-day)
   - Schedule committee meetings in advance of full board meetings to feed recommendations
   - Reserve emergency session slots (not scheduled, but dates identified for rapid convening)

3. **Meeting Specification (Days 7-10)**
   - For each meeting, define: type, duration, format (in-person / virtual / hybrid), and primary topics
   - Assign pre-read deadlines for each meeting (standard: 5-7 business days prior)
   - Define the preparation timeline for each meeting — who needs to produce what by when
   - Establish the minutes and action item distribution timeline post-meeting
   - Identify guest presenters or external advisors needed for specific sessions

4. **Calendar Distribution and Confirmation (Days 10-14)**
   - Distribute the proposed calendar to all board members for confirmation
   - Resolve any scheduling conflicts
   - Book venues and arrange logistics for in-person sessions
   - Set up recurring calendar invitations with all relevant details
   - Distribute the finalized calendar with the annual board operations guide

5. **Ongoing Calendar Management (Continuous)**
   - Monitor for scheduling conflicts and manage changes
   - Send preparation reminders at defined intervals before each meeting
   - Track pre-read distribution compliance
   - Evaluate meeting effectiveness after each session
   - Propose mid-year adjustments if needed based on business events

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Governance Calendar Best Practices:** Map regulatory, strategic, and operational cycles
- **Operational Excellence:** Standardized processes with defined cadence and quality standards
- **Continuous Improvement:** Post-meeting feedback incorporated into future calendar design

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Calendar meets all governance and regulatory minimum requirements
- [ ] All board members confirmed availability for all scheduled meetings
- [ ] Mandatory agenda items mapped to specific meetings across the year
- [ ] Pre-read and preparation timelines defined for each meeting
- [ ] Committee meetings scheduled to feed into full board meetings
- [ ] In-person meeting logistics booked and confirmed
- [ ] Calendar distributed and accepted by all participants

## Output
- Annual board meeting calendar (dates, times, locations, formats)
- Meeting-by-meeting agenda topic allocation
- Preparation timeline for each meeting
- Board operations guide for the year
- Calendar invitations sent and confirmed

## Handoffs
- Calendar feeds into meeting-prep task for each scheduled meeting
- Strategy offsite feeds into quarterly-offsite-agenda template usage
- Committee schedule feeds into specific committee workflows
- Preparation timelines feed into quarterly-board-review and annual-planning-session tasks
- Calendar effectiveness feeds into board-effectiveness-review task

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Board member attendance | >90% across all meetings | Attendance records |
| Pre-read distribution on time | 100% of meetings | Distribution log |
| Meeting start on time | 100% | Meeting records |
| Calendar changes (unplanned) | <2 per year | Change log |
| Board member satisfaction with cadence | >4/5 | Annual board survey |
