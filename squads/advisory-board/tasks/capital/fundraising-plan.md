# Task: Fundraising Plan

## Objective
Develop and execute a fundraising strategy that secures capital on favorable terms with minimal distraction to the operating business. Ensure full board alignment on approach, terms, and process.

## Trigger
- Runway drops below 12 months and company needs additional capital
- Strategic opportunity requires capital beyond current reserves
- Market conditions are favorable for raising (not waiting for necessity)
- Annual planning identifies capital needs for the next phase

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO
- **Support:** CFO (model, data room, financial narrative), Legal (term sheet, documentation)
- **Advisory:** Board members (introductions, references, signaling, term negotiation)


## Agent Assignment

- **Lead:** capital-allocator (Charlie Munger)
- **Support:** board-chair (Board Chair), strategic-advisor (Peter Thiel), partnership-broker (Reid Hoffman)

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Clear strategic narrative and use of proceeds plan
- [ ] Financial model updated and stress-tested
- [ ] Key metrics trending in a direction that supports the raise
- [ ] Board aligned on the need to raise and general parameters
- [ ] Legal counsel engaged

## Steps

1. **Fundraise Strategy Alignment with Board (Week 1)**
   - Present fundraising memo to the board using the fundraising-memo template
   - Align on: target amount, valuation range, instrument type, timeline, investor profile
   - Define walk-away terms: minimum acceptable valuation, unacceptable deal terms
   - Identify specific board member asks (introductions, references, signal)
   - Board approves the fundraise process

2. **Preparation (Weeks 1-3)**
   - Build the investor pitch narrative and supporting deck
   - Prepare the data room with all required documents
   - Update the financial model with current data and projections
   - Prepare answers for the 50 most likely investor questions
   - Brief the team on the fundraise and set expectations for potential diligence

3. **Investor Outreach (Weeks 3-5)**
   - Execute warm introductions through board and network
   - Begin coffee meetings and initial conversations with target investors
   - Manage pipeline: track interest level, next steps, and timeline for each investor
   - CEO provides weekly updates to the board on process status

4. **Formal Process (Weeks 5-9)**
   - Partner meetings with interested investors
   - Deep-dive sessions on financials, product, and team
   - Customer and personal reference calls
   - Maintain competitive tension between interested investors

5. **Term Sheet Negotiation (Weeks 9-11)**
   - Evaluate term sheets with legal counsel and board
   - Negotiate terms per the board-approved parameters
   - If terms are outside approved parameters: return to board for revised guidance
   - Select the winning term sheet

6. **Due Diligence and Close (Weeks 11-16)**
   - Facilitate investor due diligence (legal, financial, technical)
   - Negotiate and finalize definitive agreements
   - Board approves final terms and authorizes signing
   - Wire transfer and close

## Frameworks to Apply
- **BATNA Analysis:** Know the best alternative to a negotiated agreement before entering negotiations
- **Signal Management:** Control information flow to maintain competitive dynamics
- **Parallel Process:** Run multiple investor conversations simultaneously

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Board has approved fundraise parameters (amount, valuation range, terms)
- [ ] Data room is complete and organized
- [ ] Financial model is current and stress-tested
- [ ] At least 10 qualified investors in the pipeline
- [ ] Existing investors are aligned and participating (or their position is understood)
- [ ] Team is shielded from fundraise distraction
- [ ] Walk-away terms are defined before negotiations begin

## Output
- Signed term sheet
- Completed fundraise with capital in the bank
- Updated cap table
- Board communication on terms and use of proceeds
- Post-close investor onboarding

## Handoffs
- Capital deployment per approved use of proceeds (capital-allocation-review)
- New investor relationship management (if board seat: board-composition update)
- Updated runway model with new capital
- Stakeholder communication (stakeholder-update-report)

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Process duration (start to close) | <16 weeks | Calendar |
| CEO time allocation to fundraise | <40% of time during active process | Time tracking |
| Business performance during raise | No material decline in key metrics | Dashboard |
| Terms achieved vs. target | Within board-approved range | Term sheet review |
| Investor quality | Lead investor meets target profile criteria | Board assessment |
