| Rating | Financial | Growth | Product | People |

| **Amber** | 5-15% below plan, runway 6-9 months | Growth rate 10-25% below target | NPS 20-40, uptime 99.5-99.9% | Attrition 15-25%, engagement 55-70 |
| **Red** | >15% below plan, runway <6 months | Growth rate >25% below target or negative | NPS <20, uptime <99.5% | Attrition >25%, engagement <55 |
