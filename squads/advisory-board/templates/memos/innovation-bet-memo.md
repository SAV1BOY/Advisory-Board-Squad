# Innovation Bet Decision Memo

## Template Guidelines
**Word Limit:** 1,000 words maximum (excluding headers and tables)
**Reading Time:** Under 7 minutes
**Use When:** Proposing a new innovation investment, requesting additional funding for an existing bet, or recommending the graduation, pivot, or termination of an innovation initiative.

### Executive Summary (75-100 words)
State the innovation bet, the decision being requested, the investment amount, and the strategic rationale. If this is an existing bet, include the current status and why a decision is needed now.

---

### Innovation Thesis (200-250 words)
Articulate the core thesis for this bet:
1. **The Insight:** What customer, market, or technology insight drives this bet? What do we believe that the market does not yet fully appreciate?
2. **The Opportunity:** What is the potential value if this thesis proves correct? Size the addressable opportunity.
3. **The Experiment:** What are we testing? Define the hypothesis clearly enough that it can be validated or invalidated.
4. **The Right to Win:** Why are we better positioned than others to pursue this? What capabilities, assets, or relationships give us an edge?
