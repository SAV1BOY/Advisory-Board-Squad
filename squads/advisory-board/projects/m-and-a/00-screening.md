# M&A: Screening

## Objective
Establish acquisition criteria and evaluate potential targets against a structured framework before committing resources to detailed evaluation.

## Inputs
- Strategic plan identifying gaps that acquisition could fill. Market map of potential targets. Financial capacity for acquisition.

## Process
1. Define acquisition criteria: strategic fit, size range, technology/capability gap filled, geographic relevance, cultural compatibility.
2. Build a target universe of 20-50 potential companies.
3. Apply initial screening filters: strategic fit, size, price range, availability.
4. Narrow to 5-10 targets for preliminary evaluation.
5. For each shortlisted target: summarize strategic rationale, estimate value range, assess feasibility.
6. Present shortlist to board for approval to proceed with detailed evaluation.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/strategy-blocks.md` (strategic rationale)
- `lib/utilities/moat-strength-rubric.md` (target moat assessment)
- `lib/components/decision-memo-blocks.md`

## Output
Screening report with criteria, target universe, and prioritized shortlist. Board approval to evaluate top targets.
