# M&A: Integration

## Objective
Execute the post-acquisition integration to capture the value thesis while retaining key people and preserving what made the target valuable.

## Inputs
- Completed acquisition. Integration plan from diligence phase. Target's team and assets. Retention agreements.

## Process
1. Launch integration with a 100-day plan covering: people, product, technology, customers, and operations.
2. Communicate the acquisition to all stakeholders: both companies' teams, customers, partners, and the market.
3. Execute retention plans for key personnel immediately.
4. Begin technology and product integration per the planned approach.
5. Monitor customer sentiment on both sides of the acquisition.
6. Track integration milestones weekly for the first 100 days.
7. Conduct 30, 60, and 90-day integration reviews.
8. Address cultural integration using culture-blocks.md frameworks.
9. Measure value capture against the original acquisition thesis.
10. Conduct a 6-month integration postmortem.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/culture-blocks.md` Block 6 (Cultural Integration)
- `lib/components/risk-blocks.md` (integration risk tracking)
- `scripts/tracking/action-item-tracker.md`
- `lib/patterns/postmortem-pattern.md` (integration postmortem)

## Output
100-day integration plan executed. Milestone reviews completed. Value capture tracked. Integration postmortem with lessons.

## Next Phase
Transition to normal operations with acquired assets fully integrated.
