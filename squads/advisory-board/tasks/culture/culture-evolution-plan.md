# Task: Culture Evolution Plan

## Objective
Proactively evolve the company's culture to support its next stage of growth. Culture that worked at 20 people may not work at 200. Plan and manage cultural evolution intentionally rather than letting it happen accidentally.

## Trigger
- Annual strategic planning identifies culture as an enabler or blocker of strategy
- Company is approaching a stage transition such as scaling from startup to growth stage
- Acquisition or merger requires cultural integration
- Culture audit identifies systemic issues requiring sustained intervention
- Board raises concerns about culture trajectory

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CEO with CHRO support
- **Support:** Functional leaders, culture champions across the organization
- **Advisory:** Board members with organizational development expertise

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Articulate the target culture by defining what the culture needs to look like to support the next stage strategy

2. **Step 2:** Map the gap between current culture and target culture across all key dimensions

3. **Step 3:** Identify cultural elements to preserve, elements to strengthen, and elements to let go of or change

4. **Step 4:** Design interventions across four levers including leadership behavior, organizational structure, incentive and reward systems, and rituals and communication

5. **Step 5:** Build a phased implementation plan recognizing that culture change takes 12-24 months minimum

6. **Step 6:** Establish measurement framework with leading indicators such as behavior change and lagging indicators such as engagement scores

7. **Step 7:** Present the culture evolution plan to the board for endorsement and resource commitment

8. **Step 8:** Execute with quarterly progress reviews and course corrections

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Competing Values Framework:** Assess culture across clan, adhocracy, market, and hierarchy dimensions
- **Edgar Schein's Three Levels:** Analyze culture at the artifact, espoused values, and underlying assumptions levels
- **Behavioral Indicators:** Ground all cultural assessment in observable behaviors, not aspirational statements

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Assessment combines quantitative data and qualitative insights
- [ ] Multiple organizational levels and functions represented in data collection
- [ ] Findings distinguish between symptoms and root causes
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Success metrics defined with measurement plan
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (culture change takes quarters, not weeks)
- [ ] Leadership commitment secured before proceeding

## Output
- Culture Evolution Plan document
- Phased implementation roadmap
- Measurement framework
- Quarterly progress reports
- Board endorsement
