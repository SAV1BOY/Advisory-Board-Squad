# Task: Runway Scenario Planning

## Objective
Model multiple financial scenarios to ensure the company understands its runway under various conditions. Provide the board with clear visibility into financial resilience and triggers for action.

## Trigger
- Monthly as part of financial management discipline
- When cash drops below 9 months of runway
- When revenue significantly misses plan (>15% miss)
- Before any major spending decision
- When considering a fundraise

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CFO
- **Support:** CEO, Department heads (for cost reduction scenarios)
- **Advisory:** Board finance committee

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current cash position and burn rate confirmed
- [ ] Revenue pipeline and forecast available
- [ ] Committed and discretionary costs identified
- [ ] Collection timeline data available
- [ ] Outstanding payables and receivables current

## Steps

1. **Baseline Model (Day 1)**
   - Document current cash position, monthly burn, and revenue trajectory
   - Calculate runway at current burn with zero revenue growth
   - Calculate runway at current burn with planned revenue growth
   - Identify all committed costs (cannot be reduced without contractual/legal action)

2. **Scenario Development (Days 1-3)**
   - **Scenario A: Plan Case** — Revenue and costs hit plan
   - **Scenario B: Upside** — Revenue exceeds plan by 20%, costs at plan
   - **Scenario C: Mild Stress** — Revenue misses plan by 20%, costs at plan
   - **Scenario D: Severe Stress** — Revenue misses by 40%, no new customers
   - **Scenario E: Survival Mode** — Revenue drops 50%, maximum cost reduction activated

3. **Cost Reduction Options (Days 2-4)**
   - Tier 1 cuts (immediate, minimal impact): discretionary spend, contractor reduction, hiring freeze
   - Tier 2 cuts (moderate, some impact): headcount reduction <15%, vendor renegotiation, office downsizing
   - Tier 3 cuts (severe, significant impact): headcount reduction >15%, product line sunset, market exit
   - For each tier: quantify monthly savings, implementation timeline, and strategic impact

4. **Trigger Framework (Day 4)**
   - Define specific financial triggers for each action tier
   - Example: "If cash drops below $X or runway drops below Y months, activate Tier 1 cuts"
   - Pre-approve triggers with the CEO so that action can be taken without board delay
   - Board approves the trigger framework (not individual cuts)

5. **Board Presentation (Day 5)**
   - Present all scenarios with visual cash runway charts
   - Present trigger framework for board approval
   - Highlight the scenario that most closely matches current trajectory
   - Recommend any immediate actions if current trajectory is concerning

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Scenario Planning:** Base, stress, and survival scenarios with quantified cash impact
- **Trigger-Based Management:** Pre-defined action triggers to avoid delayed response
- **Fixed vs. Variable Cost Analysis:** Understand cost structure flexibility

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] All five scenarios modeled with monthly cash projections
- [ ] Cost reduction tiers are specific (not generic "reduce costs")
- [ ] Trigger thresholds are quantified and actionable
- [ ] Revenue assumptions are based on pipeline data, not hopes
- [ ] Model has been stress-tested for accuracy

## Output
- Runway scenario model (5 scenarios, monthly projections)
- Cost reduction playbook (3 tiers)
- Trigger framework (pre-approved action thresholds)
- Visual cash runway charts for board

## Handoffs
- Trigger breaches activate cost reduction playbook
- Severe scenarios may trigger fundraising-plan development
- Model informs all capital allocation decisions
- Updated monthly and presented quarterly to board

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Model accuracy | Actual cash within 10% of forecast scenario | Monthly comparison |
| Runway maintenance | Always >6 months under base case | Monthly monitoring |
| Trigger framework currency | Updated monthly | Review date tracking |
| Board confidence in financial visibility | >4/5 satisfaction | Board survey |
