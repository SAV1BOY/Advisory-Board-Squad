# Partnership Deal: Terms

## Objective
Negotiate and define the deal structure, economic model, governance, and exit provisions.

## Inputs
- Due diligence findings (01-evaluation.md). Legal counsel. Business objectives.

## Process
1. Define scope: precisely what each party will do and deliver.
2. Structure economic model: revenue share, licensing, cost sharing.
3. Define IP ownership and data sharing terms.
4. Set performance commitments and SLAs.
5. Design governance structure: steering committee, meeting cadence, escalation path.
6. Define term, renewal, exit clauses, and dispute resolution.
7. Legal review of all terms.

## Tools/Frameworks
- `lib/components/partnership-blocks.md` (Block 3: Terms and Structure)
- `lib/components/decision-memo-blocks.md` (decision memo for partnership approval)

## Output
Term sheet or partnership agreement draft. Board recommendation.
