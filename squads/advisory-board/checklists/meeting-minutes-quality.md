# Meeting Minutes Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that meeting minutes are a reliable record of decisions, commitments, and rationale that enable accountability and institutional memory. Minutes are not transcripts; they are curated records of what was decided, why, by whom, and what happens next. This checklist prevents the failure mode of meetings that produce discussion but no documented decisions or follow-through.

## When to Use
- After every board meeting, advisory session, or significant leadership meeting.
- Before minutes are distributed to attendees for review.
- During annual audits of governance documentation.

## Gate Criteria

### Decision Recording
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Every decision made during the meeting is recorded with the exact resolution language.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each decision records who proposed, who seconded (for formal votes), and the vote count.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Abstentions and dissenting votes are recorded by name.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The rationale for each decision is summarized in one to two sentences.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Decisions are numbered sequentially for easy reference in future meetings.

### Action Items
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Every action item has a single named owner (not a team or committee).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Every action item has a specific deadline.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Action items are written as concrete deliverables, not vague commitments.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Action items from the previous meeting are reviewed with status updates.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Overdue action items are flagged and escalation plans noted.

### Completeness and Accuracy
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: All agenda items are addressed in the minutes, including items deferred to future meetings.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Key discussion points are summarized without attributing specific statements to individuals (unless requested).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Material information presented during the meeting is referenced or attached.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Conflicts of interest declared during the meeting are recorded.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Attendees and absentees are listed.

### Distribution and Approval
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Draft minutes are distributed within 48 hours of the meeting.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A review and approval process with a defined timeline exists.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Approved minutes are stored in the designated governance repository.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Minutes are accessible to all authorized parties but protected from unauthorized access.

## Red Flags
- Minutes read as a transcript rather than a decision record.
- Action items have no owners or deadlines.
- Minutes are distributed more than one week after the meeting.
- Dissenting views are omitted or softened.
- Minutes from the last three meetings have unresolved action items with no escalation.
- No formal approval process exists for minutes.

## Escalation Triggers
- A dispute arises about what was decided because minutes are ambiguous.
- Minutes are discovered to be materially inaccurate after approval.
- Legal or regulatory proceedings require meeting records and the minutes are inadequate.
- Action items are consistently not completed and the issue is not addressed in subsequent meetings.
- A board member formally objects to the accuracy of the minutes.
