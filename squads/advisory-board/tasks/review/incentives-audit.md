# Task: Incentives Audit

## Objective
Review compensation, bonus, equity, and non-financial incentive structures to ensure they drive desired behaviors and align with company strategy and values. Identify perverse incentives that may be driving unintended behavior.

## Trigger
- Annually before compensation cycle
- When strategy changes require different behaviors
- When performance review data reveals incentive-behavior misalignment
- When the board questions whether incentives are driving the right outcomes
- After a values violation that may be incentive-driven

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** CHRO with CFO
- **Support:** CEO, functional leaders, compensation consultant if engaged
- **Advisory:** Board compensation committee

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Current compensation and incentive structure documentation
- [ ] Performance review data
- [ ] Strategy and values documentation
- [ ] Market benchmark data
- [ ] Budget for compensation adjustments

## Steps

1. **Step 1:** Map current incentive structures across all levels including base, bonus, equity, recognition, and non-monetary incentives

2. **Step 2:** For each incentive element identify the specific behavior it is designed to drive and assess whether it actually drives that behavior

3. **Step 3:** Identify perverse incentives where the incentive structure encourages behavior that conflicts with strategy, values, or long-term health

4. **Step 4:** Assess internal equity across roles, levels, functions, and demographic groups

5. **Step 5:** Benchmark incentive structures against market for competitiveness and retention effectiveness

6. **Step 6:** Develop recommendations for realignment including specific changes to bonus metrics, equity vesting, commission structures, and recognition programs

7. **Step 7:** Present findings to the board compensation committee with specific recommendations and cost implications

8. **Step 8:** Incentives Audit Report; Perverse incentive identification; Realignment recommendations with cost model; Updated incentive structure documentation

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Retrospective Analysis:** Structured look-back that separates process quality from outcome quality
- **Continuous Improvement Cycle:** Plan-Do-Check-Act applied to governance and management processes
- **Balanced Scorecard:** Multi-dimensional assessment that prevents over-indexing on any single metric

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Review is evidence-based with specific data points, not impressions
- [ ] Both positive and negative findings are presented honestly
- [ ] Root causes identified, not just symptoms
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and resourced
- [ ] Historical comparison included to show trends
- [ ] Board has clear insight into what needs to change

## Output
- Compensation changes feed into budget planning
- Equity changes may require board or shareholder approval
- Incentive realignment feeds into performance-review-exec criteria
- Values alignment feeds into culture-evolution-plan
