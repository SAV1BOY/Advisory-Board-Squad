# Task: Regulatory Compliance Check

## Objective
Review the organization's regulatory compliance posture across all applicable jurisdictions and domains. Identify compliance gaps, assess the risk of non-compliance, ensure required filings and certifications are current, and recommend remediation actions.

## Trigger
- Quarterly compliance review cycle
- Entry into a new market or jurisdiction
- Launch of a product or feature with regulatory implications (data handling, financial transactions, healthcare)
- Regulatory change or new legislation in applicable jurisdictions
- Customer or partner compliance inquiry or audit request
- Incident or near-miss with compliance implications
- Pre-fundraising or pre-acquisition due diligence

## Agents Involved
- **Lead:** General Counsel or Compliance Officer
- **Support:** CFO, CTO (for data and technology compliance), CHRO (for employment law), functional compliance owners
- **Advisory:** Board members with legal or regulatory expertise, external legal counsel

## Pre-Requisites
- [ ] Regulatory register — list of all applicable regulations by jurisdiction and domain
- [ ] Current compliance status for each regulation
- [ ] Filing and certification calendar with deadlines
- [ ] Audit findings and remediation status from prior reviews
- [ ] Data processing and privacy impact assessments current
- [ ] Third-party compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.) status

## Steps

1. **Regulatory Landscape Review (Days 1-5)**
   - Update the regulatory register — add any new regulations that apply since the last review
   - Identify regulatory changes in applicable jurisdictions (new laws, amended regulations, enforcement guidance)
   - Assess the impact of any regulatory changes on current operations
   - Review industry-specific regulatory developments (fintech, healthtech, edtech, etc.)
   - Identify upcoming regulatory deadlines (filings, renewals, certifications)

2. **Compliance Status Assessment (Days 5-10)**
   - Review compliance status for each applicable regulation:
     - Data privacy (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, etc.)
     - Financial regulations (if applicable — SOX, PCI-DSS, banking regulations)
     - Employment law (labor standards, immigration, workplace safety)
     - Industry-specific regulations
     - Corporate governance requirements
   - Verify all required filings and registrations are current
   - Review certification status and renewal timelines (SOC 2, ISO, etc.)
   - Assess compliance of third-party vendors and partners

3. **Gap Analysis and Risk Assessment (Days 10-15)**
   - Identify compliance gaps — areas where current practices do not fully meet regulatory requirements
   - Score each gap by severity (impact of non-compliance) and likelihood of detection/enforcement
   - Assess the financial exposure of each gap — fines, penalties, litigation risk
   - Identify gaps that require immediate remediation vs. planned remediation
   - Review prior audit findings — are all remediation items closed?

4. **Remediation Planning (Days 15-18)**
   - For each identified gap, develop a remediation plan with:
     - Specific actions required
     - Owner responsible
     - Timeline for completion
     - Cost estimate
     - Dependencies
   - Prioritize remediation by risk severity and regulatory deadline pressure
   - Identify quick wins — gaps that can be closed immediately with minimal effort
   - Estimate ongoing compliance costs for new regulatory requirements

5. **Board Reporting (Days 18-22)**
   - Prepare a compliance posture summary for the board
   - Highlight material compliance risks and their financial exposure
   - Present remediation plans and resource requirements
   - Request board direction on any compliance decisions with strategic implications
   - Update the risk register with compliance-related risks

## Frameworks to Apply
- **Compliance Risk Matrix:** Severity of non-compliance x likelihood of enforcement
- **Defense in Depth:** Multiple layers of compliance controls
- **Regulatory Horizon Scanning:** Proactively identify regulatory changes before they take effect
- **Third-Party Risk Management:** Extend compliance assessment to vendors and partners

## Checklists

### Quality Gate
- [ ] Regulatory register is comprehensive and current
- [ ] All applicable regulations assessed with current compliance status
- [ ] All required filings and certifications verified as current or scheduled
- [ ] Gaps identified with severity scoring and financial exposure estimates
- [ ] Remediation plans have owners, timelines, and cost estimates
- [ ] Third-party and vendor compliance assessed
- [ ] Board has visibility into material compliance risks

## Output
- Regulatory compliance status report
- Updated regulatory register
- Gap analysis with risk scoring
- Remediation plan with prioritization
- Filing and certification calendar (updated)
- Board compliance briefing

## Handoffs
- Compliance risks feed into risk-register-update task
- Remediation costs feed into budget-review task
- Technology compliance gaps feed into technology-stack-review task
- Data privacy findings feed into cyber-and-fraud-gate task
- Employment law findings feed into compensation-review and team-health-check tasks
- Compliance posture supports fundraising-plan and m-and-a-evaluation due diligence

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory filing compliance | 100% on time | Filing calendar |
| Open compliance gaps | Zero critical or high gaps open beyond remediation deadline | Gap tracker |
| Certification currency | All certifications current | Certification log |
| Audit finding closure rate | 100% within agreed timelines | Audit tracker |
| Regulatory change response time | Assessed within 30 days of regulatory change | Change log |
