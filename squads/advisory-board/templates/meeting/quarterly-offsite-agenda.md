## Day 1 Agenda

### Session 1: State of the Business (90 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** CEO and CFO
**Format:** 30-minute presentation + 60-minute discussion

- Business performance review against annual plan
- Financial position, runway, and key metrics
- Customer and market highlights
- Team and organizational health
- Top 3 wins and top 3 concerns since last offsite

**Break (15 minutes)**

### Session 2: Strategic Deep Dive (120 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** [Presenter]
**Pre-Read:** [Strategic brief or memo]
**Format:** 20-minute context + 100-minute structured discussion

**Topic:** [Primary strategic topic — e.g., market expansion, product strategy pivot, competitive response]

**Discussion Framework:**
1. Context and framing (assumes pre-read completed)
2. Round-robin: Each member shares initial position (2 min each)
3. Open debate and stress-testing
4. Decision or direction capture

**Working Lunch (60 minutes)**
Informal discussion or guest speaker: [Topic / Speaker]

### Session 3: Innovation and Growth (90 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** CTO / CPO
**Pre-Read:** Innovation portfolio brief

- Innovation portfolio review and health check
- Horizon 2 and 3 bets — progress, learnings, recommendations
- New opportunity evaluation
- Resource allocation discussion

**Break (15 minutes)**

### Session 4: Risk and Governance (60 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** Board Chair
- Risk register review and emerging threats
- Board effectiveness and governance improvements
- Compliance and regulatory update

**Day 1 Wrap-Up (15 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM**
- Capture decisions made and open items
- Preview Day 2 agenda

**Evening:** Team dinner — [Location and time]

---

## Day 2 Agenda

### Session 5: People and Organization (90 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** CEO / CHRO
- Leadership team assessment and development
- Succession planning review
- Culture health check
- Talent pipeline and key hires
- Compensation and equity discussion (executive session if needed)

**Break (15 minutes)**

### Session 6: Forward Planning (90 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** CEO
**Format:** Collaborative working session

- Strategic priorities for next quarter
- Key initiatives and resource allocation
- OKR or goal-setting for next period
- Board engagement model and upcoming decisions

**Break (15 minutes)**

### Session 7: Executive Session (30 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** Board Chair
**Attendees:** Board members only (management excused)

### Session 8: Closing (30 minutes) — HH:MM - HH:MM
**Lead:** Chair

- Summary of all decisions made across both days
- Confirm action items with owners and deadlines
- Feedback on offsite format and effectiveness
- Confirm next quarterly offsite date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Adjournment

## Logistics

### Pre-Offsite
- [ ] Venue booked and confirmed
- [ ] Travel and accommodation arranged for all attendees
- [ ] Pre-read materials distributed by deadline
- [ ] AV and presentation equipment confirmed
- [ ] Dietary and accessibility requirements confirmed
- [ ] Emergency contact list distributed

### During Offsite
- [ ] Decision tracker ready (live document)
- [ ] Action item template ready
- [ ] Photographer arranged (if applicable)
- [ ] Breakout rooms available for small-group work

### Post-Offsite
- [ ] Decision summary distributed within 48 hours
- [ ] Action items entered in tracker within 24 hours
- [ ] Full meeting notes distributed within 5 business days
- [ ] Follow-up materials sent as committed
- [ ] Offsite effectiveness survey sent to attendees
