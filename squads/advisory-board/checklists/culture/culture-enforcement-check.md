# Culture Enforcement Check Checklist

## Purpose
Verifies that cultural values are enforced through organizational systems, not just good intentions. Culture is what you reward and what you punish. This checklist ensures that enforcement mechanisms exist and are applied consistently.

## When to Use
- When assessing whether culture is operational or aspirational.
- When reviewing HR systems for cultural alignment.
- When a values violation occurs and the response is being determined.
- During organizational health reviews.

## Gate Criteria

### Reward Systems
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Recognition programs explicitly acknowledge values-aligned behavior.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Promotion criteria include demonstrated values alignment with specific evidence required.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Compensation decisions consider cultural contribution, not just financial results.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Stories of values-driven behavior are shared publicly within the organization.

### Accountability Systems
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Performance management includes values-based assessment.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Consequences for values violations are defined and known.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Consequences are applied consistently regardless of the violator's seniority or performance.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: A progressive discipline process for values violations is documented.

### Hiring and Separation
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Interview processes include structured values assessment.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Offer decisions require values assessment sign-off, not just skills assessment.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Separation decisions for values violations are treated as seriously as performance-based separations.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Exit interviews probe for culture and values experience.

### Monitoring and Feedback
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Regular pulse surveys measure whether values are experienced in daily work.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Anonymous reporting channels exist for values violations.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Cultural health data is reviewed by leadership as frequently as financial data.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The enforcement system itself is assessed for fairness and consistency.

## Red Flags
- High performers who violate values are retained and even promoted.
- No one can describe the consequences for violating company values.
- Enforcement varies dramatically between departments or levels.
- The anonymous reporting channel receives zero reports (suggesting distrust, not perfection).
- Cultural enforcement is delegated entirely to HR with no leadership involvement.

## Escalation Triggers
- A values violation by a senior leader is not addressed within 30 days.
- A pattern of values violations emerges in a specific team or function.
- Legal action alleges that cultural enforcement is discriminatory.
- Employee surveys show that values enforcement is perceived as unfair or selective.
