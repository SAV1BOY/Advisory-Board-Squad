# Board Effectiveness Score

## Definition

A weighted composite metric measuring the overall effectiveness of the advisory board across five dimensions: strategic value, decision quality, accountability and oversight, risk readiness, and composition dynamics. Scored on a 1.0-5.0 scale using the board-effectiveness-rubric.md.

## How to Measure

1. Each board member independently completes the board-effectiveness-rubric quarterly.
2. Scores are averaged across all respondents for each dimension.
3. The weighted composite is calculated per the rubric formula:
   - Strategic Value (25%) + Decision Quality (25%) + Accountability (20%) + Risk Readiness (15%) + Composition (15%)
4. Perception gaps (where individual scores differ by 2+ points) are flagged for discussion.
5. An external facilitator aggregates and presents results annually.

## Target Range

| Rating | Score | Status |
|--------|-------|--------|
| World-class | 4.5 - 5.0 | Aspirational target |
| Effective | 3.5 - 4.4 | Operating target |
| Adequate | 2.5 - 3.4 | Requires improvement plan |
| Underperforming | 1.5 - 2.4 | Urgent intervention needed |
| Dysfunctional | 1.0 - 1.4 | Governance overhaul required |

**Operating target: 3.8+ with quarter-over-quarter improvement on the weakest dimension.**

## Data Sources

- Board member self-assessments (quarterly rubric completion)
- Meeting effectiveness scores (aggregated from meeting-effectiveness-rubric.md)
- Decision quality scores (aggregated from decision-quality-rubric.md)
- Action item completion rates (from action-item-tracker.md)
- Decision registry data (decision throughput, reversal rate, lead time)
- Risk registry data (risk identification timeliness, mitigation effectiveness)

## Update Cadence

- **Full assessment:** Annually (first board meeting of fiscal year)
- **Light-touch self-assessment:** Quarterly
- **Component metric feeds:** Monthly (meeting scores, decision scores roll up automatically)
- **Trend reporting:** Quarterly — presented at the board meeting with quarter-over-quarter comparison
