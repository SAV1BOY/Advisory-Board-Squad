# Partnership Incentive Alignment Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures that the incentive structures in partnership agreements create genuine mutual value rather than conflict. Misaligned incentives in partnerships lead to one party extracting value at the other's expense, eventually destroying the relationship.

## When to Use
- Before signing partnership agreements.
- During annual partnership reviews.
- When partnership performance is below expectations.
- When one party expresses dissatisfaction with the partnership economics.

## Gate Criteria
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Both parties' economic incentives are explicitly mapped and tested for conflicts.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Revenue sharing or compensation structures reward mutual success.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Neither party can profit from the other's failure.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Performance metrics are aligned: both parties are measured on the same outcomes.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The agreement addresses what happens when priorities diverge.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Investment obligations are proportional to benefit received.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The incentive structure has been red-teamed for gaming opportunities.

## Red Flags
- One party benefits regardless of overall partnership performance.
- The partnership creates an incentive for one party to minimize effort or investment.
- Economic terms were negotiated without understanding both parties' full cost structure.
- No review mechanism exists to adjust incentives as conditions change.

## Escalation Triggers
- One partner consistently underperforms their obligations while receiving full economic benefit.
- The incentive structure is discovered to be creating unintended behavior.
- A partner unilaterally changes their strategy in a way that misaligns incentives.
- Disputes about economics or performance exceed the governance process's resolution capacity.
