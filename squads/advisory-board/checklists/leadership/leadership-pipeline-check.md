# Leadership Pipeline Check Checklist

## Purpose
Ensures the organization is developing a pipeline of future leaders, not just managing current ones. A healthy pipeline means the organization can fill critical roles from within, reducing disruption and preserving culture. This checklist detects whether leadership development is systematic or left to chance.

## When to Use
- During annual talent reviews and succession planning.
- When key roles are difficult to fill internally.
- When assessing organizational readiness for growth.
- When attrition of high-potential employees increases.

## Gate Criteria

### Pipeline Identification
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: High-potential individuals are identified through a systematic process, not just manager nomination.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The pipeline includes candidates for roles one and two levels above their current position.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Diversity in the pipeline reflects or exceeds the diversity of the current leadership team.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Pipeline candidates are known to senior leadership, not just HR.

### Development Investment
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Each pipeline candidate has a documented development plan with specific experiences and milestones.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Development includes stretch assignments, cross-functional exposure, and mentoring.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Development budgets are allocated and spent, not just budgeted.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Progress on development plans is reviewed at least semi-annually.

### Pipeline Health Metrics
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The percentage of leadership roles filled internally is tracked and meets target (typically 60-70%).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Time to readiness for pipeline candidates is tracked.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Pipeline retention rate is monitored (are high-potentials staying).
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The pipeline depth for critical roles meets minimum standards (at least 2 candidates per role).

### Organizational Support
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Senior leaders actively sponsor and mentor pipeline candidates.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Leadership development is a standing agenda item in leadership team meetings.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: The organization's culture supports learning from failure, which is essential for development.
- [ ] **Pass/Fail**: Pipeline candidates' career aspirations are regularly assessed and aligned.

## Red Flags
- All leadership roles are filled by external hires because no internal candidates are ready.
- High-potential identification is biased toward demographics similar to current leadership.
- Development plans exist on paper but development experiences are not actually provided.
- Pipeline candidates are leaving for development opportunities at other organizations.
- Leadership development has been cut from the budget in the last cost reduction.

## Escalation Triggers
- A critical leadership role is vacant for more than 90 days due to pipeline gaps.
- Regrettable attrition of pipeline candidates exceeds 15% annually.
- Internal fill rate for leadership roles drops below 50%.
- The pipeline lacks any candidates for the CEO or C-suite succession.
- Development plans are abandoned during busy periods and not resumed.
