# Info Product Industry Reference

## Business Model Characteristics
- **Revenue models:** Course sales, membership/subscription, coaching, community, certification, live events
- **Margins:** 80-95% gross margin on digital products. Among the highest of any business model.
- **Key dynamic:** Expertise and personal brand drive demand. The product is the person's knowledge and reputation.
- **Scale pattern:** One-to-many (courses, books) provides leverage. One-to-one (coaching) provides premium pricing but limits scale.

## Key Metrics
- **Customer acquisition cost:** Often high in competitive niches. Paid social, content marketing, partnerships, and affiliates.
- **Average order value / cart value:** Info product businesses often use product ladders: free → low-ticket → high-ticket
- **Completion rate:** What % of buyers actually complete the course? Low completion correlates with low satisfaction and low repeat purchase.
- **Refund rate:** > 10% signals product-market mismatch or over-promising
- **Repeat purchase / ascension rate:** % of buyers who purchase a higher-ticket product. Healthy businesses convert 10-20% up the ladder.
- **Community engagement:** For membership models, active participation rate predicts retention

## Growth Strategy Patterns
- **Content-to-product funnel:** Free content (blog, podcast, YouTube) → email list → low-ticket product → high-ticket product
- **Launch model:** Time-limited enrollment windows create scarcity and event-driven revenue spikes
- **Evergreen model:** Always-available purchase with automated marketing sequences
- **Community-first:** Build the community, then monetize with products and services the community requests

## Board Considerations
- Evaluate founder dependency: if the business is entirely built on one person's brand, that's both the asset and the risk
- Monitor refund rates and completion rates as product quality indicators
- For high-ticket programs, ensure delivery matches the promise. Regulatory scrutiny on income claims is increasing.
- Diversification away from single-platform dependency (e.g., Instagram, YouTube) is a strategic priority
